Ce qui manque encore

Cette classe n'est pas encore complète : voici précisément ce qui n'a pas encore été sourcé, plutôt que de le passer sous silence.

  • Real, explicit source caveat: Icy Veins' own guide says the level-20 Enhancement toolkit is too limited to show the spec's real playstyle yet ("the full playstyle isn't really on display yet until we see more of the Beta open up") -- this class's real level-20 kit is genuinely thinner than every other class catalogued so far, not an artifact of under-research this pass.
  • None of Stormstrike, Flurry, Maelstrom Weapon or Windfury Weapon (Enhancement's real signature abilities in other WoW versions) are reachable at 11 points and none are named in the real level-20 rotation text -- not looked up.
  • Lightning Shield, Flametongue Weapon (group alternative to Rockbiter), Frost Shock, Fire Nova, totems (Searing/Strength of Earth/Stoneskin/Healing Stream) named in the real rotation but not looked up.
  • "talents.restoration" is an empty placeholder. Note: the guide also flags a real alternative build spending points in Restoration (Mindfulness + Water Shield) for better early Mana sustain, at the cost of blocking a deeper Enhancement path later -- not sourced here.
  • Elemental (2026-09-25): real, explicit source caveat -- Icy Veins' own guide says Elemental's best talents (Lava Burst, Elemental Alacrity) "are none of these abilities... available at Level 20", describing them as "a preview of sorts for what is to come." The real usable level-20 rotation is just Lightning Bolt (filler) + Flame Shock (maintained DoT). Both abilities below use their real Rank 1 tooltips (level 1 and level 10 respectively) rather than whatever higher rank is actually trainable by 20 -- a disclosed conservative approximation, not the exact level-20 numbers, after this pass's searches for the exact intermediate rank IDs didn't turn up a clean result in reasonable time. Fire Nova (3+ target tool) and Earth Shock (already sourced above, used more as an interrupt/utility per Elemental's own guide than a core rotation piece) are real but not part of Elemental's own single-target priority text.
