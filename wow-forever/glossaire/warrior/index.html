Les noms de talents ci-dessus ne sont pas encore cliquables : leurs identifiants internes (extraits des données client de la bêta) ne correspondent pas à un vrai identifiant de sort Wowhead vérifié, donc aucune infobulle n'est affichée plutôt que de pointer vers une mauvaise page.

Ce qui manque encore

Cette classe n'est pas encore complète : voici précisément ce qui n'a pas encore été sourcé, plutôt que de le passer sous silence.

  • Heroic Strike's low-rank values (the rank actually trainable by level 20, not the level-60 max rank already recorded below): not yet looked up. Icy Veins' own level-20 guide says to generally avoid it while leveling (Rage-inefficient at that stage), so this is low priority.
  • Charge Rank 2/3's own level requirements weren't checked (Rank 1, level 4, is recorded below) -- low priority, since Charge can't be used in combat at all, so it only ever contributes one small Rage tick at the very start of a sustained single-target fight.
  • Cleave (AoE rage-dump, off-topic for single-target modeling but needed if multi-target is ever modeled): not looked up.
  • Battle Shout's exact level-20-trainable rank: rank 1 (wowhead_spell_id 6673, +12 AP) is recorded below and confirmed "Requires level 1", so it likely stays rank 1 through level 20 -- but this wasn't cross-checked against the other 6 ranks' own level requirements to confirm no earlier rank than max is trainable before 20.
  • Sunder Armor's real DPS value can't be computed yet: needs a sourced armor-mitigation formula and a real target armor value, neither looked up.
  • "talents.arms" and "talents.protection" are empty placeholders, not yet populated -- relevant since a Fury build can still spend points in Arms (e.g. Improved Heroic Strike), and this file should end up covering the whole class, not just the Fury tree.
  • Real, sourced finding (2026-09-25): Icy Veins' own level-20 Arms Warrior guide spends its 11 points identically to Fury (5/5 Cruelty, 5/5 Unbridled Wrath, 1/1 Piercing Howl, all in the Fury tree) -- Arms' own deeper talents simply aren't reachable at this level cap. The only real level-20 differences between the two guides are: Arms uses a two-handed weapon (Fury dual-wields) and Arms' rotation includes Slam as a filler (Fury's doesn't, see warrior_slam's own notes).
  • Protection Warrior (tank, 2026-09-25): real level-20 build sourced (talents.protection below), but its own real single-target priority (Icy Veins) doesn't include Rend or Overpower at all -- Overpower needs Battle Stance, which the guide explicitly says Protection only visits briefly to Charge in before swapping back to Defensive Stance. The two abilities the guide DOES name, Revenge (procs off successfully Block/Dodge/Parry-ing an incoming attack) and Sunder Armor (already sourced above, no direct-damage effect of its own, just a threat/armor-debuff tool), aren't usefully simulable here: this project's simulator has no incoming-damage/attack-table-against-the-player model at all, so a proc that triggers off the player being hit can't be resolved. Revenge itself (real spell, not yet looked up) and the real Block chance formula are both open gaps if that ever changes.
