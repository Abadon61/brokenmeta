Quick answers: leveling 1 to 60

What is the level cap in WoW Forever?

The level cap is 60. The first talent point comes at level 10, then one per level up to 51 points at level 60.

Which dungeons should I run to level from 1 to 60 in WoW: Forever?

In the client data, dungeons range from level 13 (Hall of Thanes) to level 60 (Scholomance, Sunken Temple, Blackrock Depths). Each dungeon's level range is in this guide, with a link to its loot page. All dungeons →

Which talent build should I take to level my class?

Every specialization guide has a "Leveling from 1 to 60" section: the talent order step by step (derived from the level-60 build, not tested while leveling), the dungeons of each level band and the spec's best item in each. Leveling by class →

Which class levels fastest?

We have no measure of leveling speed: our simulator measures level 60. A dedicated page says what we know about the classes, what is an opinion, and points to the community's views. Which class to level? →

How long does it take to reach level 60?

We give no duration: no reliable source measures it for Forever, and the time depends on the class, the route and the dungeons.

At what level do profession ranks unlock?

According to the game client, the Journeyman rank is learned at level 10, Expert at level 20 and Artisan at level 35. Profession guides →

Are WoW Classic's leveling zones the same in Forever?

That is not confirmed. Forever extends the original Azeroth with new content (Zephras Isle, Mount Hyjal, Riverglades): the zone level ranges in this guide come from WoW Classic and will be checked at launch.

Can you reach level 60 in WoW Forever?

Yes: the level cap is 60. The zone and dungeon levels in this guide come from the client tables and from Classic, not from games we played.

What is confirmed and what is not

  • Dungeons: levels read from the client data (build 1.60.1.69913); the Hall of Thanes (13-18) and the Ruins of Lordaeron (15-20) come from Blizzard's announcement.
  • Professions: character levels required to learn each rank, read from the game client.
  • Zones: WoW Classic levels (Warcraft Wiki), and each zone's side (Alliance, Horde or shared) as it is in Classic. Nothing confirms yet that they are the same in Forever, where the world is extended with new content: they are indicative.
  • Talents: the first point comes at level 10, then one per level (client rules). The order of the points of each specialization is derived from its level-60 build and was not tested while leveling a character.
