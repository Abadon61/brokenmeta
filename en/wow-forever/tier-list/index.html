WoW: Forever level 60 tier list: which class is the best?

Simulator dataGame build 1.60.1.70450Updated October 28, 2026

Each spec is simulated at level 60 with the best gear known from the beta, optimized talents and raid buffs. Tiers S to D come from the gap to the best spec of the same role: nothing is placed by hand. This ranking only counts the items that have already been datamined: many items are still undiscovered, so it can change.

The highest simulated DPS today is Warlock Affliction with 754 DPS; the tiers are computed from that value.

In short: which class is the best?

Warlock Affliction is the strongest DPS spec in our simulation (754 DPS). S tier: Warlock Affliction and Hunter Beast Mastery. A tier: Priest Shadow and Warlock Destruction. For tanking, Warrior Protection comes first (1,213 TPS).

Which class should you pick for your playstyle?

Our simulation measures single-target power, not how fun a class is. Here is the best spec of each style according to our measures, and what we cannot say yet.

  • Melee : Druid Feral Combat (592 DPS)
  • Ranged : Hunter Beast Mastery (732 DPS)
  • Spellcaster : Warlock Affliction (754 DPS)
  • Tank : Warrior Protection (1,213 TPS), ranked by threat per second: survival is not simulated yet.
  • Versatility : Druid, Warrior and Paladin offer both a DPS spec and a tank spec in our measures (healing is not simulated).
  • Solo and leveling : we do not simulate solo play; see the 1-60 leveling guide.
  • Dungeons : our simulation is single target, with no area damage; loot and bosses are in the dungeon pages.
  • Beginner : we do not rank how hard a class is to play, for lack of reliable data. Start from an S or A tier class you like, then read its spec guide.

What about healers?

Healers are not ranked: the simulator does not model healing over a full fight yet, and we will not show an invented number. Their guides remain available in the classes section. See the spec guides.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best class in WoW: Forever at level 60?

In our simulation the highest DPS spec is Warlock Affliction (754 DPS, build 1.60.1.70450). The full ranking of the 20 DPS specs is on this page, with the method.

How are the tiers of this tier list built?

Each spec is compared with the best one of its role: S from 95% of its DPS, A from 88%, B from 80%, C from 72%, D below. Nothing is placed by hand.

Will the tier list change?

Yes: the ranking only counts items that have already been datamined, many items are still undiscovered, and values move with patches. The update date and the game build are shown at the top of the page, and the simulator is rerun after every major change.

Why are healers not ranked?

The simulator does not model healing over a full fight yet. Rather than show an invented number, we leave them out for now.

How this tier list is computed
  • Simulator: BrokenMeta.gg's level 60 engine (BrokenMeta.gg), the same as the "DPS simulator" page. Spell and stat tables come from the beta client (build 1.60.1.70450). Items (dungeon, raid and crafted gear) come from the Wowhead Forever pages read on October 17, 2026.
  • Fight: 180 seconds against a stationary level 63 boss, 6000 runs per spec; the DPS shown is the mean, with its standard error on the detail page.
  • Gear: the best set the optimizer finds among the items known from the beta (dungeons, raid, crafted gear). No item is added by hand. Only items that have already been datamined are counted: many are still to be discovered, and new items will move the ranking.
  • Tiers: S = at least 95% of the best spec's DPS, A = 88%, B = 80%, C = 72%, D = below. Tanks are compared with each other, on threat per second.
  • Race: Human (Orc for Shaman), with the racials read from the beta client (October 15 rework, build 1.60.1.70425); WoW Classic's weapon skill no longer exists in Forever, where weapon specializations were replaced by critical strike chance. For melee, Classic's attack table (skill 300 against a defense of 315 for a level 63 boss) is assumed to apply: Forever does not confirm it, and if it differs, the melee specs will move.
  • Limits: a single-target fight with no movement or boss mechanics; some WoW Classic values stand in when Forever does not give them yet (flagged in the simulator). The ranking will move with patches.
