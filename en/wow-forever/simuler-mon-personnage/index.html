Simulate my character

Paste the BrokenMeta addon export: this page simulates your character's DPS with its real stats and weapons, then compares it with the best-in-slot (BiS) level-20 gear for its spec. Same engine as our DPS ranking.

How it works

  1. Download the BrokenMeta addon and unzip it into World of Warcraft's _classic_beta_\Interface\AddOns folder, then restart the game. Download the BrokenMeta addon 0.6.0 (.zip)
  2. In game, remove temporary buffs (they inflate the character sheet stats), then type /bmw export.
  3. Copy the text with Ctrl+C, paste it below and run the simulation.

The simulation runs entirely in your browser: the export is never sent to any server. It contains no character name and no realm. The first run downloads the Python engine (about 11 MB); later runs are instant. To help improve the simulator, you can anonymously share the addon's measurements: Share my data →

What the simulation does not model yet

  • Only level 20 (the beta's current cap) is calibrated: above it, rotations and spell ranks no longer match.
  • The export carries all your talents, but the simulator only applies the few talents whose numeric effect is sourced.
  • No mana regeneration or shortage, no wand, no Warlock pet: the same limits as our DPS ranking.
  • Stats come from the character sheet at export time: an active buff skews them.
