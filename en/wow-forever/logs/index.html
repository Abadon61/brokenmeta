BrokenLogs: combat log analysis

Computed in your browserNothing uploadedLarge files accepted

Drop your combat log file: the site splits the encounters, computes DPS, healing and deaths, then tells you in a few sentences what cost you time. Everything is computed in your browser: the file never leaves your computer.

How to do it

  1. In game, turn on Options → Network → Advanced Combat Logging, then type /combatlog before the fight.
  2. Play your dungeon or raid, then type /combatlog again to stop. The WoWCombatLog-….txt file is in the game's Logs folder.
  3. Drop it below. Even a file of several hundred MB is read in a few seconds.

How the numbers are computed
  • Damage = amount - overkill + damage absorbed by a shield (it counts as dealt). Pets and guardians are added to their owner.
  • Healing = amount - overhealing. Overhealing is shown separately.
  • DPS = damage / encounter duration. Active DPS divides by the time the player is really acting (a gap longer than 3 s between two actions is not counted).
  • An encounter runs from ENCOUNTER_START to ENCOUNTER_END. Outside of those, a combat starts at the first hit between a player and a creature and ends after 12 s without damage.
  • The game logs every hit twice (attacker side and target side): only one is kept. Feigned deaths are not counted.
  • Class and specialization come from the log's COMBATANT_INFO line. If the game does not provide them, the player is shown without a class.
