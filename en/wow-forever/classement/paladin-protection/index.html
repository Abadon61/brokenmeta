Why these talents

A build has 51 points. A tier-n talent only unlocks after 5 × (n − 1) points spent higher in the same tree (the numbers on the left of each tree give the tier and the points required): some points are therefore gate points, taken to reach a talent further down.

  • Holy: 8 points. The deepest talent, Divine Strength, is in tier 1 and needs 0 points in the tree.
  • Protection: 43 points. The deepest talent, Holy Shield, is in tier 7 and needs 30 points in the tree.

43 points in Protection and 8 in Holy. Holy Shield (−147 points, 12% of the score) is the central talent; Reckoning (−50), One-Handed Weapon Specialization (−55), Anticipation (−18), Improved Righteous Fury (−20) and Redoubt (−20) strengthen threat and blocking.

Shield Specialization, Improved Seal of Fury and Templar's Bulwark are not simulated; Sacred Duty and Swift Judgement are at zero. The 8 Holy points (Divine Strength, Divine Intellect) have no effect for a tank: filler.

The talents that pay the most: Holy Shield (−147.1 score), One-Handed Weapon Specialization (−55.4 score), Reckoning (−50.4 score).

Points with no measured effect in the simulator (gate, utility or an effect that is not simulated): Shield Specialization, Improved Seal of Fury, Templar's Bulwark.

A talent's value is what the specialization loses when it is taken out of the build, on the same character and the same 180-second simulation.

Rotation

  1. Keep Seal of Righteousness up; Judgement on cooldown.
  2. Holy Shield on cooldown: more blocks and holy damage on each block.
  3. Holy Strike on cooldown.
  4. Reckoning: an extra attack after each block.
