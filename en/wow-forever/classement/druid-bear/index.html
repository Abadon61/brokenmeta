  • Feral Swiftness 2/2Increases your movement speed while in Cat Form by 30%, and increases your chance to Dodge by 4%.
  • Thick Hide 3/3While in Bear Form, Cat Form, Dire Bear Form, or Moonkin Form, you gain 3 additional base Armor per level and another 2.00 base Armor for each point of defense skill beyond five times your level. This amount can be further increased by multipliers from those forms.
  • Savage Fury 2/2Increases the damage caused by your Claw, Rake, Shred, Maul, and Swipe abilities by 10%.
  • Sharpened Claws 2/2Increases your critical strike chance while in Bear Form, Dire Bear Form, or Cat Form by 6%.
  • Shredding Attacks 3/3Reduces the Energy cost of your Shred ability by 18 and reduces the Rage cost of your Lacerate ability by -3.
  • Blood Frenzy 2/2Gives you a 100% chance to gain an additional 5 Rage any time you get a critical strike while in Bear Form or Dire Bear Form. In addition, your non-periodic critical strikes from Cat Form abilities that generate Combo Points have a 100% chance to add an additional Combo Point.
  • Predatory Strikes 3/3Increases your melee Attack Power in Cat Form, Bear Form, and Dire Bear Form by 150% of your level.
  • Leader of the Pack 1/1While in Cat Form, Bear Form, or Dire Bear Form, the Leader of the Pack increases the critical strike chance of all party members within 45 yards by 3%, exclusive with Moonkin Aura.

  • Natural Reaction 5/5Increases your dodge chance by 5%, and gives you a 100% chance to gain 5 Rage each time you dodge.
