Why these talents

A build has 51 points. A tier-n talent only unlocks after 5 × (n − 1) points spent higher in the same tree (the numbers on the left of each tree give the tier and the points required): some points are therefore gate points, taken to reach a talent further down.

  • Fire: 32 points. The deepest talent, Combustion, is in tier 7 and needs 30 points in the tree.
  • Frost: 19 points. The deepest talent, Frost Channeling, is in tier 3 and needs 10 points in the tree.

32 points in Fire and 19 in Frost. Improved Scorch (−61 DPS), Fire Power (−52), Ignite (−56), Critical Mass (−54), Improved Fireball (−45) and Master of Elements (−22) form the core; Pyroblast, Heating Up and Combustion add 9 to 15 DPS each.

The 19 Frost points are taken for Elemental Precision (+5% hit, −27 DPS); Ice Shards, Frost Channeling, Piercing Ice and Improved Frostbolt are worth only about 2 DPS each and fill the 51-point total for lack of a useful Fire talent.

The talents that pay the most: Improved Scorch (−60.6 DPS), Ignite (−56.0 DPS), Critical Mass (−53.5 DPS).

A talent's value is what the specialization loses when it is taken out of the build, on the same character and the same 180-second simulation.

Stat caps

Each curve shows the simulated character's DPS as a stat is added on top of its gear: the left point is the current gear. A dashed line marks the estimated cap, the point where every extra point is worth less than 20% of what it gave at the start.

Stat priority

From the most to the least profitable for one unit of stat added to the simulated gear. Only the stats that bring DPS are listed.

  1. Hit: +0.66 DPS per rating point (up to about 10.8%)
  2. Intellect: +0.54 DPS per point
  3. Spell power: +0.50 DPS per point
  4. Crit: +0.47 DPS per rating point
  5. Haste: +0.06 DPS per rating point

Rotation

  1. Frostfire Bolt as the filler.
  2. Pyroblast when Heating Up has shortened its cast.
  3. Fire Blast on cooldown.
  4. Scorch to keep the Improved Scorch effect up.
  5. Combustion on cooldown; Evocation and the mana gem for mana.
