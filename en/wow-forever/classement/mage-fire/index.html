Mage Fire: level 60 build, talents, rotation and DPS

This is what our simulator gets for the Mage Fire at level 60: the gear the optimizer finds among the items known from the beta, the preset talents, the rotation followed and the damage of every ability over a 180-second fight against a stationary boss.

Rotation

  1. Frostfire Bolt as the filler.
  2. Pyroblast when Heating Up has shortened its cast.
  3. Fire Blast on cooldown.
  4. Scorch to keep the Improved Scorch effect up.
  5. Combustion on cooldown; Evocation and the mana gem for mana.

Talents chosen

Fire 32 points

  • Improved Fireball 5/5Reduces the casting time of your Fireball and Frostfire Bolt spells by 0.5 sec.
  • Incineration 3/3Increases the critical strike chance of your Fire Blast, Ice Lance, Arcane Blast, and Scorch spells by 6%.
  • Wake of Fire 2/2Reduces the cooldown of your Fire Blast spell by -2 sec. Killing a non-trivial target increases the critical strike chance of your next Fire Blast cast within 30 sec by 50%.
  • Ignite 5/5Your critical strikes from Fire damage spells cause the target to burn for an additional 40% of your spell's damage over 4 sec.
  • Pyroblast 1/1Hurls an immense fiery boulder that causes 110 Fire damage and an additional 44 Fire damage over 12 sec.
  • Heating Up 1/1Non-periodic critical strikes with Fireball, Frostfire Bolt, Fire Blast, and Scorch reduce the cast time of your next Pyroblast cast within 20 sec by 25%, stacking up to 3 times.
  • Improved Scorch 3/3Your Scorch spell has a 100% chance to cause your target to be vulnerable to Fire damage. This vulnerability increases all Fire damage you deal to your target by 3% and lasts 30 sec, stacking up to 5 times.
  • Master of Elements 3/3Your Fire and Frost critical strikes will refund 30% of their base mana cost.
  • Critical Mass 3/3Increases the critical strike chance of your Fire spells by 6%.
  • Fire Power 5/5Increases the damage done by your Fire spells by 10%.
  • Combustion 1/1When activated, this spell causes each of your Fire damage spell hits to increase your critical strike chance with Fire damage spells by 10%. This effect lasts until you have caused 3 non-periodic critical strikes with Fire spells.

Frost 19 points

  • Elemental Precision 5/5Improves your chance to hit with Frost and Fire spells by 5%.
  • Improved Frostbolt 3/5Reduces the casting time of your Frostbolt spell by 0.3 sec.
  • Ice Shards 5/5Increases the critical strike damage bonus of your Frost spells by 100%.
  • Frost Channeling 3/3Reduces the mana cost of your Frost spells by 15% and reduces the threat caused by your Frost spells by 30%.
  • Piercing Ice 3/3Increases the damage done by your Frost spells by 6%.

Buffs and consumables in the simulation

Every simulation gets all the buffs a 20-player raid can bring (one class or specialization of each kind) and the consumables of its role: scrolls, flask or elixirs, juju, food. There are no world buffs: they do not exist in WoW: Forever raids.

Ally buffs

  • Arcane Brilliance (Mage) — +31 Intellect
  • Greater Blessing of Kings (Paladin) — +10% to all stats
  • Greater Blessing of Wisdom (Paladin) — +40 mana / 5 s
  • Greater Blessing of Might (Paladin) — +133 attack power
  • Gift of the Wild (Druid) — +16 Strength, +16 Agility, +16 Stamina, +16 Intellect, +16 Spirit
  • Prayer of Spirit (Priest) — +40 Spirit
  • Prayer of Fortitude (Priest) — +70 Stamina
  • Mana Spring Totem (Shaman) — +25 mana / 5 s
  • Moonkin Aura (Balance Druid) — +3% spell crit

Consumables

  • Scroll of Spirit — +15 Spirit
  • Scroll of Stamina — +16 Stamina
  • Flask of Ancient Knowledge — +180 spell power
  • Juju Guile — +30 Intellect
  • Spirit of Zanza — +50 Stamina, +50 Spirit
  • Mageblood Potion — +12 mana / 5 s
  • Brilliant Wizard Oil — +36 spell power, +1% spell crit

Target debuffs

Not simulated: Windfury Totem, Power Infusion, Innervate and Judgements (proc or cooldown effects). Values come from the beta client tables; weapon oils are Classic estimates. The consumables worth the most for this specialization are ranked in its guide.

Assumptions

A 180-second fight against a stationary level-63 boss, the raid buffs and consumables of the class's family, a standard race, item effects on. Level-60 base stats and several Classic values are estimates, flagged in the simulator. Results will move with the game.
