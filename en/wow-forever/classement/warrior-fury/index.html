Why these talents

A build has 51 points. A tier-n talent only unlocks after 5 × (n − 1) points spent higher in the same tree (the numbers on the left of each tree give the tier and the points required): some points are therefore gate points, taken to reach a talent further down.

  • Arms: 14 points. The deepest talent, Anger Management, is in tier 3 and needs 10 points in the tree.
  • Fury: 37 points. The deepest talent, Bloodthirst, is in tier 7 and needs 30 points in the tree.

37 in Fury and 14 in Arms. The talents that weigh the most (DPS lost without them, measured in the simulator): Flurry (−78, 14%), Bloodthirst (−56, 10%), Dual Wield Specialization (−55), Cruelty (−29), Furious Precision (−23), Death Wish (−17), Unbridled Wrath (−14).

Measured at about zero or not simulated (gate points, filler or non-damage effects): Lingering Rage, Enrage, Improved Rend, Improved Tactical Mastery, Improved Overpower, Anger Management.

The October 15 patch changes the line: Bloodthirst rises to 45% of attack power, Precision and Boundless Rage are gone, Dual Wield Specialization gives off-hand rage instead of hit (off-hand hit now comes from Furious Precision) and Raging Blows no longer makes Whirlwind strike with the off hand (it lowers Whirlwind's cost by 3 rage). Points with no measured effect are gate points.

The talents that pay the most: Flurry (−74.5 DPS), Dual Wield Specialization (−55.2 DPS), Bloodthirst (−53.5 DPS).

Points with no measured effect in the simulator (gate, utility or an effect that is not simulated): Lingering Rage, Improved Tactical Mastery.

A talent's value is what the specialization loses when it is taken out of the build, on the same character and the same 180-second simulation.

Rotation

  1. Bloodthirst whenever it is ready.
  2. Whirlwind on cooldown (3 rage cheaper with Raging Blows).
  3. Execute under 20% target health.
  4. Heroic Strike on the next swing with spare rage.
  5. Berserker Rage, Bloodrage, Death Wish and Recklessness on cooldown.

Assumptions

A 180-second fight against a stationary level-63 boss, the raid buffs and consumables of the class's family, a standard race (Human, Orc for Shaman) with the racials read from the beta client (WoW Classic's weapon skill no longer exists in Forever: weapon specializations give critical strike chance), item effects on. Level-60 base stats and several Classic values are estimates, flagged in the simulator. Results will move with the game.

The core mechanics (attack table, rage, mana regeneration, resistances, Classic base stats and race bonuses) come from the open-source simulator WoWSims (MIT licence): github.com/wowsims/classic
