Why these talents

A build has 51 points. A tier-n talent only unlocks after 5 × (n − 1) points spent higher in the same tree (the numbers on the left of each tree give the tier and the points required): some points are therefore gate points, taken to reach a talent further down.

  • Arms: 10 points. The deepest talent, Improved Tactical Mastery, is in tier 2 and needs 5 points in the tree.
  • Fury: 41 points. The deepest talent, Bloodthirst, is in tier 7 and needs 30 points in the tree.

41 points in Fury and 10 in Arms. The build follows the Fury line up to Bloodthirst (tier 7, 30 points required) and takes everything that raises attacks: Dual Wield Specialization (−107 DPS without it) and Flurry (−76) are worth nearly 30% of the DPS together, and Cruelty, Bloodthirst, Death Wish and Raging Blows add 21 to 31 DPS each.

Enrage and Boundless Rage have no measured effect: so they act as gate points toward Flurry and Bloodthirst. The remaining 10 points go into Arms (Improved Heroic Strike, 5 DPS; the rest is filler) for lack of a more useful Fury talent.

The talents that pay the most: Dual Wield Specialization (−106.8 DPS), Flurry (−88.7 DPS), Bloodthirst (−38.4 DPS).

Points with no measured effect in the simulator (gate, utility or an effect that is not simulated): Improved Tactical Mastery, Improved Cleave.

A talent's value is what the specialization loses when it is taken out of the build, on the same character and the same 180-second simulation.

Stat caps

Each curve shows the simulated character's DPS as a stat is added on top of its gear: the left point is the current gear. A dashed line marks the estimated cap, the point where every extra point is worth less than 20% of what it gave at the start.

Stat priority

From the most to the least profitable for one unit of stat added to the simulated gear. Only the stats that bring DPS are listed.

  1. Haste: +0.60 DPS per rating point
  2. Crit: +0.54 DPS per rating point
  3. Strength: +0.53 DPS per point
  4. Agility: +0.42 DPS per point
  5. Hit: +0.39 DPS per rating point (up to about 21.8%)
  6. Attack power: +0.28 DPS per point

Rotation

  1. Bloodthirst whenever it is ready.
  2. Whirlwind (and Raging Blows' Whirlwind) on cooldown.
  3. Execute under 20% target health.
  4. Heroic Strike on the next swing with spare rage.
  5. Berserker Rage, Bloodrage, Death Wish and Recklessness on cooldown.
