Rotation

  1. Bloodthirst whenever it is ready.
  2. Whirlwind (and Raging Blows' Whirlwind) on cooldown.
  3. Execute under 20% target health.
  4. Heroic Strike on the next swing with spare rage.
  5. Berserker Rage, Bloodrage, Death Wish and Recklessness on cooldown.

Talents chosen

Arms 10 points

  • Improved Heroic Strike 3/3Reduces the cost of your Heroic Strike ability by 3 Rage.
  • Improved Rend 3/3Increases the Bleed damage done by your Rend ability by 35%.
  • Improved Tactical Mastery 4/5Tactical Mastery lets you retain up to an additional 12 Rage when you change stances.

Fury 41 points

  • Cruelty 5/5Improves your chance to get a critical strike with melee attacks by 5%.
  • Unbridled Wrath 5/5Gives you a 60% chance to generate 1 additional Rage when you deal melee damage with a weapon. This effect is increased to 2 Rage for two-handed weapons.
  • Boundless Rage 3/3Increases your maximum Rage by 30.
  • Improved Cleave 3/3Reduces the Rage cost of your Cleave ability by -3.
  • Dual Wield Specialization 5/5Increases your off-hand weapon damage by 25% and chance to hit with off-hand attacks by 10%.
  • Enrage 5/5Gives you a 30% chance to deal 10% increased Physical damage for 12 sec after being the victim of any damaging attack.
  • Improved Execute 2/2Reduces the Rage cost of your Execute ability by 5.
  • Raging Blows 1/1Causes your Whirlwind to also strike with your Off Hand weapon, and reduces the Rage cost of your Cleave ability by -2.
  • Death Wish 1/1When activated, increases your Physical damage done by 20% and makes you immune to Fear effects, but increases all damage you take by 5%. Lasts 30 sec.
  • Precision 3/3Improves your chance to hit by 3%.
  • Flurry 5/5Increases your melee attack speed by 25% for your next 3 swings after dealing a melee critical strike.
  • Improved Berserker Rage 2/2Your Berserker Rage ability will instantly generate 10 Rage and has a 100% chance to remove all movement impairing effects when activated.
  • Bloodthirst 1/1Instantly attack the target causing damage equal to 35% of your Attack Power plus 30 and increasing your movement speed by 10% for 10 sec.
