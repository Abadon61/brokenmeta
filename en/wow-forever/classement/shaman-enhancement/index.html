Why these talents

A build has 51 points. A tier-n talent only unlocks after 5 × (n − 1) points spent higher in the same tree (the numbers on the left of each tree give the tier and the points required): some points are therefore gate points, taken to reach a talent further down.

  • Elemental: 18 points. The deepest talent, Elemental Focus, is in tier 3 and needs 10 points in the tree.
  • Enhancement: 33 points. The deepest talent, Rage of the Farseer, is in tier 7 and needs 30 points in the tree.

The build relies on Stormstrike (−151 DPS without it, 28% of the DPS), Flurry (−52) and Mental Dexterity (−40: +100% of intellect as attack power). Thundering Strikes (+5% crit) and Elemental Devastation (+9% melee crit for 10 s after a spell crit, −24 DPS) complete the crit line; Elemental Devastation is worth 11 more DPS than Call of Flame for the same points.

Maelstrom Weapon, Improved Stormstrike, Convection and Elemental Focus measure close to zero: with 4 Maelstrom stacks gained per minute the simulator rarely casts a Lightning Bolt (it needs 3). The points mostly stay to reach Rage of the Farseer (tier 7, 30 points required, −14 DPS); Maelstrom and Windfury's proc chances are Classic estimates.

The 18 Elemental points have no better use in the Enhancement tree: Elemental Devastation and Concussion are the only ones that pay.

The talents that pay the most: Stormstrike (−188.3 DPS), Flurry (−52.3 DPS), Thundering Strikes (−31.0 DPS).

A talent's value is what the specialization loses when it is taken out of the build, on the same character and the same 180-second simulation.

Rotation

  1. Opening: Searing Totem, then Flame Shock (Strength of Earth and Grace of Air already up); the Totem is put down again every 55 seconds.
  2. Stormstrike on cooldown: it gives +20% nature damage to the next Lightning Bolt, Chain Lightning or Earth Shock and, with Improved Stormstrike, keeps half the mana regeneration while casting for 15 seconds.
  3. Lightning Bolt right after Stormstrike to take the +20% (it is worth more there than on Earth Shock); then Lightning Bolt from 3 Maelstrom Weapon stacks: each stack takes 4% per rank off its cast time and mana cost, and melee swings go on while it is cast.
  4. Earth Shock in the shock slot (shared 6 s cooldown), Flame Shock as soon as its damage over time is about to end. Frost Shock gives the same result as Earth Shock, and Fire Nova adds nothing measurable: casting time is the limit, not the cooldowns.
  5. Windfury Weapon on the two-hand weapon (2 extra attacks); Rage of the Farseer on cooldown.
