Why these talents

A build has 51 points. A tier-n talent only unlocks after 5 × (n − 1) points spent higher in the same tree (the numbers on the left of each tree give the tier and the points required): some points are therefore gate points, taken to reach a talent further down.

  • Beast Mastery: 33 points. The deepest talent, Bestial Wrath, is in tier 7 and needs 30 points in the tree.
  • Marksmanship: 18 points. The deepest talent, Efficiency, is in tier 2 and needs 5 points in the tree.

33 points in Beast Mastery and 18 in Marksmanship. The pet is the engine: Frenzy (−32 DPS), Ferocity (−27), Unleashed Fury (−23), Deadly Aspects (−23), Bestial Wrath (−14) and Focused Fire (−12). Bestial Swiftness, Intimidation and Endurance Training are not simulated.

The 18 Marksmanship points give the hunter's own shot talents: Careful Aim (−48), Efficiency (−33), Lethal Attacks (−22) and Improved Stings (−8), which count as much as half of the Beast tree.

The talents that pay the most: Careful Aim (−48.2 DPS), Efficiency (−33.1 DPS), Frenzy (−32.2 DPS).

Points with no measured effect in the simulator (gate, utility or an effect that is not simulated): Endurance Training, Bestial Swiftness, Intimidation.

A talent's value is what the specialization loses when it is taken out of the build, on the same character and the same 180-second simulation.

Rotation

  1. Hunter's Mark on the target before the fight (+110 ranged attack power).
  2. Auto Shot continuously.
  3. Arcane Shot and Multi-Shot on cooldown.
  4. Keep Serpent Sting up.
  5. Bestial Wrath and Rapid Fire on cooldown.
  6. The pet attacks continuously (bite and claw).
