Why these talents

A build has 51 points. A tier-n talent only unlocks after 5 × (n − 1) points spent higher in the same tree (the numbers on the left of each tree give the tier and the points required): some points are therefore gate points, taken to reach a talent further down.

  • Arcane: 19 points. The deepest talent, Arcane Meditation, is in tier 4 and needs 15 points in the tree.
  • Frost: 32 points. The deepest talent, Winter's Chill, is in tier 6 and needs 25 points in the tree.

32 points in Frost and 19 in Arcane. Fingers of Frost (−122 DPS, 19% of the DPS), Improved Frostbolt (−93), Ice Shards (−86) and Ice Lance (−61) carry the specialization, followed by Shatter (−49), Winter's Chill (−47), Elemental Precision (−41) and Piercing Ice (−30).

The Arcane points are mostly for Arcane Meditation (mana regeneration while casting, −13 DPS); Arcane Focus, Arcane Impact and Arcane Concentration have no effect on frost spells, and Wand Specialization and Arcane Subtlety are not simulated. Frost Channeling is neutral.

The talents that pay the most: Fingers of Frost (−122.0 DPS), Improved Frostbolt (−92.6 DPS), Ice Shards (−85.9 DPS).

Points with no measured effect in the simulator (gate, utility or an effect that is not simulated): Wand Specialization, Arcane Subtlety.

A talent's value is what the specialization loses when it is taken out of the build, on the same character and the same 180-second simulation.

Rotation

  1. Frostbolt as the filler.
  2. Ice Lance on a Frozen target (Fingers of Frost, Winter's Chill).
  3. Evocation and the mana gem for mana.
