Why these talents

A build has 51 points. A tier-n talent only unlocks after 5 × (n − 1) points spent higher in the same tree (the numbers on the left of each tree give the tier and the points required): some points are therefore gate points, taken to reach a talent further down.

  • Holy: 19 points. The deepest talent, Reverence, is in tier 3 and needs 10 points in the tree.
  • Retribution: 32 points. The deepest talent, Twist of Light, is in tier 7 and needs 30 points in the tree.

32 points in Retribution and 19 in Holy. Seal of Command (−107 DPS, 24% of the DPS) is the central talent, with Vengeance (−28), Conviction (−17), Champion of the Light (−13), Two-Handed Weapon Specialization (−13) and Sacred Arbiter (−11). Benediction, Sanctified Judgement, Instrument of Law and Twist of Light measure at zero: they are gate points toward tier 7.

Holy brings Improved Seals (−23 DPS) and Divine Strength (−11); Divine Intellect has no effect for a melee paladin, and Healing Light and Reverence are healing, not simulated.

The talents that pay the most: Seal of Command (−106.5 DPS), Vengeance (−31.7 DPS), Improved Seals (−28.3 DPS).

Points with no measured effect in the simulator (gate, utility or an effect that is not simulated): Healing Light, Reverence.

A talent's value is what the specialization loses when it is taken out of the build, on the same character and the same 180-second simulation.

Rotation

  1. Keep Seal of Command up; Judgement of Command on cooldown.
  2. Holy Strike on cooldown.
  3. Hammer of Wrath in the execute phase.
  4. Two-handed melee swings deal the rest of the damage.
