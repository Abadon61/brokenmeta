Why these talents

A build has 51 points. A tier-n talent only unlocks after 5 × (n − 1) points spent higher in the same tree (the numbers on the left of each tree give the tier and the points required): some points are therefore gate points, taken to reach a talent further down.

  • Assassination: 18 points. The deepest talent, Lethality, is in tier 3 and needs 10 points in the tree.
  • Combat: 33 points. The deepest talent, Adrenaline Rush, is in tier 7 and needs 30 points in the tree.

33 points in Combat and 18 in Assassination. Dual Wield Specialization (−38 DPS), Improved Sinister Strike (−24), Precision (−18) and Adrenaline Rush (−15) carry the specialization; Hack and Slash, Weapon Expertise and Aggression add 8 to 12 DPS each.

The 18 Assassination points are for Malice (+crit, −21), Improved Slice and Dice (−22) and the gate points Ruthlessness, Murder and Lethality. Puncturing Wounds has no measured effect and Lightning Reflexes (dodge) is not simulated.

The talents that pay the most: Dual Wield Specialization (−38.2 DPS), Improved Sinister Strike (−23.9 DPS), Improved Slice and Dice (−22.4 DPS).

Points with no measured effect in the simulator (gate, utility or an effect that is not simulated): Lightning Reflexes, Murder.

A talent's value is what the specialization loses when it is taken out of the build, on the same character and the same 180-second simulation.

Stat caps

Each curve shows the simulated character's DPS as a stat is added on top of its gear: the left point is the current gear. A dashed line marks the estimated cap, the point where every extra point is worth less than 20% of what it gave at the start.

Stat priority

From the most to the least profitable for one unit of stat added to the simulated gear. Only the stats that bring DPS are listed.

  1. Agility: +0.38 DPS per point
  2. Hit: +0.35 DPS per rating point
  3. Crit: +0.31 DPS per rating point
  4. Haste: +0.31 DPS per rating point
  5. Strength: +0.21 DPS per point
  6. Attack power: +0.20 DPS per point

Rotation

  1. Keep Slice and Dice up.
  2. Sinister Strike to build combo points.
  3. Eviscerate at 5 combo points.
  4. Adrenaline Rush and Blade Flurry on cooldown.
  5. Instant Poison on the main hand, Deadly Poison on the off hand.
