Why these talents

A build has 51 points. A tier-n talent only unlocks after 5 × (n − 1) points spent higher in the same tree (the numbers on the left of each tree give the tier and the points required): some points are therefore gate points, taken to reach a talent further down.

  • Combat: 20 points. The deepest talent, Dual Wield Specialization, is in tier 4 and needs 15 points in the tree.
  • Subtlety: 31 points. The deepest talent, Quietus, is in tier 6 and needs 25 points in the tree.

31 points in Subtlety and 20 in Combat. Most Subtlety talents are stealth and utility (Camouflage, Setup, Initiative, Premeditation...): the simulator does not value them, and the ones it does (Serrated Blades, Hemorrhage, Quietus) are worth about 3 DPS each.

The DPS comes from the 20 Combat points, Dual Wield Specialization (−41) and Precision (−11). This is why the specialization ranks last: its real strengths (stealth opener, Rupture, control) are not simulated.

The talents that pay the most: Dual Wield Specialization (−40.9 DPS), Precision (−11.2 DPS), Hemorrhage (−4.1 DPS).

Points with no measured effect in the simulator (gate, utility or an effect that is not simulated): Camouflage, Lightning Reflexes, Setup, Improved Ambush, Ghostly Strike, Initiative, Improved Distract, Premeditation, Dirty Deeds.

A talent's value is what the specialization loses when it is taken out of the build, on the same character and the same 180-second simulation.

Rotation

  1. Keep Slice and Dice up.
  2. Hemorrhage (or Backstab with daggers) to build combo points.
  3. Eviscerate at 5 combo points.
  4. Instant Poison on the main hand, Deadly Poison on the off hand.
