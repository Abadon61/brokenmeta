Warrior Protection: level 60 build, talents, rotation and threat

This is what our simulator gets for the Warrior Protection at level 60: the gear the optimizer finds among the items known from the beta, the preset talents, the rotation followed and the damage of every ability over a 180-second fight against a stationary boss.

Rotation

  1. Shield Slam on cooldown.
  2. Revenge after a dodge, parry or block.
  3. Heroic Strike with spare rage (it gives more threat per rage point).
  4. Sunder Armor with the rest of the rage.
  5. Bloodrage on cooldown.
