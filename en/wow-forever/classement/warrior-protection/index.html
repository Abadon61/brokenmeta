Why these talents

A build has 51 points. A tier-n talent only unlocks after 5 × (n − 1) points spent higher in the same tree (the numbers on the left of each tree give the tier and the points required): some points are therefore gate points, taken to reach a talent further down.

  • Arms: 11 points. The deepest talent, Improved Charge, is in tier 2 and needs 5 points in the tree.
  • Protection: 40 points. The deepest talent, Shield Slam, is in tier 7 and needs 30 points in the tree.

40 points in Protection and 11 in Arms. For a tank the score measures useful threat while staying alive: Shield Slam (−155 points, 11%) and Defiance (−74, threat) count the most, with Bastion (−49) and Anticipation, Toughness and Improved Revenge (−18 to −21 each) next.

Last Stand, Vanguard and Concussion Blow are survival or control utility that is not simulated; Shield Specialization, Master of Defense, Improved Sunder Armor and Focused Rage measure at zero. The 11 Arms points are Deflection (parry, −23), gate points and Improved Heroic Strike.

The talents that pay the most: Shield Slam (−155.0 score), Defiance (−74.4 score), Bastion (−48.7 score).

Points with no measured effect in the simulator (gate, utility or an effect that is not simulated): Improved Charge, Improved Tactical Mastery, Last Stand, Vanguard, Concussion Blow.

A talent's value is what the specialization loses when it is taken out of the build, on the same character and the same 180-second simulation.

Buffs and consumables in the simulation

Assumptions

A 180-second fight against a stationary level-63 boss, the raid buffs and consumables of the class's family, a standard race, item effects on. Level-60 base stats and several Classic values are estimates, flagged in the simulator. Results will move with the game.
