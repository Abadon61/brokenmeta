Why these talents

A build has 51 points. A tier-n talent only unlocks after 5 × (n − 1) points spent higher in the same tree (the numbers on the left of each tree give the tier and the points required): some points are therefore gate points, taken to reach a talent further down.

  • Affliction: 32 points. The deepest talent, Wrack, is in tier 7 and needs 30 points in the tree.
  • Destruction: 19 points. The deepest talent, Ruin, is in tier 3 and needs 10 points in the tree.

32 points in Affliction and 19 in Destruction. Wrack (−51 DPS), Pandemic (−33), Suppression (−28), Malevolence (−25), Improved Corruption (−25), Shadow Mastery (−23) and Malediction (−16) raise the damage over time; Siphon Life is neutral.

The 19 Destruction points give Bane (−27 DPS, faster spells), Improved Shadow Bolt (−25), Ruin (−17) and Cataclysm (−9). Shadowburn has no measured effect.

The talents that pay the most: Wrack (−50.8 DPS), Bane (−34.2 DPS), Pandemic (−32.6 DPS).

A talent's value is what the specialization loses when it is taken out of the build, on the same character and the same 180-second simulation.

Stat caps

Each curve shows the simulated character's DPS as a stat is added on top of its gear: the left point is the current gear. A dashed line marks the estimated cap, the point where every extra point is worth less than 20% of what it gave at the start.

Stat priority

From the most to the least profitable for one unit of stat added to the simulated gear. Only the stats that bring DPS are listed.

  1. Spell power: +0.62 DPS per point
  2. Hit: +0.48 DPS per rating point (up to about 10.8%)
  3. Crit: +0.41 DPS per rating point
  4. Intellect: +0.20 DPS per point

Rotation

  1. Keep Bane of Agony (the damage-over-time curse), Corruption and Siphon Life up, and Immolate as well.
  2. Wrack on cooldown.
  3. Shadow Bolt as the filler (instant with Nightfall).
  4. Life Tap when mana runs low.
  5. The Imp casts Firebolt continuously.
