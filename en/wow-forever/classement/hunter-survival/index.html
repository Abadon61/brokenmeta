Why these talents

A build has 51 points. A tier-n talent only unlocks after 5 × (n − 1) points spent higher in the same tree (the numbers on the left of each tree give the tier and the points required): some points are therefore gate points, taken to reach a talent further down.

  • Marksmanship: 19 points. The deepest talent, Efficiency, is in tier 2 and needs 5 points in the tree.
  • Survival: 32 points. The deepest talent, Lacerating Strikes, is in tier 7 and needs 30 points in the tree.

32 points in Survival and 19 in Marksmanship. Almost all the DPS comes from Marksmanship: Efficiency (−36 DPS), Careful Aim (−42), Lethal Attacks (−21) and Improved Stings (−8). In Survival only Surefooted (−14) and Lightning Reflexes (−10) pay; Improved Tracking, Survivalist and Clever Traps are unsimulated utility.

The melee-oriented Survival talents (Predator's Edge, Lacerating Strikes, Expose Prey) have no effect for a ranged hunter; they only serve to reach Lightning Reflexes in tier 6. For melee, see the Melee Hunter.

The talents that pay the most: Careful Aim (−41.6 DPS), Efficiency (−36.3 DPS), Lethal Attacks (−20.9 DPS).

Points with no measured effect in the simulator (gate, utility or an effect that is not simulated): Improved Tracking, Hawk Eye, Survivalist, Clever Traps.

A talent's value is what the specialization loses when it is taken out of the build, on the same character and the same 180-second simulation.

Stat caps

Each curve shows the simulated character's DPS as a stat is added on top of its gear: the left point is the current gear. A dashed line marks the estimated cap, the point where every extra point is worth less than 20% of what it gave at the start.

Stat priority

From the most to the least profitable for one unit of stat added to the simulated gear. Only the stats that bring DPS are listed.

  1. Hit: +0.51 DPS per rating point (up to about 7.5%)
  2. Agility: +0.31 DPS per point
  3. Crit: +0.29 DPS per rating point
  4. Intellect: +0.28 DPS per point
  5. Attack power: +0.18 DPS per point
  6. Spirit: +0.08 DPS per point

Rotation

  1. Hunter's Mark on the target before the fight (+110 ranged attack power).
  2. Auto Shot continuously.
  3. Arcane Shot and Multi-Shot on cooldown.
  4. Keep Serpent Sting up.
  5. Rapid Fire on cooldown.
  6. The pet attacks continuously.
