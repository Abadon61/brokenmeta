Warrior Arms: level 60 build, talents, rotation and DPS

This is what our simulator gets for the Warrior Arms at level 60: the gear the optimizer finds among the items known from the beta, the preset talents, the rotation followed and the damage of every ability over a 180-second fight against a stationary boss.

Rotation

  1. Slam as the main strike, right after the main-hand swing, without delaying the next one (Improved Slam): at 15 rage it pays more per rage than Mortal Strike.
  2. Overpower when it lights up: after a target dodge, or from Bloodthrill.
  3. Rend every 21 seconds to feed Bloodthrill.
  4. Execute under 20% health, Heroic Strike with spare rage.
  5. Mortal Strike is not taken: the simulator measures about 10% more DPS with that point elsewhere (to be confirmed with the beta's real rage values).

Why these talents

A build has 51 points. A tier-n talent only unlocks after 5 × (n − 1) points spent higher in the same tree (the numbers on the left of each tree give the tier and the points required): some points are therefore gate points, taken to reach a talent further down.

  • Arms: 35 points. The deepest talent, Improved Slam, is in tier 6 and needs 25 points in the tree.
  • Fury: 16 points. The deepest talent, Improved Execute, is in tier 4 and needs 15 points in the tree.

35 points in Arms and 16 in Fury. Improved Slam (−184 DPS, 30% of the DPS) makes the specialization: it removes Slam's swing delay and makes Slam the main strike (about 16 Slams per minute). Bloodthrill (−43), Impale (−39), Improved Overpower (−32), Cruelty (−31), Two-Handed Weapon Specialization (−22), Anger Management (−15) and Unbridled Wrath (−14) add the rest.

Mortal Strike is not taken: the simulator measured it in the wrong direction (30 rage for a strike barely stronger than Slam, which costs 15) and the build gains about 61 DPS (+11%) by putting that point in Improved Execute. The result depends on how much rage is available, to be confirmed with the beta's real values. Sweeping Strikes and Improved Tactical Mastery are not simulated.

The talents that pay the most: Improved Slam (−183.8 DPS), Bloodthrill (−42.8 DPS), Impale (−38.6 DPS).

Points with no measured effect in the simulator (gate, utility or an effect that is not simulated): Improved Tactical Mastery, Improved Cleave, Sweeping Strikes.

Buffs and consumables in the simulation

Every simulation gets all the buffs a 20-player raid can bring (one class or specialization of each kind) and the consumables of its role: scrolls, flask or elixirs, juju, food. There are no world buffs: they do not exist in WoW: Forever raids.

Assumptions

A 180-second fight against a stationary level-63 boss, the raid buffs and consumables of the class's family, a standard race, item effects on. Level-60 base stats and several Classic values are estimates, flagged in the simulator. Results will move with the game.
