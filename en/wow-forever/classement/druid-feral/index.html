Why these talents

A build has 51 points. A tier-n talent only unlocks after 5 × (n − 1) points spent higher in the same tree (the numbers on the left of each tree give the tier and the points required): some points are therefore gate points, taken to reach a talent further down.

  • Balance: 14 points. The deepest talent, Nature's Majesty, is in tier 2 and needs 5 points in the tree.
  • Feral Combat: 37 points. The deepest talent, Berserk, is in tier 7 and needs 30 points in the tree.

37 points in Feral and 14 in Balance. The energy rotation makes the difference: Shifting Power (−86 DPS, 16% of the DPS) and Improved Shifting Power (−26) regain energy, Shredding Attacks (−73) lowers Shred's cost, Predatory Instincts (−48), Sharpened Claws (−28), Savage Fury (−20), Rend and Tear (−19), Blood Frenzy (−20) and Berserk (−16) follow.

The 14 Balance points pay almost nothing (Nature's Majesty +crit, −18 DPS; Improved Wrath, Genesis and Nature's Reach are at zero): they fill the 51-point total. Thick Hide (armor) has no effect on DPS.

The talents that pay the most: Shifting Power (−86.4 DPS), Shredding Attacks (−72.9 DPS), Predatory Instincts (−48.4 DPS).

A talent's value is what the specialization loses when it is taken out of the build, on the same character and the same 180-second simulation.

Rotation

  1. Keep Savage Roar and Rip up.
  2. Shred to build combo points, keep Rake up.
  3. Ferocious Bite at 5 combo points.
  4. Tiger's Fury on cooldown, Shifting Power to regain energy, Berserk on cooldown.
