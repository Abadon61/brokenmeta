Shaman Elemental: level 60 build, talents, rotation and DPS

This is what our simulator gets for the Shaman Elemental at level 60: the gear the optimizer finds among the items known from the beta, the preset talents, the rotation followed and the damage of every ability over a 180-second fight against a stationary boss.

Rotation

  1. Searing Totem dropped every 55 seconds.
  2. Keep Flame Shock up.
  3. Lava Burst on cooldown (+20% damage if Flame Shock is up).
  4. Lightning Bolt as the filler; Lightning Overload adds free bolts.
  5. Mana limits the fight: mana potion and gem as soon as they pay off, and the Mindfulness points (regeneration while casting) are worth more than any remaining damage talent.
  6. Chain Lightning, Earth Shock and Fire Nova cost too much mana for one target and are not cast (measured: -43 to -65 DPS).

Why these talents

A build has 51 points. A tier-n talent only unlocks after 5 × (n − 1) points spent higher in the same tree (the numbers on the left of each tree give the tier and the points required): some points are therefore gate points, taken to reach a talent further down.

  • Elemental: 31 points. The deepest talent, Lava Burst, is in tier 7 and needs 30 points in the tree.
  • Enhancement: 10 points. The deepest talent, Thundering Strikes, is in tier 1 and needs 0 points in the tree.
  • Restoration: 10 points. The deepest talent, Mindfulness, is in tier 2 and needs 5 points in the tree.

The build spends 31 points in Elemental to reach Lava Burst (tier 7, 30 points required), 10 in Enhancement for Thundering Strikes (+5% crit) and Ancestral Knowledge (+10% intellect), and 10 in Restoration.

Mana is the limit, not raw damage: Mindfulness (50% of mana regeneration continues while casting) is worth as much as Elemental Alacrity, the best damage talent. It needs 5 points in the first Restoration row, hence Totemic Focus (cheaper totem, almost no effect) as a gate point.

Reverberation (shock cooldown) is worth almost nothing: the rotation only casts Flame Shock, which never waits for its cooldown. It stays at 4 ranks because Lava Burst needs 30 points in the tree. Tidal Focus adds 2% hit (assumption: it applies to offensive spells).

Elemental Devastation and Improved Fire Nova are not taken: the first does not help a caster (it gives melee crit), the second powers a spell that is switched off on one target.

The talents that pay the most: Mindfulness (−54.4 DPS), Elemental Alacrity (−52.3 DPS), Elemental Fury (−29.5 DPS).
