Why these talents

A build has 51 points. A tier-n talent only unlocks after 5 × (n − 1) points spent higher in the same tree (the numbers on the left of each tree give the tier and the points required): some points are therefore gate points, taken to reach a talent further down.

  • Elemental: 31 points. The deepest talent, Elemental Mastery, is in tier 7 and needs 30 points in the tree.
  • Enhancement: 10 points. The deepest talent, Thundering Strikes, is in tier 1 and needs 0 points in the tree.
  • Restoration: 10 points. The deepest talent, Mindfulness, is in tier 2 and needs 5 points in the tree.

31 in Elemental and 10 in Enhancement and 10 in Restoration. The talents that weigh the most (DPS lost without them, measured in the simulator): Elemental Alacrity (−85, 16%), Call of Flame (−63, 12%), Mindfulness (−54), Elemental Fury (−53), Convection (−30), Tidal Focus (−27), Elemental Focus (−22).

Measured at about zero or not simulated (gate points, filler or non-damage effects): Reverberation, Totemic Focus.

Elemental Mastery (tier 7 talent: Lightning Bolt and Lava Burst cast 0.5 s faster and Chain Lightning has no cooldown for 15 s, every 3 minutes) replaces Call of Thunder; Call of Flame also lengthens Flame Shock. Fire Nova (lower damage but lower cost), Chain Lightning (-35% cost) and Earth Shock were measured again: they stay worse than Lightning Bolt and Lava Burst on one target (about -10 to -19% DPS).

The talents that pay the most: Elemental Alacrity (−85.1 DPS), Call of Flame (−63.2 DPS), Mindfulness (−53.9 DPS).

A talent's value is what the specialization loses when it is taken out of the build, on the same character and the same 180-second simulation.

Rotation

  1. Searing Totem dropped every 55 seconds.
  2. Keep Flame Shock up.
  3. Lava Burst on cooldown (+20% damage if Flame Shock is up).
  4. Lightning Bolt as the filler; Lightning Overload adds free bolts.
  5. Mana limits the fight: mana potion and gem as soon as they pay off, and the Mindfulness points (regeneration while casting) are worth more than any remaining damage talent.
  6. Chain Lightning, Earth Shock and Fire Nova take the place of a Lightning Bolt or a Lava Burst, which pay more on one target, and are not cast (measured: about −10 to −19% DPS).

Assumptions

A 180-second fight against a stationary level-63 boss, the raid buffs and consumables of the class's family, a standard race (Human, Orc for Shaman) with the racials read from the game client (WoW Classic's weapon skill no longer exists in Forever: weapon specializations give critical strike chance), item effects on. Level-60 base stats and several Classic values are estimates, flagged in the simulator. Results will move with the game.

The core mechanics (attack table, rage, mana regeneration, resistances, Classic base stats and race bonuses) come from the open-source simulator WoWSims (MIT licence): github.com/wowsims/classic
