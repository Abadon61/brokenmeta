Why these talents

A build has 51 points. A tier-n talent only unlocks after 5 × (n − 1) points spent higher in the same tree (the numbers on the left of each tree give the tier and the points required): some points are therefore gate points, taken to reach a talent further down.

  • Discipline: 19 points. The deepest talent, Mental Strength, is in tier 4 and needs 15 points in the tree.
  • Shadow: 32 points. The deepest talent, Shadowform, is in tier 7 and needs 30 points in the tree.

32 points in Shadow and 19 in Discipline. Shadowform (−133 DPS, 23% of the DPS) and Mind Flay (without it the rotation has no filler spell: −190 DPS) are essential; Darkness (−52), Shadow Weaving (−48), Improved Mind Flay (−36) and Shadow Focus (+hit, −27) come next.

The 19 Discipline points pay little: Twin Disciplines (−10 DPS), the rest (Mental Agility, Inner Focus, Meditation, Mental Strength) is worth less than 2 DPS in this simulation. Power in Light (healing) and Spirit Tap are not simulated.

The talents that pay the most: Mind Flay (−190.3 DPS), Shadowform (−133.0 DPS), Darkness (−51.5 DPS).

Points with no measured effect in the simulator (gate, utility or an effect that is not simulated): Spirit Tap, Power in Light, Vampiric Embrace.

Buffs and consumables in the simulation

Every simulation gets all the buffs a 20-player raid can bring (one class or specialization of each kind) and the consumables of its role: scrolls, flask or elixirs, juju, food. There are no world buffs: they do not exist in WoW: Forever raids.

  • Arcane Brilliance (Mage) — +31 Intellect
  • Greater Blessing of Kings (Paladin) — +10% to all stats
  • Greater Blessing of Wisdom (Paladin) — +40 mana / 5 s
  • Greater Blessing of Might (Paladin) — +133 attack power
  • Gift of the Wild (Druid) — +16 Strength, +16 Agility, +16 Stamina, +16 Intellect, +16 Spirit
  • Prayer of Spirit (Priest) — +40 Spirit
  • Prayer of Fortitude (Priest) — +70 Stamina
  • Mana Spring Totem (Shaman) — +25 mana / 5 s

  • Juju Guile — +30 Intellect

Not simulated: Windfury Totem, Power Infusion, Innervate and Judgements (proc or cooldown effects). Values come from the beta client tables; weapon oils are Classic estimates. The consumables worth the most for this specialization are ranked in its guide.

Assumptions

A 180-second fight against a stationary level-63 boss, the raid buffs and consumables of the class's family, a standard race, item effects on. Level-60 base stats and several Classic values are estimates, flagged in the simulator. Results will move with the game.
