Why these talents

A build has 51 points. A tier-n talent only unlocks after 5 × (n − 1) points spent higher in the same tree (the numbers on the left of each tree give the tier and the points required): some points are therefore gate points, taken to reach a talent further down.

  • Discipline: 19 points. The deepest talent, Mental Agility, is in tier 3 and needs 10 points in the tree.
  • Shadow: 32 points. The deepest talent, Shadowform, is in tier 7 and needs 30 points in the tree.

32 in Shadow and 19 in Discipline. The talents that weigh the most (DPS lost without them, measured in the simulator): Mind Flay (−214, 31%), Shadowform (−213, 31%), Darkness (−63), Shadow Weaving (−59), Improved Mind Flay (−46), Shadow Focus (−32), Twin Disciplines (−14).

Measured at about zero or not simulated (gate points, filler or non-damage effects): Spirit Tap, Improved Shadow Word: Pain, Vampiric Embrace, Devouring Contagion, Power in Light, Mental Agility, Inner Focus, Meditation.

Devouring Plague comes back every 24 s (60 s before the patch), Shadow Word: Death every 18 s and Improved Mind Flay raises each Mind Flay tick by 10 / 20% over the previous one. Mental Strength only gives 2% Intellect per rank now.

The talents that pay the most: Mind Flay (−214.1 DPS), Shadowform (−213.2 DPS), Darkness (−62.8 DPS).

Points with no measured effect in the simulator (gate, utility or an effect that is not simulated): Spirit Tap, Power in Light, Vampiric Embrace.

A talent's value is what the specialization loses when it is taken out of the build, on the same character and the same 180-second simulation.

Rotation

  1. Keep Shadow Word: Pain and Devouring Plague up.
  2. Mind Blast on cooldown.
  3. Shadow Word: Death when ready.
  4. Mind Flay as the filler.
  5. Inner Focus for mana.

Assumptions

A 180-second fight against a stationary level-63 boss, the raid buffs and consumables of the class's family, a standard race (Human, Orc for Shaman) with the racials read from the beta client (WoW Classic's weapon skill no longer exists in Forever: weapon specializations give critical strike chance), item effects on. Level-60 base stats and several Classic values are estimates, flagged in the simulator. Results will move with the game.
