Why these talents

A build has 51 points. A tier-n talent only unlocks after 5 × (n − 1) points spent higher in the same tree (the numbers on the left of each tree give the tier and the points required): some points are therefore gate points, taken to reach a talent further down.

  • Discipline: 19 points. The deepest talent, Mental Strength, is in tier 4 and needs 15 points in the tree.
  • Shadow: 32 points. The deepest talent, Shadowform, is in tier 7 and needs 30 points in the tree.

32 points in Shadow and 19 in Discipline. Shadowform (−187 DPS, 28% of the DPS) and Mind Flay (without it the rotation has no filler spell: −190 DPS) are essential; Darkness (−60), Shadow Weaving (−56), Improved Mind Flay (−42) and Shadow Focus (+hit, −27) come next.

The 19 Discipline points pay little: Twin Disciplines (−12 DPS), the rest (Mental Agility, Inner Focus, Meditation, Mental Strength) is worth less than 2 DPS in this simulation. Power in Light (healing) and Spirit Tap are not simulated.

The talents that pay the most: Mind Flay (−225.6 DPS), Shadowform (−187.4 DPS), Darkness (−60.1 DPS).

Points with no measured effect in the simulator (gate, utility or an effect that is not simulated): Spirit Tap, Power in Light, Vampiric Embrace.

A talent's value is what the specialization loses when it is taken out of the build, on the same character and the same 180-second simulation.

Each curve shows the simulated character's DPS as a stat is added on top of its gear: the left point is the current gear. A dashed line marks the estimated cap, the point where every extra point is worth less than 20% of what it gave at the start.

Stat priority

From the most to the least profitable for one unit of stat added to the simulated gear. Only the stats that bring DPS are listed.

  1. Hit: +0.58 DPS per rating point (up to about 12.8%)
  2. Spell power: +0.45 DPS per point
  3. Crit: +0.39 DPS per rating point
  4. Intellect: +0.11 DPS per point

Rotation

  1. Keep Shadow Word: Pain and Devouring Plague up.
  2. Mind Blast on cooldown.
  3. Shadow Word: Death when ready.
  4. Mind Flay as the filler.
  5. Inner Focus for mana.
