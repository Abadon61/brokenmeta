Wand Specialization Discipline · Tier 1 · 2 ranks · Icy Veins : 2 pts · Recommended

Icy Veins (level 30): Increases your damage with Wands by 13%.

Twin Disciplines Discipline · Tier 1 · 5 ranks · Icy Veins : 3 pts · Recommended

Icy Veins (level 30): Increases the damage and healing of your instant cast spells by 1%.

Holy Precision Discipline · Tier 2 · 3 ranks · Icy Veins : 1 pts · Recommended

Icy Veins (level 30): Improves your chance to hit with Holy spells by 6%.

Improved Power Word: Shield Discipline · Tier 2 · 3 ranks · Icy Veins : 3 pts · Recommended

Icy Veins (level 30): Increases the damage absorbed by your Power Word: Shield by 7%.

Martyrdom Discipline · Tier 2 · 2 ranks · Icy Veins : 1 pts · Recommended

Icy Veins (level 30): Gives you a 50% chance to gain Focused Casting for 6 sec after being the victim of a melee or ranged critical strike. The Focused Casting effect prevents you from losing casting time when taking damage and increases your resistance to Interrupt effects by 20%.

Mental Agility Discipline · Tier 3 · 3 ranks · Icy Veins : 1 pts · Recommended

Icy Veins (level 30): Reduces the mana cost of your Smite, Holy Fire, and instant cast spells by 3%.

Inner Focus Discipline · Tier 3 · 1 rank · Icy Veins : 1 pts · Recommended

Icy Veins (level 30): When activated, reduces the Mana cost of your next spell by 100% and increases its critical effect chance by 25% if it is a non-periodic spell and capable of a critical effect.

Meditation Discipline · Tier 3 · 3 ranks · Icy Veins : 3 pts · Recommended

Icy Veins (level 30): Allows 17% of your Mana regeneration to continue while casting.

Soul Warding Discipline · Tier 4 · 1 rank · Icy Veins : 1 pts · Recommended

Icy Veins (level 30): Reduces the cooldown on your Power Word: Shield spell by -4 sec and reduces its mana cost by 15%.

Spirit Tap Shadow · Tier 1 · 5 ranks · Icy Veins : 5 pts · Recommended

Icy Veins (level 30): Gives you a 20% chance to increase your Spirit by 100% for 15 sec after killing a non-trivial target or when an enemy afflicted by your Vampiric Embrace dies. For the duration, 50% of your Mana regeneration will continue while casting.

Stat priority

In order of importance, according to Icy Veins.

  1. Healing Power
  2. Mp5
  3. Spirit
  4. Intellect
  5. Critical Strike

Consumables

What the Icy Veins guide lists for this specialization.

  • Water (less delay between pulls)
  • Food (health between pulls)
  • Healing potions
  • Mana potions
  • Bandages (a heal over time, usable in combat and on others)

Suggested professions

  • Engineering (with Mining, especially for Gnomes)
  • Alchemy + Herbalism
  • Tailoring + Enchanting (gear)
  • First Aid (secondary)

The last-tier talents

These are the deepest talents in the tree: they require 30 points already spent in the specialization. Official game text, at maximum rank.

Power Infusion Tier 7 · 1 rank

Infuses the target with power, increasing their spell damage and healing done by 20% for 15 sec.

Single-rank talents

Talents you take only once, with their tier (official text at maximum rank).

Inner Focus Tier 3 · 1 rank

When activated, reduces the Mana cost of your next spell by 100% and increases its critical effect chance by 25% if it is a non-periodic spell and capable of a critical effect.

Soul Warding Tier 4 · 1 rank

Reduces the cooldown on your Power Word: Shield spell by -4 sec and reduces its mana cost by 15%.

Penance Tier 5 · 1 rank

Launches a volley of holy light at the target, causing 81 Holy damage to an enemy, or 184 healing to an ally, instantly and every 1 sec for 2 sec.
