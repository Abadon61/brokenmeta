Twin Disciplines Discipline · Tier 1 · 5 ranks · 5 pts · Recommended

Rank 5/5: Increases the damage and healing of your instant cast spells by 5%.

Improved Power Word: Shield Discipline · Tier 2 · 3 ranks · 3 pts · Recommended

Rank 3/3: Increases the damage absorbed by your Power Word: Shield by 20%.

Martyrdom Discipline · Tier 2 · 2 ranks · 2 pts · Recommended

Rank 2/2: Gives you a 100% chance to gain Focused Casting for 6 sec after being the victim of a melee or ranged critical strike. The Focused Casting effect prevents you from losing casting time when taking damage and increases your resistance to Interrupt effects by 20%.

Mental Agility Discipline · Tier 3 · 3 ranks · 3 pts · Recommended

Rank 3/3: Reduces the mana cost of your Smite, Holy Fire, and instant cast spells by 10%.

Inner Focus Discipline · Tier 3 · 1 rank · 1 pts · Recommended

Rank 1/1: When activated, reduces the Mana cost of your next spell by 100% and increases its critical effect chance by 25% if it is a non-periodic spell and capable of a critical effect.

Meditation Discipline · Tier 3 · 3 ranks · 3 pts · Recommended

Rank 3/3: Allows 50% of your Mana regeneration to continue while casting.

Mental Strength Discipline · Tier 4 · 5 ranks · 5 pts · Recommended

Rank 5/5: Increases your total Intellect by 15%.

Soul Warding Discipline · Tier 4 · 1 rank · 1 pts · Recommended

Rank 1/1: Reduces the cooldown on your Power Word: Shield spell by -4 sec and reduces its mana cost by 15%.

Penance Discipline · Tier 5 · 1 rank · 1 pts · Recommended

Rank 1/1: Launches a volley of holy light at the target, causing 81 Holy damage to an enemy, or 184 healing to an ally, instantly and every 1 sec for 2 sec.

Renewed Hope Discipline · Tier 5 · 5 ranks · 3 pts · Recommended

Rank 3/5: Your heals from Flash Heal, Binding Heal, Lesser Heal, Heal, Greater Heal, and Penance gain 6% increased critical strike chance when cast on targets with Weakened Soul, and reduce the remaining duration of Weakened Soul on their target by -3 sec.

Divine Aegis Discipline · Tier 6 · 3 ranks · 3 pts · Recommended

Rank 3/3: Your critical heals create a protective shield on the target, absorbing 15% of the amount healed. Lasts 12 sec.

Power Infusion Discipline · Tier 7 · 1 rank · 1 pts · Recommended

Rank 1/1: Infuses the target with power, increasing their spell damage and healing done by 20% for 15 sec.

Improved Renew Holy · Tier 1 · 3 ranks · 3 pts · Recommended

Rank 3/3: Increases the amount healed by your Renew spell by 15%.

Holy Specialization Holy · Tier 1 · 5 ranks · 5 pts · Recommended

Rank 5/5: Increases the critical effect chance of your Holy spells by 5%.

Divine Fury Holy · Tier 2 · 5 ranks · 5 pts · Recommended

Rank 5/5: Reduces the casting time of your Smite, Holy Fire, Heal, and Greater Heal spells by 0.5 sec.

Inspiration Holy · Tier 3 · 3 ranks · 3 pts · Recommended

Rank 3/3: Your non-periodic critical heals increase your target's Armor by 25% for 15 sec.

Improved Healing Holy · Tier 4 · 3 ranks · 3 pts · Recommended

Rank 3/3: Reduces the Mana cost of your Lesser Heal, Heal, Greater Heal, Penance, and Prayer of Mending spells by 15%.

Binding Heal Holy · Tier 4 · 1 rank · 1 pts · Recommended

Rank 1/1: Heals a friendly target and the caster for 247. Low threat.

Level 60 rotation

Healing is not simulated yet: this is the healing rotation Icy Veins recommends (level 30), reworded.

  1. Power Word: Shield on the target about to be hit (942 absorbed for 500 mana: inefficient, use it for timing, not volume).
  2. Penance on cooldown (12 s cooldown, instant).
  3. Renew on the tank (3.35 healing per mana) and Greater Heal as the main heal (3.18 per mana); critical heals create a shield with Divine Aegis.
  4. Flash Heal only in emergencies (2.77 per mana); Prayer of Mending on cooldown.
  5. Inner Focus before a big heal, Power Infusion on yourself or another healer.

Healing is not simulated in combat. This rotation is derived from the client numbers (base heal, healing-power coefficient, mana, cast time) at 350 healing power and without talents: it gives an efficiency order, not a simulation result.

What Icy Veins recommends (level 30)

  • Power Word: Shield as needed or to buy time; Heal for a moderate heal; Renew against chip damage.
  • Flash Heal only in emergencies (costly); Prayer of Healing when the party is hurt; cleanse when it is worth the mana.

Our comparison

Agreed with Icy Veins on Power Word: Shield, Heal, Renew and Flash Heal in emergencies. The numbers show Power Word: Shield is the least mana-efficient (1.9 per mana): it buys time, it does not heal in volume. Penance and Divine Aegis come from the Discipline talents.
