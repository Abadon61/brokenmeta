Wand Specialization Discipline · Tier 1 · 2 ranks · Icy Veins : 2 pts · Recommended

Icy Veins (level 30): Increases your damage with Wands by 13%.

Twilight Focus Holy · Tier 1 · 3 ranks · Icy Veins : 3 pts · Recommended

Icy Veins (level 30): Gives you a 23% chance to avoid interruption caused by damage while casting any spell.

Shadow Focus Shadow · Tier 1 · 5 ranks · Icy Veins : 1 pts · Recommended

Icy Veins (level 30): Improves your chance to hit with Shadow spells by 1%.

Spirit Tap Shadow · Tier 1 · 5 ranks · Icy Veins : 5 pts · Recommended

Icy Veins (level 30): Gives you a 20% chance to increase your Spirit by 100% for 15 sec after killing a non-trivial target or when an enemy afflicted by your Vampiric Embrace dies. For the duration, 50% of your Mana regeneration will continue while casting.

Improved Shadow Word: Pain Shadow · Tier 2 · 2 ranks · Icy Veins : 2 pts · Recommended

Icy Veins (level 30): Increases the duration of your Shadow Word: Pain spell by 3 sec.

Shadow Reach Shadow · Tier 2 · 2 ranks · Icy Veins : 2 pts · Recommended

Icy Veins (level 30): Increases the range of your offensive Shadow spells by 10%.

Improved Mind Blast Shadow · Tier 3 · 5 ranks · Icy Veins : 2 pts · Recommended

Icy Veins (level 30): Reduces the cooldown of your Mind Blast spell by 0.5 sec.

Mind Flay Shadow · Tier 3 · 1 rank · Icy Veins : 1 pts · Recommended

Icy Veins (level 30): Assault the target's mind with Shadow energy, causing 63 Shadow damage over 3 sec and slowing their movement speed by 50%.

Improved Mind Flay Shadow · Tier 3 · 2 ranks · Icy Veins : 2 pts · Recommended

Icy Veins (level 30): Your Mind Flay now deals 10% more damage, gains 5 yards increased range, but slows the target's movement speed by 35%.

Vampiric Embrace Shadow · Tier 4 · 1 rank · Icy Veins : 1 pts · Recommended

Icy Veins (level 30): Afflicts your target with Shadow energy that causes all party members to be healed for 20% of any Shadow spell damage you deal for 30 sec.

The last-tier talents

These are the deepest talents in the tree: they require 30 points already spent in the specialization. Official game text, at maximum rank.

Shadowform Tier 7 · 1 rank

Assume Shadowform, increasing your Shadow damage by 10%, reducing the Mana cost of all Shadow spells by 50%, increasing the critical strike damage bonus of your Shadow spells by 100%, and reducing Physical damage taken by you by 15%. However, you may not cast healing spells while in this form.

Single-rank talents

Talents you take only once, with their tier (official text at maximum rank).

Mind Flay Tier 3 · 1 rank

Assault the target's mind with Shadow energy, causing 63 Shadow damage over 3 sec and slowing their movement speed by 50%.

Vampiric Embrace Tier 4 · 1 rank

Afflicts your target with Shadow energy that causes all party members to be healed for 20% of any Shadow spell damage you deal for 30 sec.

Silence Tier 5 · 1 rank

Silences the target, preventing them from casting spells for 5 sec and interrupting their spellcasts for 3 sec.
