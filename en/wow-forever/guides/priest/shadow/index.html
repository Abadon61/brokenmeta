Shadow Focus Shadow · Tier 1 · 5 ranks · 5 pts · Recommended

Rank 5/5: Improves your chance to hit with Shadow spells by 5%.

Spirit Tap Shadow · Tier 1 · 5 ranks · 5 pts · Recommended

Rank 5/5: Gives you a 100% chance to increase your Spirit by 100% for 15 sec after killing a non-trivial target or when an enemy afflicted by your Vampiric Embrace dies. For the duration, 50% of your Mana regeneration will continue while casting.

Improved Shadow Word: Pain Shadow · Tier 2 · 2 ranks · 2 pts · Recommended

Rank 2/2: Increases the duration of your Shadow Word: Pain spell by 6 sec.

Improved Mind Blast Shadow · Tier 3 · 5 ranks · 5 pts · Recommended

Rank 5/5: Reduces the cooldown of your Mind Blast spell by 2.5 sec.

Mind Flay Shadow · Tier 3 · 1 rank · 1 pts · Recommended

Rank 1/1: Assault the target's mind with Shadow energy, causing 63 Shadow damage over 3 sec and slowing their movement speed by 50%.

Improved Mind Flay Shadow · Tier 3 · 2 ranks · 2 pts · Recommended

Rank 2/2: Your Mind Flay now deals 20% more damage, gains 10 yards increased range, but slows the target's movement speed by 20%.

Vampiric Embrace Shadow · Tier 4 · 1 rank · 1 pts · Recommended

Rank 1/1: Afflicts your target with Shadow energy that causes all party members to be healed for 20% of any Shadow spell damage you deal for 30 sec.

Shadow Weaving Shadow · Tier 4 · 3 ranks · 3 pts · Recommended

Rank 3/3: Your Shadow damage spells have a 100% chance to increase the Shadow damage you deal by 2% for 15 sec, stacking up to 5 times.

Devouring Contagion Shadow · Tier 5 · 2 ranks · 2 pts · Recommended

Rank 2/2: Reduces the mana cost of your Devouring Plague by 50%. Targets that die while Devouring Plague is active spreads it, jumping to a nearby enemy within 10 yds for the remaining duration.

Darkness Shadow · Tier 6 · 5 ranks · 5 pts · Recommended

Rank 5/5: Increases your Shadow damage done by 10%.

Shadowform Shadow · Tier 7 · 1 rank · 1 pts · Recommended

Rank 1/1: Assume Shadowform, increasing your Shadow damage by 10%, reducing the Mana cost of all Shadow spells by 50%, increasing the critical strike damage bonus of your Shadow spells by 100%, and reducing Physical damage taken by you by 15%. However, you may not cast healing spells while in this form.

Power in Light Discipline · Tier 1 · 5 ranks · 5 pts · Recommended

Rank 5/5: Your Smite and Penance spells deal 10% increased damage to targets afflicted with your Holy Fire.

Twin Disciplines Discipline · Tier 1 · 5 ranks · 5 pts · Recommended

Rank 5/5: Increases the damage and healing of your instant cast spells by 5%.

Mental Agility Discipline · Tier 3 · 3 ranks · 3 pts · Recommended

Rank 3/3: Reduces the mana cost of your Smite, Holy Fire, and instant cast spells by 10%.

Inner Focus Discipline · Tier 3 · 1 rank · 1 pts · Recommended

Rank 1/1: When activated, reduces the Mana cost of your next spell by 100% and increases its critical effect chance by 25% if it is a non-periodic spell and capable of a critical effect.

Meditation Discipline · Tier 3 · 3 ranks · 3 pts · Recommended

Rank 3/3: Allows 50% of your Mana regeneration to continue while casting.

Mental Strength Discipline · Tier 4 · 5 ranks · 2 pts · Recommended

How to read these measures

Measured by the simulator on the character above: the DPS gained by one more point of each stat, with the same random draws on both sides. The relative value compares each stat with one point of spell power. Crit, hit and haste are counted per rating point, like Strength or Agility, so they compare directly; they respect the caps: hit at the cap adds nothing. A value near zero or negative is within the margin of error or tied to a limit (mana, cap).

DPS gained when points of each stat are added on top of the simulated gear. Click a stat (or its row in the table) to bring its curve forward; the ring marks the estimated cap, past which every point is worth less than 20% of what it gave at the start.

Level 60 rotation

  1. Keep Shadow Word: Pain and Devouring Plague up.
  2. Mind Blast on cooldown.
  3. Shadow Word: Death when ready.
  4. Mind Flay as the filler.
  5. Inner Focus for mana.
Method, Icy Veins recommendations and comparison

Our simulator's priority at level 60, set against what Icy Veins recommends. Their guides are written for the level-30 beta and for leveling: we keep what everyone agrees on, adopt what the simulation confirmed at level 60 and say where the two differ.

What Icy Veins recommends (level 30)

  • Power Word: Shield and Inner Fire, Mind Blast to pull, then Devouring Plague, Shadow Word: Pain and Mind Flay (watch your mana).
  • Finish with the wand to save mana; Smite instead of Mind Flay before it is learned.

Our comparison

Agreed: Mind Blast, Devouring Plague, Shadow Word: Pain, Mind Flay as the filler, plus Shadow Word: Death. Finishing with the wand is a leveling habit, not part of the rotation against a boss.

Method and enchant notes

The gear our optimizer picks for this specialization among the items known from the beta (dungeons, the few raid items already known and crafted gear, which can be bought or made for you: prices and materials are not taken into account), with item effects simulated. Nothing is hand-picked: it is the result of the ranking's computation, and it will change as new items are discovered.

Enchantments up to skill 300, valued by the simulator when they give flat stats or flat weapon damage. The character does not need the profession: they can be bought or applied by an enchanter; Leatherworking armor kits (chest, legs, feet, hands) and Engineering scopes are counted too. A kit and an enchantment share the same permanent slot: a piece carries one or the other, and the legs, which have no enchantment, get the kit; spell-proc enchants (Crusader, Fiery Weapon...) and school damage are not simulated. The beta data has no ring enchantment up to skill 300.
