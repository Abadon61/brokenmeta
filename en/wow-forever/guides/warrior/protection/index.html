The last-tier talents

These are the deepest talents in the tree: they require 30 points already spent in the specialization. Official game text, at maximum rank.

Shield Slam Tier 7 · 1 rank

Slam the target with your shield, causing 430 damage, increased by your Block Value, and has a 50% chance of dispelling 1 magic effect on the target. Causes a very high amount of threat.

Single-rank talents

Talents you take only once, with their tier (official text at maximum rank).

Last Stand Tier 3 · 1 rank

When activated, this ability temporarily grants you 30% of your maximum health for 20 sec. After the effect expires, the health is lost.

Vanguard Tier 4 · 1 rank

Your Charge ability is now usable while in Defensive Stance.

Concussion Blow Tier 5 · 1 rank

Stuns the target for 5 sec.
