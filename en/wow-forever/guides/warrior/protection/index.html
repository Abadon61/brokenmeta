Shield Specialization Protection · Tier 1 · 5 ranks · Icy Veins : 5 pts · Recommended

Icy Veins (level 30): Increases your chance to Block attacks with your shield by 1% and grants you a 20% chance to generate 5 Rage when you Block.

Anticipation Protection · Tier 1 · 5 ranks · Icy Veins : 2 pts · Recommended

Icy Veins (level 30): Increases your Defense Skill by 4.

Improved Thunder Clap Protection · Tier 2 · 3 ranks · Icy Veins : 3 pts · Recommended

Icy Veins (level 30): Reduces the Rage cost of your Thunder Clap ability by 2.

Master of Defense Protection · Tier 3 · 2 ranks · Icy Veins : 2 pts · Recommended

Icy Veins (level 30): Grants you a 50% chance to generate 5 Rage when you Dodge or Parry while a shield is equipped.

Improved Disarm Protection · Tier 4 · 3 ranks · Icy Veins : 3 pts · Recommended

Icy Veins (level 30): Reduces the cooldown of your Disarm ability by 7 secs.

Defiance Protection · Tier 3 · 3 ranks · Icy Veins : 3 pts · Recommended

Icy Veins (level 30): Increases all threat generated in Defensive stance by an additional 5% while a shield is equipped.

Improved Sunder Armor Protection · Tier 4 · 3 ranks · Icy Veins : 1 pts · Recommended

Icy Veins (level 30): Reduces the Rage cost of your Sunder Armor ability by 1.

Improved Shield Bash Protection · Tier 5 · 2 ranks · Icy Veins : 1 pts · Recommended

Icy Veins (level 30): Gives your Shield Bash ability a 50% chance to Silence the target for 3 sec.

Concussion Blow Protection · Tier 5 · 1 rank · Icy Veins : 1 pts · Recommended

Icy Veins (level 30): Stuns the target for 5 sec.

The last-tier talents

These are the deepest talents in the tree: they require 30 points already spent in the specialization. Official game text, at maximum rank.

Shield Slam Tier 7 · 1 rank

Slam the target with your shield, causing 430 damage, increased by your Block Value, and has a 50% chance of dispelling 1 magic effect on the target. Causes a very high amount of threat.

Single-rank talents

Talents you take only once, with their tier (official text at maximum rank).

Last Stand Tier 3 · 1 rank

When activated, this ability temporarily grants you 30% of your maximum health for 20 sec. After the effect expires, the health is lost.

Vanguard Tier 4 · 1 rank

Your Charge ability is now usable while in Defensive Stance.

Concussion Blow Tier 5 · 1 rank

Stuns the target for 5 sec.
