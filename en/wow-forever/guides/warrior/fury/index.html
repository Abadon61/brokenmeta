Fury Warrior: specialization guide

Melee DPS #11 in the ranking · 562 DPS

Fury is the Melee DPS specialization of the Warrior in WoW: Forever. This page summarizes its talent tree as it appears in the beta client (build 1.60.1.70425); the role is the one stated by the Icy Veins guide for the specialization.

Quick answers: Fury Warrior

Is Fury Warrior a good spec in WoW Forever?

In our level-60 simulation (180 seconds on a stationary boss, raid buffs, best known gear), Fury Warrior ranks #11 of 20 DPS specs with 562 DPS. #1 is Affliction Warlock (754 DPS). A stationary boss does not tell everything: group utility and moving fights matter too.

Which talents should Fury Warrior take?

Our simulator's build uses 51 points, split Arms 14 / Fury 37. The talents that weigh the most in the simulation: Flurry, Dual Wield Specialization, Bloodthirst. Open this build in the talent calculator →

What gear should Fury Warrior wear?

Without raid items, the best set the simulator finds gives 550 DPS, −2.1% against the set that includes raid items. Stat priority: Weapon Damage > Hit Chance > Critical Strike. Full gear and enchantments →

What rotation does Fury Warrior play?

Opening: Bloodthirst whenever it is ready ; Whirlwind on cooldown (3 rage cheaper with Raging Blows) ; Execute under 20% target health. Full rotation →

Which race should Fury Warrior pick?

The top race in our simulation: High Order Skyborne / Windshaper Skyborne. All races of the class →

The first talent point comes at level 10, then one per level up to 51 at level 60. The guide gives the talent order step by step (derived from the level-60 build, not tested while leveling), the dungeons of each level band and the spec's best item in each. See the leveling →

How to read these measures

Measured by the simulator on the character above: the DPS gained by one more point of each stat, with the same random draws on both sides. The relative value compares each stat with one point of attack power. Crit, hit and haste are counted per rating point, like Strength or Agility, so they compare directly; they respect the caps: hit at the cap adds nothing. A value near zero or negative is within the margin of error or tied to a limit (mana, cap).

DPS gained when points of each stat are added on top of the simulated gear. Click a stat (or its row in the table) to bring its curve forward; the ring marks the estimated cap, past which every point is worth less than 20% of what it gave at the start.

Level 60 rotation

  1. Bloodthirst whenever it is ready.
  2. Whirlwind on cooldown (3 rage cheaper with Raging Blows).
  3. Execute under 20% target health.
  4. Heroic Strike on the next swing with spare rage.
  5. Berserker Rage, Bloodrage, Death Wish and Recklessness on cooldown.
Method, Icy Veins recommendations and comparison

Our simulator's priority at level 60, set against what Icy Veins recommends. Their guides are written for the level-30 beta and for leveling: we keep what everyone agrees on, adopt what the simulation confirmed at level 60 and say where the two differ.

What Icy Veins recommends (level 30)

  • Keep Battle Shout up, Bloodrage between pulls, Charge to open, Demoralizing Shout on the target.
  • Victory Rush after a kill, Rend early, Overpower after a dodge, Sunder Armor once or twice.
  • Execute is not recommended while leveling.

Our comparison

Against a level-60 boss, Bloodthirst, Whirlwind, Heroic Strike and Execute (under 20% health) do the damage. Victory Rush (after a kill) and Demoralizing Shout do not apply to a boss. Rend only pays off for Arms with Bloodthrill (measured).

Method and enchant notes

The gear our optimizer picks for this specialization among the items known from the beta (dungeons, the few raid items already known and crafted gear, which can be bought or made for you: prices and materials are not taken into account), with item effects simulated. Nothing is hand-picked: it is the result of the ranking's computation, and it will change as new items are discovered.

Enchantments up to skill 300, valued by the simulator when they give flat stats or flat weapon damage. The character does not need the profession: they can be bought or applied by an enchanter; Leatherworking armor kits (chest, legs, feet, hands) and Engineering scopes are counted too. A kit and an enchantment share the same permanent slot: a piece carries one or the other, and the legs, which have no enchantment, get the kit; spell-proc enchants (Crusader, Fiery Weapon...) and school damage are not simulated. The beta data has no ring enchantment up to skill 300.
