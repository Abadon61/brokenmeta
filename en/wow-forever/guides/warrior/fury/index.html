Level 60 rotation

Our simulator's priority at level 60, set against what Icy Veins recommends. Their guides are written for the level-30 beta and for leveling: we keep what everyone agrees on, adopt what the simulation confirmed at level 60 and say where the two differ.

  1. Bloodthirst whenever it is ready.
  2. Whirlwind (and Raging Blows' Whirlwind) on cooldown.
  3. Execute under 20% target health.
  4. Heroic Strike on the next swing with spare rage.
  5. Berserker Rage, Bloodrage, Death Wish and Recklessness on cooldown.

What Icy Veins recommends (level 30)

  • Keep Battle Shout up, Bloodrage between pulls, Charge to open, Demoralizing Shout on the target.
  • Victory Rush after a kill, Rend early, Overpower after a dodge, Sunder Armor once or twice.
  • Execute is not recommended while leveling.

Our comparison

Against a level-60 boss, Bloodthirst, Whirlwind, Heroic Strike and Execute (under 20% health) do the damage. Victory Rush (after a kill) and Demoralizing Shout do not apply to a boss. Rend only pays off for Arms with Bloodthrill (measured).
