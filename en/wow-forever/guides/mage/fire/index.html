Talent details of the build (16)
Wake of Fire Fire · Tier 1 · 2 ranks · 2 pts · Recommended

Rank 2/2: Reduces the cooldown of your Fire Blast spell by -2 sec. Killing a non-trivial target increases the critical strike chance of your next Fire Blast cast within 30 sec by 50%.

Incineration Fire · Tier 1 · 3 ranks · 3 pts · Recommended

Rank 3/3: Increases the critical strike chance of your Fire Blast, Ice Lance, Arcane Blast, and Scorch spells by 6%.

Improved Fireball Fire · Tier 1 · 5 ranks · 5 pts · Recommended

Rank 5/5: Reduces the casting time of your Fireball and Frostfire Bolt spells by 0.5 sec.

Ignite Fire · Tier 2 · 5 ranks · 5 pts · Recommended

Rank 5/5: Your critical strikes from Fire damage spells cause the target to burn for an additional 40% of your spell's damage over 4 sec.

Pyroblast Fire · Tier 3 · 1 rank · 1 pts · Recommended

Rank 1/1: Hurls an immense fiery boulder that causes 110 Fire damage and an additional 44 Fire damage over 12 sec.

Improved Scorch Fire · Tier 4 · 3 ranks · 3 pts · Recommended

Rank 3/3: Your Scorch spell has a 100% chance to cause your target to be vulnerable to Fire damage. This vulnerability increases all Fire damage you deal to your target by 3% and lasts 30 sec, stacking up to 5 times.

Heating Up Fire · Tier 4 · 1 rank · 1 pts · Recommended

Rank 1/1: Non-periodic critical strikes with Fireball, Frostfire Bolt, Fire Blast, and Scorch reduce the cast time of your next Pyroblast cast within 20 sec by 25%, stacking up to 3 times.

Master of Elements Fire · Tier 4 · 3 ranks · 3 pts · Recommended

Rank 3/3: Your Fire and Frost critical strikes will refund 30% of their base mana cost.

Critical Mass Fire · Tier 5 · 3 ranks · 3 pts · Recommended

Rank 3/3: Increases the critical strike chance of your Fire spells by 6%.

Fire Power Fire · Tier 6 · 5 ranks · 5 pts · Recommended

Rank 5/5: Increases the damage done by your Fire spells by 10%.

Combustion Fire · Tier 7 · 1 rank · 1 pts · Recommended

Rank 1/1: When activated, this spell causes each of your Fire damage spell hits to increase your critical strike chance with Fire damage spells by 10%. This effect lasts until you have caused 3 non-periodic critical strikes with Fire spells.

Improved Frostbolt Frost · Tier 1 · 5 ranks · 3 pts · Recommended

Rank 3/5: Reduces the casting time of your Frostbolt spell by 0.3 sec.

Elemental Precision Frost · Tier 1 · 5 ranks · 5 pts · Recommended

Rank 5/5: Improves your chance to hit with Frost and Fire spells by 5%.

Ice Shards Frost · Tier 2 · 5 ranks · 5 pts · Recommended

Rank 5/5: Increases the critical strike damage bonus of your Frost spells by 100%.

Piercing Ice Frost · Tier 3 · 3 ranks · 3 pts · Recommended

Rank 3/3: Increases the damage done by your Frost spells by 6%.

Frost Channeling Frost · Tier 3 · 3 ranks · 3 pts · Recommended

Rank 3/3: Reduces the mana cost of your Frost spells by 15% and reduces the threat caused by your Frost spells by 30%.

How to read these measures

Measured by the simulator on the character above: the DPS gained by one more point of each stat, with the same random draws on both sides. The relative value compares each stat with one point of spell power. Crit, hit and haste are counted per rating point, like Strength or Agility, so they compare directly; they respect the caps: hit at the cap adds nothing. A value near zero or negative is within the margin of error or tied to a limit (mana, cap).

DPS gained when points of each stat are added on top of the simulated gear. Click a stat (or its row in the table) to bring its curve forward; the ring marks the estimated cap, past which every point is worth less than 20% of what it gave at the start.

Level 60 rotation

  1. Frostfire Bolt as the filler.
  2. Pyroblast when Heating Up has shortened its cast.
  3. Fire Blast on cooldown.
  4. Scorch to keep the Improved Scorch effect up.
  5. Combustion on cooldown; Evocation and the mana gem for mana.
Method, Icy Veins recommendations and comparison

Our simulator's priority at level 60, set against what Icy Veins recommends. Their guides are written for the level-30 beta and for leveling: we keep what everyone agrees on, adopt what the simulation confirmed at level 60 and say where the two differ.

What Icy Veins recommends (level 30)

  • Open with Pyroblast, Fireball as the filler (Frostfire Bolt replaces it from level 40), Fire Blast to finish.
  • Area: Flamestrike, Blast Wave, Arcane Explosion.

Our comparison

Agreed: Pyroblast (with Heating Up), Fire Blast, Frostfire Bolt as the filler (it does replace Fireball), plus Scorch for Improved Scorch and Combustion. Area spells do not count against one target.

Method and enchant notes

The gear our optimizer picks for this specialization among the items known from the beta (dungeons, the few raid items already known and crafted gear, which can be bought or made for you: prices and materials are not taken into account), with item effects simulated. Nothing is hand-picked: it is the result of the ranking's computation, and it will change as new items are discovered.

Enchantments up to skill 300, valued by the simulator when they give flat stats or flat weapon damage. The character does not need the profession: they can be bought or applied by an enchanter; Leatherworking armor kits (chest, legs, feet, hands) and Engineering scopes are counted too. A kit and an enchantment share the same permanent slot: a piece carries one or the other, and the legs, which have no enchantment, get the kit; spell-proc enchants (Crusader, Fiery Weapon...) and school damage are not simulated. The beta data has no ring enchantment up to skill 300.
