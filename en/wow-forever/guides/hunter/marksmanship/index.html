Lethal Attacks Marksmanship · Tier 1 · 5 ranks · 5 pts · Recommended

Rank 5/5: Increases your critical strike chance with all attacks by 5%.

Improved Stings Marksmanship · Tier 2 · 3 ranks · 3 pts · Recommended

Rank 3/3: Increases the damage of your Serpent Sting ability by 20%, reduces the cooldown of your Viper Sting ability by -6 sec, and increases the duration of your Scorpid Sting ability by 45 sec.

Efficiency Marksmanship · Tier 2 · 5 ranks · 5 pts · Recommended

Rank 5/5: Reduces the Mana cost of your Shots, Stings, and melee abilities by 15%.

Careful Aim Marksmanship · Tier 2 · 5 ranks · 5 pts · Recommended

Rank 5/5: Increases your Attack Power by 100% of your Intellect.

Rapid Killing Marksmanship · Tier 3 · 2 ranks · 2 pts · Recommended

Rank 2/2: Reduces the cooldown on your Rapid Fire ability by -2 min. In addition, when you kill a non-trivial enemy or it dies while afflicted by your Serpent Sting, you gain Rapid Killing, increasing the damage of your next Shot ability within 20 sec by 20%.

Improved Arcane Shot Marksmanship · Tier 3 · 5 ranks · 5 pts · Recommended

Rank 5/5: Reduces the cooldown of your Arcane Shot by 1.5 sec. Does not affect the cooldown of abilities which share a cooldown with Arcane Shot.

Lone Wolf Marksmanship · Tier 3 · 1 rank · 1 pts · Recommended

Rank 1/1: You deal 20% increased damage with all attacks while you do not have an active pet.

Trueshot Aura Marksmanship · Tier 4 · 1 rank · 1 pts · Recommended

Rank 1/1: Increases the Ranged Attack Power of party members within 45 yds by 30.

Mortal Shots Marksmanship · Tier 4 · 5 ranks · 5 pts · Recommended

Rank 5/5: Increases the critical strike damage bonus on all ranged abilities by 30%.

Barrage Marksmanship · Tier 5 · 3 ranks · 3 pts · Recommended

Rank 3/3: Increases the damage done by your Multi-Shot, Aimed Shot, and Volley abilities by 10%.

Ranged Weapon Specialization Marksmanship · Tier 6 · 5 ranks · 5 pts · Recommended

Rank 5/5: Increases the damage you deal with ranged weapons by 5%.

Deadly Aspects Beast Mastery · Tier 1 · 5 ranks · 5 pts · Recommended

Rank 5/5: While Aspect of the Hawk is active, Auto Shot has a 10% chance of increasing ranged attack speed by 30% for 12 sec. While Aspect of the Beast is active, all melee auto attacks have a 10% chance of increasing melee attack speed by 30% for 12 sec.

Endurance Training Beast Mastery · Tier 1 · 5 ranks · 5 pts · Recommended

Rank 5/5: Increases the Health and Armor of your pets by 15%.

Focused Fire Beast Mastery · Tier 2 · 2 ranks · 1 pts · Recommended

Rank 1/2: Increases all damage you and your pet deal by 1% while your pet is active.

Level 60 rotation

Our simulator's priority at level 60, set against what Icy Veins recommends. Their guides are written for the level-30 beta and for leveling: we keep what everyone agrees on, adopt what the simulation confirmed at level 60 and say where the two differ.

  1. Hunter's Mark on the target before the fight (+110 ranged attack power).
  2. Auto Shot continuously.
  3. Arcane Shot on cooldown.
  4. Keep Serpent Sting up.
  5. Multi-Shot on cooldown.
  6. Rapid Fire on cooldown.
  7. Sniper Shot is not taken: its 4-second cast stops Auto Shot and costs about 4% of the DPS.

What Icy Veins recommends (level 30)

  • Hunter's Mark and Serpent Sting first, pet sent in first.
  • Aimed Shot on cooldown, Auto Shot in between; Arcane Shot if mana is capped.

Our comparison

Agreed, and added: Hunter's Mark (+110 ranged attack power, client value), Serpent Sting, Arcane Shot. Different: at level 60 on one target Multi-Shot does about 4% better than Aimed Shot (2-second cast) in the simulator, and Sniper Shot (a 4-second cast that stops Auto Shot) costs about 4%: the build does not take it.
