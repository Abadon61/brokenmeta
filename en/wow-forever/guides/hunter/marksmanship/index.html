Lethal Attacks Marksmanship · Tier 1 · 5 ranks · Icy Veins : 5 pts · Recommended

Icy Veins (level 30): Increases your critical strike chance with all attacks by 1%.

Efficiency Marksmanship · Tier 2 · 5 ranks · Icy Veins : 2 pts · Recommended

Icy Veins (level 30): Reduces the Mana cost of your Shots, Stings, and melee abilities by 3%.

Careful Aim Marksmanship · Tier 2 · 5 ranks · Icy Veins : 5 pts · Recommended

Icy Veins (level 30): Increases your Attack Power by 20% of your Intellect.

Rapid Killing Marksmanship · Tier 3 · 2 ranks · Icy Veins : 2 pts · Recommended

Icy Veins (level 30): Reduces the cooldown on your Rapid Fire ability by -1 min. In addition, when you kill a non-trivial enemy or it dies while afflicted by your Serpent Sting, you gain Rapid Killing, increasing the damage of your next Shot ability within 20 sec by 10%.

Lone Wolf Marksmanship · Tier 3 · 1 rank · Icy Veins : 1 pts · Recommended

Icy Veins (level 30): You deal 20% increased damage with all attacks while you do not have an active pet.

Trueshot Aura Marksmanship · Tier 4 · 1 rank · Icy Veins : 1 pts · Recommended

Icy Veins (level 30): Increases the Ranged Attack Power of party members within 45 yds by 30.

Mortal Shots Marksmanship · Tier 4 · 5 ranks · Icy Veins : 5 pts · Recommended

Icy Veins (level 30): Increases the critical strike damage bonus on all ranged abilities by 6%.

The last-tier talents

These are the deepest talents in the tree: they require 30 points already spent in the specialization. Official game text, at maximum rank.

Sniper Shot Tier 7 · 1 rank

A long-range shot that deals ranged damage plus 160 and increases the range of your next 3 Shots by 10 yards for 10 sec.

Single-rank talents

Talents you take only once, with their tier (official text at maximum rank).

Lone Wolf Tier 3 · 1 rank

You deal 20% increased damage with all attacks while you do not have an active pet.

Trueshot Aura Tier 4 · 1 rank

Increases the Ranged Attack Power of party members within 45 yds by 30.

Scatter Shot Tier 5 · 1 rank

A short-range shot that deals 50% weapon damage and disorients the target for 4 sec. Any damage caused will remove the effect. Turns off your attack when used.
