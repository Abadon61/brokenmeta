Level 60 rotation

Our simulator's priority at level 60, set against what Icy Veins recommends. Their guides are written for the level-30 beta and for leveling: we keep what everyone agrees on, adopt what the simulation confirmed at level 60 and say where the two differ.

Survival (ranged)

  1. Hunter's Mark on the target before the fight (+110 ranged attack power).
  2. Auto Shot continuously.
  3. Arcane Shot and Multi-Shot on cooldown.
  4. Keep Serpent Sting up.
  5. Rapid Fire on cooldown.
  6. The pet attacks continuously.

Survival (melee)

  1. Melee swings with two weapons.
  2. Raptor Strike on cooldown.
  3. Mongoose Bite right after a target dodge.
  4. Strider Kick on cooldown.
  5. The pet attacks continuously.

What Icy Veins recommends (level 30)

  • Ranged: Hunter's Mark, Serpent Sting, Aimed Shot on cooldown, Auto Shot, Arcane Shot with spare mana.
  • Melee: Serpent Sting, Raptor Strike on cooldown, Mongoose Bite whenever available, Strider Kick on cooldown. At level 30, melee starts to pass ranged on one target for a full Survival build.

Our comparison

Agreed on both variants (Hunter's Mark added to the ranged one). Different: Icy Veins sees melee passing ranged at level 30; with the known level-60 gear the simulator keeps ranged ahead (about 493 DPS against 422), and Multi-Shot does 6% better than Aimed Shot there.

The last-tier talents

These are the deepest talents in the tree: they require 30 points already spent in the specialization. Official game text, at maximum rank.

Lacerating Strikes Tier 7 · 1 rank

Your Mongoose Bite also causes the target to Bleed for damage equal to 40% of the damage done by Mongoose Bite over 21 sec

Single-rank talents

Talents you take only once, with their tier (official text at maximum rank).

Deterrence Tier 3 · 1 rank

When activated, increases your Dodge and Parry chance by 25% for 10 sec.

Counterattack Tier 4 · 1 rank

A strike that becomes active after parrying an opponent's attack. This attack deals 50% weapon damage plus 26 and immobilizes the target for 5 sec. Counterattack cannot be blocked, dodged, or parried.

Strider Kick Tier 5 · 1 rank

A powerful kick that deals 100% melee weapon damage and increases movement speed by 30% for 3 sec.
