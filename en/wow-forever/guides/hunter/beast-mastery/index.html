Talent details of the build (15)
Deadly Aspects Beast Mastery · Tier 1 · 5 ranks · 5 pts · Recommended

Rank 5/5: While Aspect of the Hawk is active, Auto Shot has a 10% chance of increasing ranged attack speed by 30% for 12 sec. While Aspect of the Beast is active, all melee auto attacks have a 10% chance of increasing melee attack speed by 30% for 12 sec.

Endurance Training Beast Mastery · Tier 1 · 5 ranks · 5 pts · Recommended

Rank 5/5: Increases the Health and Armor of your pets by 15%.

Focused Fire Beast Mastery · Tier 2 · 2 ranks · 2 pts · Recommended

Rank 2/2: Increases all damage you and your pet deal by 2% while your pet is active.

Bestial Swiftness Beast Mastery · Tier 3 · 1 rank · 1 pts · Recommended

Rank 1/1: Increases the movement speed of your pets by 30%.

Unleashed Fury Beast Mastery · Tier 3 · 5 ranks · 5 pts · Recommended

Rank 5/5: Increases the damage done by your pets and hawks by 15%.

Ferocity Beast Mastery · Tier 4 · 5 ranks · 5 pts · Recommended

Rank 5/5: Increases the critical strike chance of your pets and hawks by 10%.

Summon Hawk Beast Mastery · Tier 4 · 1 rank · 1 pts · Recommended

Rank 1/1: Command a hawk to dive-bomb your targeted enemy, dealing 32 (+ 5% of your ranged attack power) Physical damage and continuing its assault for 18 sec. Only 2 hawks can be active at once. Summon Hawk shares its cooldown with Arcane Shot.

Intimidation Beast Mastery · Tier 5 · 1 rank · 1 pts · Recommended

Rank 1/1: Command your pet to Stun the target for 3 sec on its next successful attack, which also gains 100% increased critical strike chance. Generates high threat.

Bestial Discipline Beast Mastery · Tier 5 · 2 ranks · 2 pts · Recommended

Rank 2/2: Increases the Focus regeneration of your pets by 20% and allows 50% of your Mana regeneration to continue while casting.

Frenzy Beast Mastery · Tier 6 · 5 ranks · 5 pts · Recommended

Rank 5/5: Gives your pet a 100% chance to gain a 30% attack speed increase for 8 sec after dealing a critical strike.

Bestial Wrath Beast Mastery · Tier 7 · 1 rank · 1 pts · Recommended

Rank 1/1: Send your pet into a rage causing 50% additional damage for 18 sec. While enraged, the beast does not feel pity or remorse or fear and it cannot be stopped unless killed.

Lethal Attacks Marksmanship · Tier 1 · 5 ranks · 5 pts · Recommended

Rank 5/5: Increases your critical strike chance with all attacks by 5%.

Improved Stings Marksmanship · Tier 2 · 3 ranks · 3 pts · Recommended

Rank 3/3: Increases the damage of your Serpent Sting ability by 20%, reduces the cooldown of your Viper Sting ability by -6 sec, and increases the duration of your Scorpid Sting ability by 45 sec.

Efficiency Marksmanship · Tier 2 · 5 ranks · 5 pts · Recommended

Rank 5/5: Reduces the Mana cost of your Shots, Stings, and melee abilities by 15%.

Careful Aim Marksmanship · Tier 2 · 5 ranks · 5 pts · Recommended

Rank 5/5: Increases your Attack Power by 100% of your Intellect.

How to read these measures

Measured by the simulator on the character above: the DPS gained by one more point of each stat, with the same random draws on both sides. The relative value compares each stat with one point of attack power. Crit, hit and haste are counted per rating point, like Strength or Agility, so they compare directly; they respect the caps: hit at the cap adds nothing. A value near zero or negative is within the margin of error or tied to a limit (mana, cap).

DPS gained when points of each stat are added on top of the simulated gear. Click a stat (or its row in the table) to bring its curve forward; the ring marks the estimated cap, past which every point is worth less than 20% of what it gave at the start.

Level 60 rotation

  1. Hunter's Mark on the target before the fight (+110 ranged attack power).
  2. Auto Shot continuously.
  3. Arcane Shot and Multi-Shot on cooldown.
  4. Keep Serpent Sting up.
  5. Bestial Wrath and Rapid Fire on cooldown.
  6. The pet attacks continuously (bite and claw).
Method, Icy Veins recommendations and comparison

Our simulator's priority at level 60, set against what Icy Veins recommends. Their guides are written for the level-30 beta and for leveling: we keep what everyone agrees on, adopt what the simulation confirmed at level 60 and say where the two differ.

What Icy Veins recommends (level 30)

  • Hunter's Mark and Serpent Sting first, pet sent in first; Summon Hawk on cooldown to keep 2 hawks out.
  • Aimed Shot on cooldown, Auto Shot in between; Arcane Shot if mana is capped; Multi-Shot instead of Aimed Shot against several enemies.

Our comparison

Agreed, and added: Hunter's Mark (+110 ranged attack power, client value), Serpent Sting, Summon Hawk, the pet's attacks, Arcane Shot. Different: at level 60 on one target Multi-Shot does about 4% better than Aimed Shot (which costs a 2-second cast), so the simulator fires Multi-Shot.

Method and enchant notes

The gear our optimizer picks for this specialization among the items known from the beta (dungeons, the few raid items already known and crafted gear, which can be bought or made for you: prices and materials are not taken into account), with item effects simulated. Nothing is hand-picked: it is the result of the ranking's computation, and it will change as new items are discovered.

Enchantments up to skill 300, valued by the simulator when they give flat stats or flat weapon damage. The character does not need the profession: they can be bought or applied by an enchanter; Leatherworking armor kits (chest, legs, feet, hands) and Engineering scopes are counted too. A kit and an enchantment share the same permanent slot: a piece carries one or the other, and the legs, which have no enchantment, get the kit; spell-proc enchants (Crusader, Fiery Weapon...) and school damage are not simulated. The beta data has no ring enchantment up to skill 300.
