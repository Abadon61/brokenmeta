Combat Rogue: specialization guide

Melee DPS #16 in the ranking · 470 DPS

Combat is the Melee DPS specialization of the Rogue in WoW: Forever. This page summarizes its talent tree as it appears in the beta client (build 1.60.1.70425); the role is the one stated by the Icy Veins guide for the specialization.

Quick answers: Combat Rogue

Is Combat Rogue a good spec in WoW Forever?

In our level-60 simulation (180 seconds on a stationary boss, raid buffs, best known gear), Combat Rogue ranks #16 of 20 DPS specs with 470 DPS. #1 is Affliction Warlock (754 DPS). A stationary boss does not tell everything: group utility and moving fights matter too.

Which talents should Combat Rogue take?

Our simulator's build uses 51 points, split Assassination 18 / Combat 33. The talents that weigh the most in the simulation: Puncturing Wounds, Improved Slice and Dice, Hack and Slash. Open this build in the talent calculator →

What gear should Combat Rogue wear?

Without raid items, the best set the simulator finds gives 465 DPS, −1.2% against the set that includes raid items. Stat priority: Weapon Damage > Hit Chance > Agility. Full gear and enchantments →

What rotation does Combat Rogue play?

Opening: Keep Slice and Dice up ; Sinister Strike to build combo points ; Eviscerate at 5 combo points. Full rotation →

Which race should Combat Rogue pick?

The top races in our simulation: Human and High Order Skyborne / Windshaper Skyborne (statistically tied). All races of the class →

Murder Assassination · Tier 2 · 2 ranks · 2 pts · Recommended

How to read these measures

Measured by the simulator on the character above: the DPS gained by one more point of each stat, with the same random draws on both sides. The relative value compares each stat with one point of attack power. Crit, hit and haste are counted per rating point, like Strength or Agility, so they compare directly; they respect the caps: hit at the cap adds nothing. A value near zero or negative is within the margin of error or tied to a limit (mana, cap).

DPS gained when points of each stat are added on top of the simulated gear. Click a stat (or its row in the table) to bring its curve forward; the ring marks the estimated cap, past which every point is worth less than 20% of what it gave at the start.

Level 60 rotation

  1. Keep Slice and Dice up.
  2. Sinister Strike to build combo points.
  3. Eviscerate at 5 combo points.
  4. Adrenaline Rush and Blade Flurry on cooldown.
  5. Instant Poison on the main hand, Deadly Poison on the off hand.
Method, Icy Veins recommendations and comparison

Our simulator's priority at level 60, set against what Icy Veins recommends. Their guides are written for the level-30 beta and for leveling: we keep what everyone agrees on, adopt what the simulation confirmed at level 60 and say where the two differ.

What Icy Veins recommends (level 30)

  • Open with Ambush from stealth, Backstab if you are behind the target, otherwise Sinister Strike.
  • Eviscerate or Slice and Dice at 5 combo points; Instant Poison on both weapons.

Our comparison

Agreed: Sinister Strike, Slice and Dice, Eviscerate at 5 points. The simulator adds Adrenaline Rush and Blade Flurry on cooldown. Poisons: Instant on the main hand and Deadly on the off hand, Classic values (assumed).

Method and enchant notes

The gear our optimizer picks for this specialization among the items known from the beta (dungeons, the few raid items already known and crafted gear, which can be bought or made for you: prices and materials are not taken into account), with item effects simulated. Nothing is hand-picked: it is the result of the ranking's computation, and it will change as new items are discovered.

Enchantments up to skill 300, valued by the simulator when they give flat stats or flat weapon damage. The character does not need the profession: they can be bought or applied by an enchanter; Leatherworking armor kits (chest, legs, feet, hands) and Engineering scopes are counted too. A kit and an enchantment share the same permanent slot: a piece carries one or the other, and the legs, which have no enchantment, get the kit; spell-proc enchants (Crusader, Fiery Weapon...) and school damage are not simulated. The beta data has no ring enchantment up to skill 300.
