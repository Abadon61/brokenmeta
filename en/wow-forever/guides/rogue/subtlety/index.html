Talent details of the build (18)
Camouflage Subtlety · Tier 1 · 5 ranks · 5 pts · Recommended

Rank 5/5: Reduces your speed penalty from your Stealth ability by 15% and reduces its cooldown by 6 sec.

Opportunity Subtlety · Tier 1 · 2 ranks · 2 pts · Recommended

Rank 2/2: Increases the damage dealt by your Backstab, Garrote, Ambush, and Mutilate abilities by 10%.

Setup Subtlety · Tier 2 · 3 ranks · 3 pts · Recommended

Rank 3/3: Gives you a 100% chance to add a Combo Point to your target after Dodging one of their attacks or fully resisting one of their spells.

Improved Ambush Subtlety · Tier 2 · 3 ranks · 3 pts · Recommended

Rank 3/3: Increases the critical strike chance of your Ambush ability by 45%.

Initiative Subtlety · Tier 3 · 3 ranks · 3 pts · Recommended

Rank 3/3: Gives you a 100% chance to add an additional combo point to your target when using your Ambush, Garrote, or Cheap Shot ability.

Ghostly Strike Subtlety · Tier 3 · 1 rank · 1 pts · Recommended

Rank 1/1: A strike that deals 125% (180% if a Dagger is equipped in your Main Hand) weapon damage and increases your chance to dodge by 15% for 7 sec. Awards 1 combo point.

Improved Distract Subtlety · Tier 3 · 2 ranks · 2 pts · Recommended

Rank 2/2: Increases the radius of your Distract ability by 5 yds, and further reduces the Stealth detection of distracted enemies as though they were an additional -2 levels lower.

Premeditation Subtlety · Tier 4 · 1 rank · 1 pts · Recommended

Rank 1/1: Adds 2 Combo Points to your target. You must add to or use those combo points within 20 sec or the combo points are lost.

Serrated Blades Subtlety · Tier 4 · 3 ranks · 3 pts · Recommended

Rank 3/3: Causes your attacks to ignore 9% of your target's Armor and increases the damage dealt by your Rupture ability by 30%.

Dirty Deeds Subtlety · Tier 5 · 2 ranks · 2 pts · Recommended

Rank 2/2: Reduces the Energy cost of your Cheap Shot and Garrote abilities by 20, and your Garrote ability no longer requires you to be behind your target.

Hemorrhage Subtlety · Tier 5 · 1 rank · 1 pts · Recommended

Rank 1/1: An instant strike that deals 100% weapon damage (145% if a Dagger is equipped) and causes the target to take 15% increased Rupture damage from the Rogue. Lasts 15 sec. Awards 1 Combo Point.

Quietus Subtlety · Tier 6 · 5 ranks · 5 pts · Recommended

Rank 5/5: Your Sinister Strike, Ghostly Strike, and Hemorrhage abilities cause 10% more damage against targets below 35% health.

Improved Eviscerate Combat · Tier 1 · 3 ranks · 3 pts · Recommended

Rank 3/3: Increases the damage done by your Eviscerate ability by 20%.

Improved Sinister Strike Combat · Tier 1 · 2 ranks · 2 pts · Recommended

Rank 2/2: Reduces the Energy cost of your Sinister Strike ability by 5.

Lightning Reflexes Combat · Tier 1 · 5 ranks · 4 pts · Recommended

Rank 4/5: Increases your Dodge chance by 4%.

Puncturing Wounds Combat · Tier 2 · 3 ranks · 3 pts · Recommended

Rank 3/3: Increases the critical strike chance of your Backstab by 30% and your Mutilate by 15%, and gives Backstab a 45% chance to add an additional Combo Point.

Precision Combat · Tier 2 · 3 ranks · 3 pts · Recommended

Rank 3/3: Improves your chance to hit by 3%.

Dual Wield Specialization Combat · Tier 4 · 5 ranks · 5 pts · Recommended

Rank 5/5: Increases the damage done by your off-hand weapon by 25%.

How to read these measures

Measured by the simulator on the character above: the DPS gained by one more point of each stat, with the same random draws on both sides. The relative value compares each stat with one point of attack power. Crit, hit and haste are counted per rating point, like Strength or Agility, so they compare directly; they respect the caps: hit at the cap adds nothing. A value near zero or negative is within the margin of error or tied to a limit (mana, cap).

DPS gained when points of each stat are added on top of the simulated gear. Click a stat (or its row in the table) to bring its curve forward; the ring marks the estimated cap, past which every point is worth less than 20% of what it gave at the start.

Level 60 rotation

  1. Keep Slice and Dice up.
  2. Hemorrhage (or Backstab with daggers) to build combo points.
  3. Eviscerate at 5 combo points.
  4. Instant Poison on the main hand, Deadly Poison on the off hand.
Method, Icy Veins recommendations and comparison

Our simulator's priority at level 60, set against what Icy Veins recommends. Their guides are written for the level-30 beta and for leveling: we keep what everyone agrees on, adopt what the simulation confirmed at level 60 and say where the two differ.

What Icy Veins recommends (level 30)

  • Hemorrhage as the combo builder at all times (no need to be behind the target); Ambush from stealth.
  • Eviscerate or Slice and Dice at 5 points; Rupture on targets that live the full duration, better than Slice and Dice at level 30.

Our comparison

Agreed: Hemorrhage at all times, Slice and Dice, Eviscerate. Rupture is not simulated yet (its client numbers are small: 35 base, +4.7 per combo point, every 2 seconds): to confirm in game.

Method and enchant notes

The gear our optimizer picks for this specialization among the items known from the beta (dungeons, the few raid items already known and crafted gear, which can be bought or made for you: prices and materials are not taken into account), with item effects simulated. Nothing is hand-picked: it is the result of the ranking's computation, and it will change as new items are discovered.

Enchantments up to skill 300, valued by the simulator when they give flat stats or flat weapon damage. The character does not need the profession: they can be bought or applied by an enchanter; Leatherworking armor kits (chest, legs, feet, hands) and Engineering scopes are counted too. A kit and an enchantment share the same permanent slot: a piece carries one or the other, and the legs, which have no enchantment, get the kit; spell-proc enchants (Crusader, Fiery Weapon...) and school damage are not simulated. The beta data has no ring enchantment up to skill 300.
