Improved Healing Wave Restoration · Tier 1 · 5 ranks · Icy Veins : 5 pts · Recommended

Icy Veins (level 30): Reduces the casting time of your Healing Wave spell by -0.1 sec.

Mindfulness Restoration · Tier 2 · 3 ranks · Icy Veins : 3 pts · Recommended

Icy Veins (level 30): Allows 17% of your Mana regeneration to continue while casting.

Natural Grace Restoration · Tier 2 · 3 ranks · Icy Veins : 2 pts · Recommended

Icy Veins (level 30): Reduces the threat generated by your spells by 5%.

Ancestral Healing Restoration · Tier 3 · 3 ranks · Icy Veins : 3 pts · Recommended

Icy Veins (level 30): Increases your target's armor value by 8% for 15 sec after getting a critical effect from one of your healing spells.

Healing Focus Restoration · Tier 3 · 3 ranks · Icy Veins : 1 pts · Recommended

Icy Veins (level 30): Gives you a 23% chance to avoid interruption caused by damage while casting any healing spell.

Water Shield Restoration · Tier 3 · 1 rank · Icy Veins : 1 pts · Recommended

Icy Veins (level 30): The caster is surrounded by 3 globes of water. When a spell, melee, or ranged attack hits the caster or when one of the caster's healing spells gets a critical result, 2% of maximum mana is restored to the caster, expending one water globe. Only one globe will activate every few seconds. Lasts 10 min. Only one Elemental Shield can be active on the Shaman at any one time.

Tidal Mastery Restoration · Tier 4 · 5 ranks · Icy Veins : 4 pts · Recommended

Icy Veins (level 30): Increases the critical effect chance of your healing spells by 1%.

Mana Tide Totem Restoration · Tier 4 · 1 rank · Icy Veins : 1 pts · Recommended

Icy Veins (level 30): Summons a Mana Tide Totem with 5 health at the feet of the caster for 12 sec that restores 88 mana every 3 seconds to group members within 30 yards.

Nature's Swiftness Restoration · Tier 5 · 1 rank · Icy Veins : 1 pts · Recommended

Icy Veins (level 30): When activated, your next Nature spell with a casting time less than 10 sec. becomes an instant cast spell.

Stat priority

In order of importance, according to Icy Veins.

Icy Veins: aim for healing and/or Mana sustain so you heal more, and for longer.

Consumables

What the Icy Veins guide lists for this specialization.

  • Water (to recover mana)
  • Lesser Healing Potion — Icy Veins mentions it alongside First Aid.
  • Minor Mageblood Elixir — Mana sustain.
  • Minor Mender's Potion — Healing boost.
  • Cooking food (+5% experience)
  • Cozy Sleeping Bag — Experience and rested bonus.

Suggested professions

  • Skinning + Leatherworking (gear)
  • Herbalism + Alchemy (potions and elixirs)

The last-tier talents

These are the deepest talents in the tree: they require 30 points already spent in the specialization. Official game text, at maximum rank.

Riptide Tier 7 · 1 rank

Heals a friendly target for 480, an additional 445 over 15 sec, and increases the effectiveness of your Chain Heal casts directly on that target by 25%.
