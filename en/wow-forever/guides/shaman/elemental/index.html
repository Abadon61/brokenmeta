Elemental Shaman: specialization guide

Elemental is the Ranged DPS specialization of the Shaman in WoW: Forever. This page summarizes its talent tree as it appears in the beta client (build 1.60.1.69913); the role is the one stated by the Icy Veins guide for the specialization.

The last-tier talents

These are the deepest talents in the tree: they require 30 points already spent in the specialization. Official game text, at maximum rank.

Lava Burst Tier 7 · 1 rank

You hurl molten lava at the target, dealing 113 Fire damage. If your Flame Shock is on the target, Lava Burst deals 20% increased damage.

Single-rank talents

Talents you take only once, with their tier (official text at maximum rank).

Elemental Focus Tier 3 · 1 rank

Gives you a 10% chance to enter a Clearcasting state after casting any Fire, Frost, or Nature damage spell. The Clearcasting state reduces the mana cost of your next damage spell by 100%.

Call of Thunder Tier 4 · 1 rank

Increases the critical strike chance of your Lightning Bolt and Chain Lightning spells by 3%.

Earthbound Tier 5 · 1 rank

Your Earthbind Totem Immobilizes nearby targets for 5 sec when cast.
