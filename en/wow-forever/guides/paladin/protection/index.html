Redoubt Protection · Tier 1 · 5 ranks · Icy Veins : 5 pts · Recommended

Icy Veins (level 30): Damaging melee attacks against you have a 10% chance to increase your chance to block by 4%. Lasts 10 sec or 5 blocks.

Precision Protection · Tier 2 · 3 ranks · Icy Veins : 3 pts · Recommended

Icy Veins (level 30): Improves your chance to hit by 1%.

Anticipation Protection · Tier 2 · 5 ranks · Icy Veins : 2 pts · Recommended

Icy Veins (level 30): Increases your Defense Skill by 4.

Improved Seal of Fury Protection · Tier 3 · 1 rank · Icy Veins : 1 pts · Recommended

Icy Veins (level 30): When Seal of Fury's shield is fully absorbed, restore 0 Mana, increased by 15% per level the attacker is above you, up to 45%.

Shield Specialization Protection · Tier 3 · 3 ranks · Icy Veins : 3 pts · Recommended

Icy Veins (level 30): Increases the amount of damage absorbed by your shield by 10%, and gives your blocks a 33% chance to restore 6% of your maximum Mana. May only occur once every 3 sec.

Deflection Retribution · Tier 1 · 5 ranks · Icy Veins : 5 pts · Recommended

Icy Veins (level 30): Increases your Parry chance by 1%.

Holy Conduit Retribution · Tier 2 · 2 ranks · Icy Veins : 2 pts · Recommended

Icy Veins (level 30): Reduces the mana cost of your Consecration, Holy Wrath, Exorcism, and Hammer of Wrath spells by 20%.

Consumables

What the Icy Veins guide lists for this specialization.

  • Water (recover mana out of combat)
  • Food (health between fights)
  • Healing potions (emergencies)
  • Mana potions (emergencies)
  • Bandages (optional thanks to the Paladin's healing)

Suggested professions

  • Engineering and Mining; or Blacksmithing instead of Mining; or double gathering / Alchemy for gold

The last-tier talents

These are the deepest talents in the tree: they require 30 points already spent in the specialization. Official game text, at maximum rank.

Holy Shield Tier 7 · 1 rank

Increases chance to block by 30% for 10 sec, and deals 110 Holy damage for each attack blocked while active. Damage caused by Holy Shield causes 20% additional threat. Each block expends a charge. 4 charges.

Single-rank talents

Talents you take only once, with their tier (official text at maximum rank).

Improved Seal of Fury Tier 3 · 1 rank

When Seal of Fury's shield is fully absorbed, restore 0 Mana, increased by 15% per level the attacker is above you, up to 45%.

Swift Judgement Tier 4 · 1 rank

Finishes the remaining cooldown on your Judgement ability and reduces the Mana cost of your next Judgement by 100%.

Templar's Bulwark Tier 5 · 1 rank

When activated, this ability grants you an absorb shield equal to 100% of your maximum health for 8 sec. Applies Forbearance for 1 min. Cannot be cast while Forbearance is active.
