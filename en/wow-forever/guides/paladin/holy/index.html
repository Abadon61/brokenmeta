Stat priority

In order of importance, according to Icy Veins (level 30).

Icy Veins: if you do not plan to really heal, look for Weapon Damage, Hit and Critical Strike instead.

Level 60 rotation

  1. Flash of Light as the main heal: 3.27 healing per mana for only 140 mana, on a 1.5 s cast.
  2. Holy Light for big heals when mana allows (2.77 per mana, 732 healing per second on a 2.5 s cast).
  3. Instant Holy Shock and Light's Vigil for emergencies; Divine Favor before an important heal to guarantee a critical (Illumination then returns mana).
  4. Lay on Hands as a last resort.
  5. Blessing of Might on the party and an aura at all times; Seal of Light for extra healing.

Healing is not simulated in combat. This rotation is derived from the client numbers (base heal, healing-power coefficient, mana, cast time) at 350 healing power and without talents: it gives an efficiency order, not a simulation result.

Method, Icy Veins recommendations and comparison

Healing is not simulated yet: this is the healing rotation Icy Veins recommends (level 30), reworded.

What Icy Veins recommends (level 30)

  • Flash of Light as the main heal (cheap, fast).
  • Holy Light for big heals when mana allows; instant Holy Shock in emergencies; Lay on Hands as a last resort.
  • Blessing of Might on the party and Devotion or Retribution Aura at all times; Seal of Light for extra healing.

Our comparison

Agreed with Icy Veins: Flash of Light as the main heal, Holy Light for big heals, Holy Shock in emergencies and Lay on Hands as a last resort. The numbers confirm it: Flash of Light is both the most efficient (3.27 per mana) and the cheapest (140 mana). Holy Shock's and Light's Vigil's numbers are not usable in the client.
