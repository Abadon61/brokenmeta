Benediction Retribution · Tier 1 · 5 ranks · Icy Veins : 5 pts · Recommended

Icy Veins (level 30): Reduces the Mana cost of all instant cast spells and abilities by 2%.

Improved Judgement Retribution · Tier 2 · 2 ranks · Icy Veins : 2 pts · Recommended

Icy Veins (level 30): Decreases the cooldown of your Judgement ability by 1 sec.

Holy Conduit Retribution · Tier 2 · 2 ranks · Icy Veins : 2 pts · Recommended

Icy Veins (level 30): Reduces the mana cost of your Consecration, Holy Wrath, Exorcism, and Hammer of Wrath spells by 20%.

Conviction Retribution · Tier 2 · 5 ranks · Icy Veins : 4 pts · Recommended

Icy Veins (level 30): Improves your chance to get a critical strike with melee attacks by 1%.

Sanctified Judgement Retribution · Tier 3 · 3 ranks · Icy Veins : 3 pts · Recommended

Icy Veins (level 30): Gives your Judgement ability a 33% chance to return 20% of the Mana cost of the judged seal.

Seal of Command Retribution · Tier 3 · 1 rank · Icy Veins : 1 pts · Recommended

Icy Veins (level 30): Gives the Paladin a chance to deal additional Holy damage equal to 70% of normal weapon damage. Only one Seal can be active on the Paladin at any one time. Lasts 30 sec. Unleashing this Seal's energy will judge an enemy, instantly causing 48.5 Holy damage, 97 if the target is stunned or incapacitated.

Pursuit of Justice Retribution · Tier 3 · 2 ranks · Icy Veins : 2 pts · Recommended

Icy Veins (level 30): Increases movement speed and mounted movement speed by 8%. This does not stack with other movement speed increasing effects.

Sacred Arbiter Retribution · Tier 4 · 1 rank · Icy Veins : 1 pts · Recommended

Icy Veins (level 30): Increases the damage of your Holy Strike ability by 20% and causes it to refresh all Judgement effects on the target.

Repentance Retribution · Tier 5 · 1 rank · Icy Veins : 1 pts · Recommended

Icy Veins (level 30): Puts the enemy target in a state of meditation, incapacitating them for up to 6 sec. Any damage caused will awaken the target. Only works against Humanoids.

The last-tier talents

These are the deepest talents in the tree: they require 30 points already spent in the specialization. Official game text, at maximum rank.

Twist of Light Tier 7 · 1 rank

Reduces the Mana cost of your Seal spells by 20%, and when you replace your Seal of Command, Seal of Righteousness, Seal of Fury, or Seal of Justice with a different Seal, you gain an Echo of that Seal. Your next melee attack applies the replaced Seal's effects, consuming the Echo.
