Benediction Retribution · Tier 1 · 5 ranks · 5 pts · Recommended

Rank 5/5: Reduces the Mana cost of all instant cast spells and abilities by 10%.

Improved Judgement Retribution · Tier 2 · 2 ranks · 2 pts · Recommended

Rank 2/2: Decreases the cooldown of your Judgement ability by 2 sec.

Conviction Retribution · Tier 2 · 5 ranks · 5 pts · Recommended

Rank 5/5: Improves your chance to get a critical strike with melee attacks by 5%.

Vindication Retribution · Tier 3 · 3 ranks · 3 pts · Recommended

Rank 3/3: Gives your damaging melee attacks a chance to reduce the target's Attack Power by -6, and increase your Attack Power by 3% for 30 sec.

Sanctified Judgement Retribution · Tier 3 · 3 ranks · 3 pts · Recommended

Rank 3/3: Gives your Judgement ability a 100% chance to return 60% of the Mana cost of the judged seal.

Seal of Command Retribution · Tier 3 · 1 rank · 1 pts · Recommended

Rank 1/1: Gives the Paladin a chance to deal additional Holy damage equal to 70% of normal weapon damage. Only one Seal can be active on the Paladin at any one time. Lasts 30 sec. Unleashing this Seal's energy will judge an enemy, instantly causing 48.5 Holy damage, 97 if the target is stunned or incapacitated.

Sacred Arbiter Retribution · Tier 4 · 1 rank · 1 pts · Recommended

Rank 1/1: Increases the damage of your Holy Strike ability by 20% and causes it to refresh all Judgement effects on the target.

Two-Handed Weapon Specialization Retribution · Tier 5 · 3 ranks · 3 pts · Recommended

Rank 3/3: Increases the damage you deal with two-handed melee weapons by 6%.

Vengeance Retribution · Tier 5 · 3 ranks · 3 pts · Recommended

Rank 3/3: Increases your Physical and Holy damage dealt by 3% for 30 sec after landing a non-periodic critical strike. Stacks up to 3 times.

Champion of the Light Retribution · Tier 6 · 3 ranks · 3 pts · Recommended

Rank 3/3: Increases your spell damage by up to 60% of your Intellect.

Instrument of Law Retribution · Tier 6 · 2 ranks · 2 pts · Recommended

Rank 2/2: Reduces the cast time of your Hammer of Wrath by -1.0 sec, and reduces all threat you generate by 20% while Righteous Fury is not active.

Twist of Light Retribution · Tier 7 · 1 rank · 1 pts · Recommended

Rank 1/1: Reduces the Mana cost of your Seal spells by 20%, and when you replace your Seal of Command, Seal of Righteousness, Seal of Fury, or Seal of Justice with a different Seal, you gain an Echo of that Seal. Your next melee attack applies the replaced Seal's effects, consuming the Echo.

Divine Strength Holy · Tier 1 · 5 ranks · 5 pts · Recommended

Rank 5/5: Increases your Strength by 10%.

Divine Intellect Holy · Tier 1 · 5 ranks · 5 pts · Recommended

Rank 5/5: Increases your total Intellect by 10%.

Healing Light Holy · Tier 2 · 3 ranks · 3 pts · Recommended

Rank 3/3: Increases the amount healed by your Holy Light, Flash of Light, and Holy Shock spells by 12%.

Improved Seals Holy · Tier 2 · 3 ranks · 3 pts · Recommended

Rank 3/3: Increases the damage done by your Seals and Judgements by 15%.

Reverence Holy · Tier 3 · 3 ranks · 3 pts · Recommended

Rank 3/3: Allows 30% of your Mana regeneration to continue while casting.

Level 60 rotation

Our simulator's priority at level 60, set against what Icy Veins recommends. Their guides are written for the level-30 beta and for leveling: we keep what everyone agrees on, adopt what the simulation confirmed at level 60 and say where the two differ.

  1. Keep Seal of Command up; Judgement of Command on cooldown.
  2. Holy Strike on cooldown.
  3. Hammer of Wrath in the execute phase.
  4. Two-handed melee swings deal the rest of the damage.

What Icy Veins recommends (level 30)

  • Always keep a Seal up: Seal of Righteousness with lots of spell power and a fast weapon, Seal of Command with a slow weapon.
  • Judgement and Holy Strike on cooldown; Seal of the Crusader before a Judgement if the target lives long; Holy Shock on cooldown for the Shockadin build; Exorcism only against undead and demons.

Our comparison

Agreed: Seal of Command (two-hand weapon), Judgement and Holy Strike on cooldown. Exorcism stays out against a boss (undead and demons only). Not simulated: the Seal of the Crusader swap and the Hammer of Justice bonus.

The last-tier talents

These are the deepest talents in the tree: they require 30 points already spent in the specialization. Official game text, at maximum rank.

Twist of Light Tier 7 · 1 rank

Reduces the Mana cost of your Seal spells by 20%, and when you replace your Seal of Command, Seal of Righteousness, Seal of Fury, or Seal of Justice with a different Seal, you gain an Echo of that Seal. Your next melee attack applies the replaced Seal's effects, consuming the Echo.

Single-rank talents

Talents you take only once, with their tier (official text at maximum rank).

Seal of Command Tier 3 · 1 rank

Gives the Paladin a chance to deal additional Holy damage equal to 70% of normal weapon damage. Only one Seal can be active on the Paladin at any one time. Lasts 30 sec. Unleashing this Seal's energy will judge an enemy, instantly causing 48.5 Holy damage, 97 if the target is stunned or incapacitated.

Sacred Arbiter Tier 4 · 1 rank

Increases the damage of your Holy Strike ability by 20% and causes it to refresh all Judgement effects on the target.

Repentance Tier 5 · 1 rank

Puts the enemy target in a state of meditation, incapacitating them for up to 6 sec. Any damage caused will awaken the target. Only works against Humanoids.
