Improved Wrath Balance · Tier 1 · 5 ranks · 5 pts · Recommended

Rank 5/5: Reduces the cast time of your Wrath spell by 0.5 sec and its Mana cost by 50%.

Genesis Balance · Tier 1 · 5 ranks · 5 pts · Recommended

Rank 5/5: Increases the periodic damage and healing done by your spells and abilities by 5%.

Moonglow Balance · Tier 2 · 3 ranks · 3 pts · Recommended

Rank 3/3: Reduces the Mana cost of your damaging spells by 25%.

Improved Moonfire Balance · Tier 2 · 2 ranks · 2 pts · Recommended

Rank 2/2: Increases the damage and critical strike chance of your Moonfire spell by 10%.

Nature's Majesty Balance · Tier 2 · 2 ranks · 2 pts · Recommended

Rank 2/2: Increases your critical strike chance with spells and melee attacks by 4%.

Nature's Reach Balance · Tier 2 · 2 ranks · 2 pts · Recommended

Rank 2/2: Increases the range of your offensive Balance spells by 20% and improves your chance to hit by 4%.

Nature's Splendor Balance · Tier 3 · 1 rank · 1 pts · Recommended

Rank 1/1: Increases the duration of your Moonfire and Rejuvenation spells by 3 sec, your Regrowth spell by 6 sec, and your Insect Swarm spell by 2 sec.

Insect Swarm Balance · Tier 4 · 1 rank · 1 pts · Recommended

Rank 1/1: The enemy target is swarmed by insects, decreasing their chance to hit with attacks by 2% and causing 48 Nature damage over 12 sec.

Vengeance Balance · Tier 4 · 5 ranks · 5 pts · Recommended

Rank 5/5: Increases the critical strike damage bonus of your Arcane and Nature spells by 100%.

Improved Starfire Balance · Tier 4 · 5 ranks · 5 pts · Recommended

Rank 5/5: Reduces the cast time of Starfire by 0.5 sec and Starfire has a 15% chance to stun its target for 3 sec.

Nature's Grace Balance · Tier 5 · 1 rank · 1 pts · Recommended

Rank 1/1: All non-periodic spell criticals grace you with a blessing of nature, increasing your spellcasting speed and reducing your global cooldown by 10% for 3 sec.

Eclipse Balance · Tier 5 · 3 ranks · 3 pts · Recommended

Rank 3/3: Your Wrath spell reduces the cast time of your next 2 Starfire spells by -0.50 sec. Stores up to 4 charges. Lasts 15 sec.

Moonfury Balance · Tier 6 · 5 ranks · 5 pts · Recommended

Rank 5/5: Increases the damage done by your Arcane and Nature spells by 10%.

Moonkin Form Balance · Tier 7 · 1 rank · 1 pts · Recommended

Rank 1/1: Shapeshift into Moonkin Form, increasing Omen of Clarity's chance to trigger by 100%, Armor contribution from items by 360%, and all party members within 45 yards have their Critical Strike chance increased by 3%, exclusive with Leader of the Pack. Also protects the caster from Polymorph effects and prevents the use of healing spells. The act of shapeshifting frees the caster of Polymorph and Movement Impairing effects.

Furor Restoration · Tier 1 · 5 ranks · 5 pts · Recommended

Rank 5/5: Gives you a 100% chance to gain 10 Rage when you shapeshift into Bear Form or Dire Bear Form. When you shift into Cat Form, you will regain 100% of the Energy you had when you were last in Cat Form, plus 10 Energy for each second you spent not in Bear Form, Cat Form, or Dire Bear Form, up to a maximum of 100 Energy.

Naturalist Restoration · Tier 2 · 5 ranks · 5 pts · Recommended

Rank 5/5: Reduces the cast time of your Healing Touch spell by 0.5 sec and increases all damage you deal by 5%.

How to read these measures

Measured by the simulator on the character above: the DPS gained by one more point of each stat, with the same random draws on both sides. The relative value compares each stat with one point of spell power. Crit, hit and haste are counted per rating point, like Strength or Agility, so they compare directly; they respect the caps: hit at the cap adds nothing. A value near zero or negative is within the margin of error or tied to a limit (mana, cap).

DPS gained when points of each stat are added on top of the simulated gear. Click a stat (or its row in the table) to bring its curve forward; the ring marks the estimated cap, past which every point is worth less than 20% of what it gave at the start.

Level 60 rotation

  1. Keep Moonfire and Insect Swarm up.
  2. Starfire as the filler.
  3. Wrath to alternate and benefit from Eclipse.
  4. Mana potions and the mana gem.
Method, Icy Veins recommendations and comparison

Our simulator's priority at level 60, set against what Icy Veins recommends. Their guides are written for the level-30 beta and for leveling: we keep what everyone agrees on, adopt what the simulation confirmed at level 60 and say where the two differ.

What Icy Veins recommends (level 30)

  • Keep Moonfire up, then Wrath; Insect Swarm after Moonfire from level 25.
  • Hurricane (area) is not available in the beta.

Our comparison

Agreed on Moonfire and Insect Swarm. At level 60 the simulator mostly casts Starfire (about 210 DPS against 56 for Wrath, see the ranking page), with Wrath as a complement.

Method and enchant notes

The gear our optimizer picks for this specialization among the items known from the beta (dungeons, the few raid items already known and crafted gear, which can be bought or made for you: prices and materials are not taken into account), with item effects simulated. Nothing is hand-picked: it is the result of the ranking's computation, and it will change as new items are discovered.

Enchantments up to skill 300, valued by the simulator when they give flat stats or flat weapon damage. The character does not need the profession: they can be bought or applied by an enchanter; Leatherworking armor kits (chest, legs, feet, hands) and Engineering scopes are counted too. A kit and an enchantment share the same permanent slot: a piece carries one or the other, and the legs, which have no enchantment, get the kit; spell-proc enchants (Crusader, Fiery Weapon...) and school damage are not simulated. The beta data has no ring enchantment up to skill 300.
