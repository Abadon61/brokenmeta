Nature's Focus Restoration · Tier 1 · 5 ranks · 5 pts · Recommended

Rank 5/5: Gives you a 70% chance to avoid interruption caused by damage while casting Arcane and Nature spells.

Furor Restoration · Tier 1 · 5 ranks · 5 pts · Recommended

Rank 5/5: Gives you a 100% chance to gain 10 Rage when you shapeshift into Bear Form or Dire Bear Form. When you shift into Cat Form, you will regain 100% of the Energy you had when you were last in Cat Form, plus 10 Energy for each second you spent not in Bear Form, Cat Form, or Dire Bear Form, up to a maximum of 100 Energy.

Naturalist Restoration · Tier 2 · 5 ranks · 5 pts · Recommended

Rank 5/5: Reduces the cast time of your Healing Touch spell by 0.5 sec and increases all damage you deal by 5%.

Subtlety Restoration · Tier 2 · 3 ranks · 3 pts · Recommended

Rank 3/3: Reduces the threat generated by your Nature and Arcane spells by 30%.

Natural Shapeshifter Restoration · Tier 2 · 3 ranks · 3 pts · Recommended

Rank 3/3: Reduces the mana cost of all shapeshifting by 30%.

Reflection Restoration · Tier 3 · 3 ranks · 3 pts · Recommended

Rank 3/3: Allows 50% of your Mana regeneration to continue while casting.

Gift of Nature Restoration · Tier 3 · 5 ranks · 5 pts · Recommended

Rank 5/5: Increases the effect of all your healing spells by 10%.

Gift of the Earthmother Restoration · Tier 3 · 1 rank · 1 pts · Recommended

Rank 1/1: Reduces the global cooldown by -0.5 seconds on your Rejuvenation, Swiftmend, and Wild Growth spells.

Tranquil Spirit Restoration · Tier 4 · 5 ranks · 5 pts · Recommended

Rank 5/5: Reduces the mana cost of your Healing Touch and Tranquility spells by 10%.

Improved Rejuvenation Restoration · Tier 4 · 3 ranks · 3 pts · Recommended

Rank 3/3: Increases the effect of your Rejuvenation spell by 15%.

Swiftmend Restoration · Tier 4 · 1 rank · 1 pts · Recommended

Rank 1/1: Instantly heals a target with an active Rejuvenation or Regrowth effect for an amount equal to the full duration of the periodic effect of one of those spells.

Nature's Swiftness Restoration · Tier 5 · 1 rank · 1 pts · Recommended

Rank 1/1: When activated, your next Nature spell becomes an instant cast spell.

Living Spirit Restoration · Tier 5 · 3 ranks · 3 pts · Recommended

Rank 3/3: Increases your Spirit by 15%.

Improved Tranquility Restoration · Tier 5 · 2 ranks · 2 pts · Recommended

Rank 2/2: Reduces threat caused by Tranquility by 100% and its cooldown by 60%.

Improved Regrowth Restoration · Tier 6 · 5 ranks · 5 pts · Recommended

Rank 5/5: Increases the critical effect chance of your Regrowth spell by 50%.

Wild Growth Restoration · Tier 7 · 1 rank · 1 pts · Recommended

Rank 1/1: Heals the target and their party for 280 over 7 sec. Party members must be within … yards of target. The amount healed is applied quickly at first, and slows down as Wild Growth reaches its full duration.

Level 60 rotation

Healing is not simulated yet: this is the healing rotation Icy Veins recommends (level 30), reworded.

  1. Rejuvenation on the tank and anyone taking light damage (3.24 healing per mana, instant).
  2. Healing Touch as the big heal (3.19 per mana, 766 healing per second on a 3.5 s cast; Naturalist takes 0.5 s off).
  3. Swiftmend after a Rejuvenation or Regrowth when a target drops low (15 s cooldown).
  4. Regrowth in emergencies (2.73 per mana but 1,200 healing per second, with heals over time).
  5. Wild Growth against an injured group (3.6 per mana across 5 targets); Tranquility for heavy area damage.

Healing is not simulated in combat. This rotation is derived from the client numbers (base heal, healing-power coefficient, mana, cast time) at 350 healing power and without talents: it gives an efficiency order, not a simulation result.

What Icy Veins recommends (level 30)

  • Rejuvenation on the tank and anyone taking light damage, Healing Touch to top up.
  • Swiftmend one second after a Rejuvenation when a target drops low.
  • Thorns on the tank before the run; Wrath or Moonfire on enemies when you have spare time.

Our comparison

Agreed with Icy Veins: Rejuvenation on the tank and light damage, Healing Touch to top up, Swiftmend after a Rejuvenation. The numbers add Regrowth for emergencies (the most healing per second, but less mana-efficient) and Wild Growth, a last-tier talent, against groups (3.6 per mana across 5 targets).
