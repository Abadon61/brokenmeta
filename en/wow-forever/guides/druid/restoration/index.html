The last-tier talents

These are the deepest talents in the tree: they require 30 points already spent in the specialization. Official game text, at maximum rank.

Wild Growth Tier 7 · 1 rank

Heals the target and their party for 280 over 7 sec. Party members must be within … yards of target. The amount healed is applied quickly at first, and slows down as Wild Growth reaches its full duration.

Single-rank talents

Talents you take only once, with their tier (official text at maximum rank).

Gift of the Earthmother Tier 3 · 1 rank

Reduces the global cooldown by -0.5 seconds on your Rejuvenation, Swiftmend, and Wild Growth spells.

Swiftmend Tier 4 · 1 rank

Instantly heals a target with an active Rejuvenation or Regrowth effect for an amount equal to the full duration of the periodic effect of one of those spells.

Nature's Swiftness Tier 5 · 1 rank

When activated, your next Nature spell becomes an instant cast spell.
