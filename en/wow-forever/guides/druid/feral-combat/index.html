The last-tier talents

These are the deepest talents in the tree: they require 30 points already spent in the specialization. Official game text, at maximum rank.

Berserk Tier 7 · 1 rank

Requires Cat Form, Bear Form, Dire Bear Form Causes your Mangle ability to strike up to 3 targets, removes its cooldown, and increases the critical strike chance of your Combo Point-generating abilities by 100%. Clears and grants immunity to Fear effects for the duration. Lasts 15 sec.

Single-rank talents

Talents you take only once, with their tier (official text at maximum rank).

Feral Charge Tier 3 · 1 rank

Requires Bear Form, Dire Bear Form Charge an enemy, immobilizing them and interrupting any spell they are casting for 4 sec. Leap behind an enemy.

Mangle Tier 4 · 1 rank

Mangle the target for 100% normal damage plus 26.

Leader of the Pack Tier 5 · 1 rank

While in Cat Form, Bear Form, or Dire Bear Form, the Leader of the Pack increases the critical strike chance of all party members within 45 yards by 3%, exclusive with Moonkin Aura.
