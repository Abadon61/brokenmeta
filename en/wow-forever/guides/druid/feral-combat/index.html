Feral Combat Druid: specialization guide

Melee DPS and tank #7 in the ranking · 592 DPS

Feral Combat is the Melee DPS and tank specialization of the Druid in WoW: Forever. This page summarizes its talent tree as it appears in the beta client (build 1.60.1.70425); the role is the one stated by the Icy Veins guide for the specialization.

Quick answers: Feral Combat Druid

Is Feral Combat Druid a good spec in WoW Forever?

In our level-60 simulation (180 seconds on a stationary boss, raid buffs, best known gear), Feral Combat Druid ranks #7 of 20 DPS specs with 592 DPS. #1 is Affliction Warlock (754 DPS). A stationary boss does not tell everything: group utility and moving fights matter too.

Which talents should Feral Combat Druid take?

Our simulator's build uses 51 points, split Balance 14 / Feral Combat 37. The talents that weigh the most in the simulation: Shifting Power, Shredding Attacks, Natural Instinct. Open this build in the talent calculator →

What gear should Feral Combat Druid wear?

Without raid items, the best set the simulator finds gives 582 DPS, −1.6% against the set that includes raid items. Stat priority: Weapon Damage > Hit Chance > Critical Strike. Full gear and enchantments →

What rotation does Feral Combat Druid play?

Opening: Keep Savage Roar and Rip up ; Shred to build combo points, keep Rake up ; Ferocious Bite at 5 combo points. Full rotation →

Talent details of the build (18)
Ferocity Feral Combat · Tier 1 · 5 ranks · 5 pts · Recommended

Rank 5/5: Reduces the cost of your Maul, Primal Bite, Swipe, Claw, and Rake abilities by 5 Rage or Energy.

Heart of the Wild Feral Combat · Tier 1 · 5 ranks · 5 pts · Recommended

Rank 5/5: Increases your Intellect by 10%. In addition, while in Bear Form or Dire Bear Form your Stamina is increased by 20% and while in Cat Form your Strength is increased by 10%.

Thick Hide Feral Combat · Tier 2 · 3 ranks · 3 pts · Recommended

Rank 3/3: While in Bear Form, Cat Form, Dire Bear Form, or Moonkin Form, you gain 3 additional base Armor per level and another 2.00 base Armor for each point of defense skill beyond five times your level. This amount can be further increased by multipliers from those forms.

Shredding Attacks Feral Combat · Tier 3 · 3 ranks · 3 pts · Recommended

Rank 3/3: Reduces the Energy cost of your Shred ability by 18 and reduces the Rage cost of your Lacerate ability by -3.

Savage Fury Feral Combat · Tier 3 · 2 ranks · 2 pts · Recommended

Rank 2/2: Increases the damage caused by your Claw, Rake, Shred, Maul, and Swipe abilities by 10%.

Sharpened Claws Feral Combat · Tier 3 · 2 ranks · 2 pts · Recommended

Rank 2/2: Increases your critical strike chance while in Bear Form, Dire Bear Form, or Cat Form by 6%.

Shifting Power Feral Combat · Tier 4 · 1 rank · 1 pts · Recommended

Rank 1/1: Instantly convert … Mana into … Energy. Shifting Power's cost is reduced by effects that reduce the cost of Shapeshifting.

Predatory Strikes Feral Combat · Tier 4 · 3 ranks · 3 pts · Recommended

Rank 3/3: Increases your melee Attack Power in Cat Form, Bear Form, and Dire Bear Form by 150% of your level.

Blood Frenzy Feral Combat · Tier 4 · 2 ranks · 2 pts · Recommended

Rank 2/2: Gives you a 100% chance to gain an additional 5 Rage any time you get a critical strike while in Bear Form or Dire Bear Form. In addition, your non-periodic critical strikes from Cat Form abilities that generate Combo Points have a 100% chance to add an additional Combo Point.

Improved Shifting Power Feral Combat · Tier 5 · 2 ranks · 2 pts · Recommended

Rank 2/2: Reduces the cooldown of your Shifting Power spell by -8 sec.

Leader of the Pack Feral Combat · Tier 5 · 1 rank · 1 pts · Recommended

Rank 1/1: While in Cat Form, Bear Form, or Dire Bear Form, the Leader of the Pack increases the melee and ranged Critical Strike chance of all party or raid members within 45 yards by 3%.

Natural Instinct Feral Combat · Tier 5 · 2 ranks · 2 pts · Recommended

Rank 2/2: Increases the critical strike damage bonus of your melee abilities by 20% and increases your spell healing by 25% of your Intellect.

Rend and Tear Feral Combat · Tier 6 · 5 ranks · 5 pts · Recommended

Rank 5/5: Increases damage done by your melee abilities on Bleeding targets by 10%.

Berserk Feral Combat · Tier 7 · 1 rank · 1 pts · Recommended

Rank 1/1: Requires Cat Form, Bear Form, Dire Bear Form Causes your Primal Bite ability to strike up to 3 targets, removes its cooldown, and increases the critical strike chance of your Combo Point-generating abilities by 100%. Clears and grants immunity to Fear effects for the duration. Lasts 15 sec.

Improved Wrath Balance · Tier 1 · 5 ranks · 5 pts · Recommended

Rank 5/5: Reduces the cast time of your Wrath spell by 0.5 sec and its Mana cost by 50%.

Genesis Balance · Tier 1 · 5 ranks · 5 pts · Recommended

Rank 5/5: Increases the periodic damage and healing done by your spells and abilities by 5%.

Nature's Majesty Balance · Tier 2 · 2 ranks · 2 pts · Recommended

Rank 2/2: Increases your critical strike chance with spells and melee attacks by 4%.

Nature's Reach Balance · Tier 2 · 2 ranks · 2 pts · Recommended

Rank 2/2: Increases the range of your offensive Balance spells by 20% and improves your chance to hit by 4%.

How to read these measures

Measured by the simulator on the character above: the DPS gained by one more point of each stat, with the same random draws on both sides. The relative value compares each stat with one point of attack power. Crit, hit and haste are counted per rating point, like Strength or Agility, so they compare directly; they respect the caps: hit at the cap adds nothing. A value near zero or negative is within the margin of error or tied to a limit (mana, cap).

DPS gained when points of each stat are added on top of the simulated gear. Click a stat (or its row in the table) to bring its curve forward; the ring marks the estimated cap, past which every point is worth less than 20% of what it gave at the start.

Level 60 rotation

Feral Combat

  1. Keep Savage Roar and Rip up.
  2. Shred to build combo points, keep Rake up.
  3. Ferocious Bite at 5 combo points.
  4. Tiger's Fury on cooldown, Shifting Power to regain energy, Berserk on cooldown.

Feral Combat (bear)

  1. Keep Lacerate stacked to 5.
  2. Primal Bite (20 rage, 6 s cooldown) on cooldown: the main rage spender, with high threat.
  3. Maul on every swing with the available rage.
  4. Swipe with the rest of the rage.
  5. Enrage on cooldown.
Method, Icy Veins recommendations and comparison

Our simulator's priority at level 60, set against what Icy Veins recommends. Their guides are written for the level-30 beta and for leveling: we keep what everyone agrees on, adopt what the simulation confirmed at level 60 and say where the two differ.

What Icy Veins recommends (level 30)

  • Cat: Shred to build combo points (Claw if you cannot get behind), Rip when the target lives long enough, Shifting Power on cooldown; Rake is secondary.
  • Bear: Primal Bite is the main rage spender, Swipe against several enemies, Maul to burn extra rage; Enrage on cooldown.

Our comparison

Agreed on both forms. Added after measuring: Primal Bite in the bear rotation (+9% threat per second, not counting its "high threat", which the client does not quantify). Cat: the simulator also keeps Savage Roar up and uses Tiger's Fury and Berserk.

Method and enchant notes

The gear our optimizer picks for this specialization among the items known from the beta (dungeons, the few raid items already known and crafted gear, which can be bought or made for you: prices and materials are not taken into account), with item effects simulated. Nothing is hand-picked: it is the result of the ranking's computation, and it will change as new items are discovered.

Enchantments up to skill 300, valued by the simulator when they give flat stats or flat weapon damage. The character does not need the profession: they can be bought or applied by an enchanter; Leatherworking armor kits (chest, legs, feet, hands) and Engineering scopes are counted too. A kit and an enchantment share the same permanent slot: a piece carries one or the other, and the legs, which have no enchantment, get the kit; spell-proc enchants (Crusader, Fiery Weapon...) and school damage are not simulated. The beta data has no ring enchantment up to skill 300.
