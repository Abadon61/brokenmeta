Frequently asked questions

Where is Blackfathom Deeps in WoW: Forever?

The Blackfathom Deeps entrance is in Ashenvale, on the coast (WoW Classic location). This zone is contested in WoW Classic: both factions can reach it.

What loot drops in Blackfathom Deeps in WoW: Forever?

38 equippable items: 25 dropped by bosses and creatures, and 13 quest rewards. Gelihast drops Algae Fists, Cursed Murloc Eye, Reef Axe.

How many bosses are in Blackfathom Deeps?

9 encounters, in this order: Ghamoo-ra, Lady Sarevess, Geilhast, Lorgus Jett, Old Serra'kis, Twilight Lord Kelris, Baron Aquanis, Aku'mai, Gelihast.

What level is Blackfathom Deeps in WoW: Forever?

Blackfathom Deeps is meant for levels 22-32 (range from the Wowhead Forever database).

What level is too high for Blackfathom Deeps?

Blackfathom Deeps is meant for levels 22-32 and its loot requires levels 17 to 26 (from our 38 items). Past level 32 you are above the dungeon's range: the "Dungeons by level" page ranks every dungeon for your level.

What does BFD stand for in WoW: Forever?

BFD is the short name of Blackfathom Deeps, the one players type to look for a group or a guide.

How to use this page
  • The "Best loot for each spec" table ranks the items with the stat weights our simulator computes for every DPS spec: damage gained per point of attack power, spell power, hit and crit, plus weapon DPS for melee and hunters. Tanks and healers are not in it: the simulator only measures damage.
  • Check the item type(s) your class can equip (e.g. Mail), a primary stat (Strength, Agility or Intellect), and the secondary stats you're after.
  • This ranking has limits: it ignores set bonuses, special effects of trinkets and weapons, Stamina and Spirit, and its weights are computed on a level-30 character. The full table is still there to check for yourself.
  • Item names are in English: their French translation is not in the data we retrieved.
