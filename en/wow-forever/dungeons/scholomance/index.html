Where is Scholomance in WoW: Forever?

What loot drops in Scholomance in WoW: Forever?

What level is Scholomance in WoW: Forever?

How to use this page
  • The "Best loot for each spec" table ranks the items with the stat weights our simulator computes for every DPS spec: damage gained per point of attack power, spell power, hit and crit, plus weapon DPS for melee and hunters. Tanks and healers are not in it: the simulator only measures damage.
  • Check the item type(s) your class can equip (e.g. Mail), a primary stat (Strength, Agility or Intellect), and the secondary stats you're after.
  • This ranking has limits: it ignores set bonuses, special effects of trinkets and weapons, Stamina and Spirit, and its weights are computed on a level-30 character. The full table is still there to check for yourself.
  • Item names are in English: their French translation is not in the data we retrieved.
