Frequently asked questions

Where is Shadowfang Keep in WoW: Forever?

The Shadowfang Keep entrance is in Silverpine Forest (WoW Classic location). This zone is Horde territory in WoW Classic, so an Alliance group has to cross enemy land to get there.

What loot drops in Shadowfang Keep in WoW: Forever?

45 equippable items: 38 dropped by bosses and creatures, and 7 quest rewards. Archmage Arugal drops Robes of Arugal, Belt of Arugal, Meteor Shard.

How many bosses are in Shadowfang Keep?

9 encounters, in this order: Rethilgore, Razorclaw the Butcher, Baron Silverlaine, Commander Springvale, Odo the Blindwatcher, Fenrus the Devourer, Wolf Master Nandos, Archmage Arugal, Deathsworn Captain.

What level is Shadowfang Keep in WoW: Forever?

Shadowfang Keep is meant for levels 22-30 (range from the Wowhead Forever database).

What level is too high for Shadowfang Keep?

Shadowfang Keep is meant for levels 22-30 and its loot requires levels 16 to 23 (from our 45 items). Past level 30 you are above the dungeon's range: the "Dungeons by level" page ranks every dungeon for your level.

What does SFK stand for in WoW: Forever?

SFK is the short name of Shadowfang Keep, the one players type to look for a group or a guide.

How to use this page
  • The "Best loot for each spec" table ranks the items with the stat weights our simulator computes for every DPS spec: damage gained per point of attack power, spell power, hit and crit, plus weapon DPS for melee and hunters. Tanks and healers are not in it: the simulator only measures damage.

  • This ranking has limits: it ignores set bonuses, special effects of trinkets and weapons, Stamina and Spirit, and its weights are computed on a level-30 character. The full table is still there to check for yourself.
