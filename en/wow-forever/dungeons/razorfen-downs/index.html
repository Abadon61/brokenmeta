Frequently asked questions

Where is Razorfen Downs in WoW: Forever?

The Razorfen Downs entrance is in the southern Barrens (WoW Classic location). This zone is Horde territory in WoW Classic, so an Alliance group has to cross enemy land to get there.

What loot drops in Razorfen Downs in WoW: Forever?

21 equippable items: 15 dropped by bosses and creatures, and 6 quest rewards. Amnennar the Coldbringer drops Icemetal Barbute, Deathchill Armor, Robes of the Lich, Bonefingers, Coldrage Dagger.

How many bosses are in Razorfen Downs?

6 encounters, in this order: Tuten'kash, Plaguemaw the Rotting, Mordresh Fire Eye, Ragglesnout, Glutton, Amnennar the Coldbringer.

What level is Razorfen Downs in WoW: Forever?

Razorfen Downs is meant for levels 37-47 (range from the Wowhead Forever database).

What level is too high for Razorfen Downs?

Razorfen Downs is meant for levels 37-47 and its loot requires levels 28 to 40 (from our 21 items). Past level 47 you are above the dungeon's range: the "Dungeons by level" page ranks every dungeon for your level.

What does RFD stand for in WoW: Forever?

RFD is the short name of Razorfen Downs, the one players type to look for a group or a guide.

How to use this page
  • The "Best loot for each spec" table ranks the items with the stat weights our simulator computes for every DPS spec: damage gained per point of attack power, spell power, hit and crit, plus weapon DPS for melee and hunters. Tanks and healers are not in it: the simulator only measures damage.
  • Check the item type(s) your class can equip (e.g. Mail), a primary stat (Strength, Agility or Intellect), and the secondary stats you're after.
  • This ranking has limits: it ignores set bonuses, special effects of trinkets and weapons, Stamina and Spirit, and its weights are computed on a level-30 character. The full table is still there to check for yourself.
  • Item names are in English: their French translation is not in the data we retrieved.
