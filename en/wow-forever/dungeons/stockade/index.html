Frequently asked questions

Where is Stormwind Stockade in WoW: Forever?

The Stormwind Stockade entrance is in Stormwind City (WoW Classic location). This zone is Alliance territory in WoW Classic, so a Horde group has to cross enemy land to get there.

What loot drops in Stormwind Stockade in WoW: Forever?

29 equippable items: 20 dropped by bosses and creatures, and 9 quest rewards. Bruegal Ironknuckle drops Jimmied Handcuffs, Iron Knuckles, Prison Shank.

How many bosses are in Stormwind Stockade?

6 encounters, in this order: Targorr the Dread, Kam Deepfury, Hamhock, Dextren Ward, Bazil Thredd, Bruegal Ironknuckle.

What level is Stormwind Stockade in WoW: Forever?

Stormwind Stockade is meant for levels 22-32 (range from the Wowhead Forever database).

What level is too high for Stormwind Stockade?

Stormwind Stockade is meant for levels 22-32 and its loot requires levels 19 to 26 (from our 29 items). Past level 32 you are above the dungeon's range: the "Dungeons by level" page ranks every dungeon for your level.

What does Stocks stand for in WoW: Forever?

Stocks is the short name of Stormwind Stockade, the one players type to look for a group or a guide.

How to use this page
  • The "Best loot for each spec" table ranks the items with the stat weights our simulator computes for every DPS spec: damage gained per point of attack power, spell power, hit and crit, plus weapon DPS for melee and hunters. Tanks and healers are not in it: the simulator only measures damage.
  • Check the item type(s) your class can equip (e.g. Mail), a primary stat (Strength, Agility or Intellect), and the secondary stats you're after.
  • This ranking has limits: it ignores set bonuses, special effects of trinkets and weapons, Stamina and Spirit, and its weights are computed on a level-30 character. The full table is still there to check for yourself.
  • Item names are in English: their French translation is not in the data we retrieved.
