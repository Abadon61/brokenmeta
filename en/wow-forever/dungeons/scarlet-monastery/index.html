Frequently asked questions

Where is Scarlet Monastery in WoW: Forever?

The Scarlet Monastery entrance is in Tirisfal Glades (WoW Classic location). This zone is Horde territory in WoW Classic, so an Alliance group has to cross enemy land to get there.

What loot drops in Scarlet Monastery in WoW: Forever?

69 equippable items: 56 dropped by bosses and creatures, and 13 quest rewards. High Inquisitor Whitemane drops Whitemane's Chapeau, Triune Amulet, Hand of Righteousness.

How many bosses are in Scarlet Monastery?

10 encounters, in this order: Interrogator Vishas, Bloodmage Thalnos, Ironspine, Fallen Champion, Azshir the Sleepless, Houndmaster Loksey, Arcanist Doan, Herod, High Inquisitor Fairbanks, High Inquisitor Whitemane.

What level is Scarlet Monastery in WoW: Forever?

Scarlet Monastery is meant for levels 26-45 (range from the Wowhead Forever database).

What level is too high for Scarlet Monastery?

Scarlet Monastery is meant for levels 26-45 and its loot requires levels 1 to 39 (from our 69 items). Past level 45 you are above the dungeon's range: the "Dungeons by level" page ranks every dungeon for your level.

What does SM stand for in WoW: Forever?

SM is the short name of Scarlet Monastery, the one players type to look for a group or a guide.

How to use this page
  • The "Best loot for each spec" table ranks the items with the stat weights our simulator computes for every DPS spec: damage gained per point of attack power, spell power, hit and crit, plus weapon DPS for melee and hunters. Tanks and healers are not in it: the simulator only measures damage.
  • Check the item type(s) your class can equip (e.g. Mail), a primary stat (Strength, Agility or Intellect), and the secondary stats you're after.
  • This ranking has limits: it ignores set bonuses, special effects of trinkets and weapons, Stamina and Spirit, and its weights are computed on a level-30 character. The full table is still there to check for yourself.
  • Item names are in English: their French translation is not in the data we retrieved.
