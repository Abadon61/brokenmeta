Broken Meta : Hub, the WoW: Forever addon

One free addon, four sections: DPS tells you what every item is worth for your spec, Professions finds an available crafter anywhere on your realm or levels your profession at the best price, Economy shows what your items are worth and the auction house deals, and Dungeons lists each boss's loot.

Broken Meta : Professions

Find an available crafter

  • The directory of crafters who run the addon and set themselves available, across your whole realm and faction, whatever their zone.
  • Their race, level, professions with skill level, and a short message ("weapons and armor, bring your mats").
  • Their recipes: one click to see everything a crafter can make, with the game's tooltip.
  • Item search: type an item name, the directory keeps the crafters who can make it.
  • A Whisper button to message them yourself. The addon never sends a message for you.
  • Your favourite crafters: a star keeps them, even when they are not announced.
  • The requests board: post "looking for [item]" with one click; crafters who know the recipe are told.

Offer your services

  1. Open your profession window once: the addon notes your recipes.
  2. In Broken Meta : Professions > My profile, write a short message and click Available.
  3. Your announce is visible to everyone for one hour. The addon warns you before it ends: one click on Renew extends it.

Level a profession at the best price

  • The Leveling tab follows the optimal route of the profession guides, from your current skill.
  • Scan the auction house from the addon: every reagent shows its current price (vendor, AH or crafted, the cheapest).
  • For each step: the total cost, the cost of one craft and the crafted item's AH price, to know whether selling it pays for your leveling.
  • The shopping list: every reagent the rest of the route needs, minus what you already carry, with a one-click AH search.
  • The Workshop: your profitable crafts (AH price minus the cut) and the recipes to learn, with where to get them.

Broken Meta : DPS

  • Every item's DPS value in its tooltip, for your spec, computed by the site's simulator.
  • Your upgrades spotted in your bags and in the dungeons of your level, with the most rewarding dungeon.
  • Your personal stat weights: compute them on Simulate my character, then import them into the addon (redo it every 5 levels, the addon reminds you).
  • A character export to paste on the site to simulate your real DPS.

Broken Meta : Economy

  • The auction house price in the tooltip of every item, from your own scans (median of the last days).
  • What your bags are worth: each item's AH and vendor price, and the better way to sell it.
  • A price's history: type a name or shift-click an item to see its price in each of your scans.
  • Deals: items listed at least 30% under their usual price, with the resale margin.

Broken Meta : Dungeons

  • Loot boss by boss for the dungeon you are in (detected automatically) or the one you pick, with the quest rewards.
  • For each item: its DPS value for your spec, the gain if it's an upgrade, and a check if you already own it.
  • A history of what you looted in dungeons, with the date.

Install the addon

  1. The easiest way: install it with the CurseForge app, which also keeps it up to date.
  2. Without the app: download the .zip on its CurseForge page and unzip it into WoW: Forever's Interface\AddOns folder. You should get a BrokenMetaWeights folder.
  3. Restart the game completely, then click the spiral button around the minimap (or type /bmw).

Frequently asked questions

Is the addon allowed by Blizzard?

Yes. It is free, its code is readable, and it automates nothing: every crafter announce comes from their own click, like with group-finder addons. Players contact each other by whisper, themselves.

Why must I click "Available" each time I log in?

The game only lets an addon post your announce after a click from you. So your availability resets when you log in, and an announce lasts one hour.

Does my data go anywhere?

No. The directory goes through the game, between addon players. Nothing is sent to the site unless you choose to share your data.

Why can't I see a friend who has the addon?

They need a recent version, your realm and faction, and to have clicked Available. Click Refresh in the Crafters tab.
