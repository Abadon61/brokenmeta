Full DPS ranking and method

Why a simulated tier list?

  • Every spec goes through the same engine, with the best known gear, optimized talents and raid buffs: the gap between two lines comes from the game, not from an opinion.
  • Tiers depend on the gap to the best spec of the same role. Nothing is placed by hand, and the ranking is recomputed when patches or new items justify it.
  • Limits are shown: a single-target fight with no movement or boss mechanics, and only items that have already been datamined. The full method is at the bottom of the DPS tier list.

Frequently asked questions

Where is the WoW Forever tier list?

On this page, which links to every ranking: level 60 DPS (simulated), tanks (threat per second), races, sets and the leveling guide. Healers are not ranked yet.

What is the best class in WoW Forever?

In our simulation the highest DPS spec is Hunter Beast Mastery with 1,370 DPS (build 1.60.1.70753). S tier holds: Hunter Beast Mastery and Hunter Marksmanship. The full ranking is on the DPS tier list.

Is your tier list up to date?

It was recomputed on November 10, 2026 with game build 1.60.1.70753. That date comes from the last simulator run, not from the page date: it changes when the numbers change.

Is there a healer or PvP tier list?

Not yet. The simulator models neither healing nor PvP, and we would rather show nothing than an invented number.
