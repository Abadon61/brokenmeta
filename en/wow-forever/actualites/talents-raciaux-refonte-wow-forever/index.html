WoW Forever: the October 15 racial rework, race by race

By BrokenMeta.gg · October 16, 2026

The October 15, 2026 WoW Forever beta build changes the racials of nine races. Human, Orc and Dwarf weapon specializations give way to critical bonuses, Tauren gain critical damage, Trolls double their regeneration and the Orc loses some defense. Here is the full table, what it means for picking a race before the November 4 launch, and what we have not simulated yet.

UPTrollHealth regeneration 10% → 20%

What it means for picking your race

  • UP Human, Orc, Dwarf: no more weapon dependence. You used to need a sword, axe or mace to use the racial. Now the crit bonus applies whatever you hold: a caster Human or a Dwarf Priest benefits too.
  • UP Tauren: a real damage racial. +2% critical damage and healing beats 1% hit once you have crit, and War Stomp comes back sooner.
  • UP Troll: the most visible gain out of combat, since health regeneration doubles. Blood Fury is unchanged, the one that moved is the Orc's.
  • NERF Orc: the big loser of the list. Blood Fury goes to a 3 minute cooldown and Shatter Curse protects much less (-5% magic damage instead of -15%), even though it now dispels curses.
  • BALANCE Gnome and Undead: consistency tweaks. Eureka! now lasts 8 s with no charges and Touch of the Grave triggers more often for casters.

Order of magnitude, not a simulation: +2% critical strike chance is worth roughly +1 to +2% damage for a character whose attacks can almost all crit. We have not run these new values through our level-60 DPS simulator yet, so no numeric race ranking is given here.

Source: development notes for the October 15, 2026 beta build. Values are the beta's and can still change before the November 4 launch. Our race pages and simulator are not recomputed with these values yet.

Frequently asked questions

Which racials change in WoW Forever?

In the October 15, 2026 build: Tauren, Dwarf, Skyborne, Human, Orc, Undead, Gnome, Night Elf and Troll. Human, Orc and Dwarf go from a weapon specialization to a crit bonus, and Troll doubles its health regeneration.

Is Orc still a good pick for a Warrior?

Blood Fury goes to a 3 minute cooldown and Shatter Curse protects less, but Savage gives +1% crit with any weapon. The racial stays decent, just less dominant than before.
