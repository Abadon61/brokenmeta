WoW Forever: the October 27 racial tuning pass, race by race and simulated

By BrokenMeta.gg · October 28, 2026

On October 26 and 27, 2026 Blizzard published a racial tuning pass for WoW Forever ahead of the November 4 launch. The Human loses half its critical strike, the Orc and Dwarf swap racials, and the Tauren loses its critical damage. We ran these values through our level-60 simulator: the Human loses 0.4 to 1.1% DPS, and the best race changes for 12 of 23 specs, but the gaps stay small.

The Skyborne keep their two utility abilities, now described more precisely: Read Leyline (Alliance) restores 50% regeneration for 15 s, Skysight (Horde) gives 15% speed for 15 s. Both persist through death and are removed on entering a raid.

What it means for DPS, according to our simulator

We simulated the racials that change damage: Human crit and Spirit, Orc Blood Fury and Command, Dwarf Artisan's Precision, Troll Berserking, Skyborne Wind Blessed. We did not simulate Tauren health, Plainsrunning, Touch of the Grave, Read Leyline or Skysight, which do not change damage on a single target. With the same gear, the Human loses between 0.4% (Demonology) and 1.1% DPS (Fury), and Orc Shamans lose 0.3% (Elemental) to 0.6% (Enhancement) with a shorter Blood Fury but no Savage.

With 2% crit the Human came first for many specs; at 1% it no longer does. Here is where the best pick changes:

  • UP Orc becomes the best pick for Warlocks and two Hunter specs. The 5% bonus weighs on Warlock and Hunter pets, and Blood Fury is back to every 2 minutes.
  • NERF The Human no longer dominates. It stays within 1% of the best for most melee and caster specs, but it is no longer first for Hunters, Warlocks or Combat and Assassination Rogues.
  • BALANCE Unchanged: Gnome stays first for Fire and Arcane Mage (its lead over Human grows from 1.4 to 2.3% in Fire), Skyborne first for Frost Mage, Fury and Arms Warrior, Subtlety Rogue and Balance Druid, Human for Paladins, Orc for Elemental Shaman.
  • BALANCE The gaps are small. Between the best and the worst race of a spec there are 0.4 to 3.6% DPS. If a race appeals to you for its look or its lore, playing another one for 0.5% makes no sense.

Frequently asked questions

Which racials change in WoW Forever before launch?

Human (crit at 1%, Spirit at 10%), Orc (Savage removed, Command added, Blood Fury at 2 min), Dwarf (Earth Fury removed, +1% hit), Tauren (Endurance now gives health only), Troll (Berserking 15 s), Undead (Touch of the Grave cooldown 15 s) and Skyborne (2% haste).

Is Orc the best pick for a Warlock or a Hunter?

In our simulation, yes for all three Warlock specs and for Beast Mastery and Survival Hunter, with a 0.6 to 1.5% lead. Marksmanship Hunter prefers Night Elf. These are small gaps: another race costs only about 1% DPS.

Is the Human still good in WoW Forever?

Yes, but it no longer dominates. With the same gear it loses 0.4 to 1.1% DPS and stays the best pick for Paladins.
