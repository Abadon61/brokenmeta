WoW Forever: the 25-book library quest opens at level 30

By BrokenMeta.gg · October 6, 2026

In the WoW Forever beta, level 30 players can now hand in 25 library books for a bigger reward. It is a second "Greater Friend of the Library" quest: you pick between the Truthseeker's Bow, the Crest of Elucidation and the Researcher's Night Light. The last two require level 40, so the bow may also get a level lock later.

What it means for you

  • 25 books at level 30: a single choice between the Truthseeker's Bow, the Crest of Elucidation and the Researcher's Night Light.
  • Hunters: the bow is worth the trip, but it may end up with a level 40 requirement too.
  • The earlier tiers stay: 10 books (necklace) and 20 books (ring).
  • Hand books in to your faction's librarian: Garion Wendell in Stormwind (Alliance), Owen Thadd in Undercity (Horde).
