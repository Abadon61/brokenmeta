Method and limits
  • For each spec, the same character (gear, talents, consumables from the level 60 ranking) is simulated with every possible race: 180 seconds against a stationary level 63 boss, 20000 runs per race and per variant (in 5 batches for the error).
  • Racials come from the beta client tables (build 1.60.1.69913): Forever reworked those of WoW Classic. Weapon specializations were replaced by critical strike chance (Human, Orc) or critical damage (Dwarf), and several racials are new (Elune's Light, Wind Blessed...). The October 15 racial rework (build 1.60.1.70425) is included, and so are the changes Blizzard announced on October 26 and 27, 2026 for the launch (Ruthless, Human Spirit, Earth Fury and Savage removed, Endurance, Command, Artisan's Precision, Blood Fury, Berserking, Wind Blessed), which are not in the tables read yet and are applied from the announcement.
  • Simulated: Ruthless (Human, +1% crit), Artisan's Precision (Dwarf, +1% hit), Command (Orc, +5% damage of Warlock and Hunter pets), Blood Fury (2 min), Berserking (15 s) and Eureka! (Gnome, 8 s of +10% damage every 120 s: without the 10% cost cut) as temporary damage bonuses, Elune's Light, Wind Blessed (+2% haste), The Human Spirit (+10% Spirit), Expansive Mind (mana). Savage, Earth Fury and Endurance's critical damage no longer exist.
  • Not simulated: Eureka!'s 10% cost cut, Undead Touch of the Grave (a health drain, with a 15 s internal cooldown, worth a few DPS at most), bonuses against Beasts and Elementals (the simulated fight is against a single boss), and the Tauren's extra health.
  • Each race's base stat bonuses (strength, agility, etc.) are not in the tables read: they are the Classic values taken from the open-source simulator WoWSims (MIT licence, github.com/wowsims/classic), not confirmed for Forever; the Skyborne have none. A racial you press (Blood Fury, Elune's Light, Berserking) is used at the best moment among four possible starts (0.2 s, 20 s, 60 s, 120 s), as a player would line it up with their other cooldowns.

Rogue races and their racials

The descriptions come from the beta client. The label says whether the simulation accounts for them.

Human Alliance

  • Perception utility, no effect on DPS

    Dramatically increases stealth detection for 20 sec.

    Duration 20 s · Cooldown 180 s
  • Ruthless simulated

    Increases your critical strike chance with all spells and attacks by 1%.

  • The Human Spirit simulated

    Spirit increased by 10%.

  • Will to Survive utility, no effect on DPS

    Instantly removes all Stun effects.

    Cooldown 180 s

Orc Horde

  • Blood Fury simulated

    Increases Attack Power and Spell Power by 10% for 15 sec.

    Duration 15 s · Cooldown 120 s
  • Hardiness utility, no effect on DPS

    Duration of Stun effects on you reduced by 20%.

  • Shatter Curse utility, no effect on DPS

    Instantly removes all Curses and Banes, and reduces all Magical damage taken by 5% for 8 sec.

    Duration 8 s · Cooldown 180 s

Dwarf Alliance

  • Find Treasure utility, no effect on DPS
  • Stoneform utility, no effect on DPS

    Instantly removes and grants immunity to all Bleed, Poison, and Disease effects, and reduces all Physical damage taken by 10% for 8 sec.

    Duration 8 s · Cooldown 180 s
  • Big Game Hunter damage, not simulated

    Damage dealt versus Beasts increased by 5%.

  • Artisan's Precision simulated

    Increases your chance to hit with spells and attacks by 1%.

Night Elf Alliance

  • Shadowmeld utility, no effect on DPS

    Activate to slip into the shadows, reducing the chance for enemies to detect your presence. Lasts until cancelled or upon moving. Using this ability in combat discourages enemies from attacking you, but increases the cooldown to 2 min.

    Cooldown 10 s
  • Quickness utility, no effect on DPS

    Dodge chance increased by 1% and movement speed increased by 2%. Night Elf Rogues and Druids are harder to detect in Stealth as if they were 1 level higher.

  • Wisp Spirit utility, no effect on DPS

    Transform into a wisp upon death, increasing movement speed by 75%.

  • Elune's Light simulated

    Increases your critical strike chance with all spells and attacks by 10% for 15 sec.

    Duration 15 s · Cooldown 180 s

Undead Horde

  • Underwater Breathing utility, no effect on DPS

    Underwater breath lasts 300% longer than normal.

  • Will of the Forsaken utility, no effect on DPS

    Instantly removes all Charm, Fear and Sleep effects.

    Duration 0.1 s · Cooldown 120 s
  • Cannibalize utility, no effect on DPS

    When activated, regenerates 7% of total Health and 7% of total Mana every 2 sec for 10 sec. Only works on Humanoid or Undead corpses within 5 yds. Any movement, action, or damage taken while Cannibalizing will cancel the effect.

    Cooldown 120 s
  • Touch of the Grave damage, not simulated

    Your spells and attacks have a 20% chance to drain Health from the target, up to 5% of your maximum Health. Internal cooldown 15 sec.

Gnome Alliance

  • Escape Artist utility, no effect on DPS

    Instantly escape the effects of any movement impairing effect and gain immunity to those effects for 3 sec.

    Duration 3 s · Cooldown 120 s
  • Engineering Specialization utility, no effect on DPS

    Your gnomish ingenuity reduces the rate of engineering devices failing or backfiring when you use them by 20%.

  • Expansive Mind damage, not simulated

    Maximum Energy increased by 5%.

  • Eureka! simulated

    Your non-periodic damaging abilities have their Energy cost reduced by 10% and deal 10% more damage.

    Duration 8 s · Cooldown 120 s

Troll Horde

  • Berserking simulated Duration 15 s · Cooldown 180 s
  • Regeneration utility, no effect on DPS

    Health regeneration rate increased by 20%. In addition, 20% of total Health regeneration will continue during combat.

  • Beast Slaying damage, not simulated

    Damage dealt versus Beasts increased by 5%.

  • Rapid Regeneration utility, no effect on DPS

    Regenerate 50% of your maximum Health over 6 sec. Any movement, action, or damage taken will cancel the effect.

    Duration 6 s · Cooldown 180 s

High Order Skyborne Alliance

  • Read Leyline utility, no effect on DPS

    Tap into the power of a nearby leyline, increasing your Health and Mana regeneration by 50% for 15 sec. It cannot be used while Innervate is active, and Innervate replaces it as the stronger effect. Used at a Ley Line, it also grants Attuned for 1 hour: while Attuned, Read Leyline increases regeneration by 100%. Attuned persists through death and is removed upon entering a raid.

    Cooldown 120 s
  • Walk on Air utility, no effect on DPS

    Glide downward through the air for 10 sec while controlling your direction of travel.

    Duration 10 s · Cooldown 90 s
  • Elemental Insight damage, not simulated

    Damage dealt versus Elementals increased by 5%.

  • Wind Blessed simulated

    Increases your spellcasting, melee, and ranged Haste by 2%.

Windshaper Skyborne Horde

  • Walk on Air utility, no effect on DPS

    Glide downward through the air for 10 sec while controlling your direction of travel.

    Duration 10 s · Cooldown 90 s
  • Elemental Insight damage, not simulated

    Damage dealt versus Elementals increased by 5%.

  • Wind Blessed simulated

    Increases your spellcasting, melee, and ranged Haste by 2%.

  • Skysight utility, no effect on DPS

Used at an elemental convergence, it also grants Charged for 1 hour: while Charged, Skysight increases movement speed by 30%. Charged persists through death and is removed upon entering a raid.

    Duration 15 s · Cooldown 120 s

Frequently asked questions

What is the best race for a Combat Rogue in WoW: Forever?

In our simulation, High Order Skyborne / Windshaper Skyborne deals the most DPS as a Combat Rogue with its racials; the lowest-placed race, Undead, is about 1.3% behind. These gaps are small: play the race you prefer unless you chase the last percent.

Can I pick any race?

No: the race and class combinations come from the beta client. Alliance and Horde races do not mix, and the Skyborne have their own classes.
