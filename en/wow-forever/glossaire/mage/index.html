What's still missing

This class isn't complete yet: here's precisely what hasn't been sourced yet, rather than leaving it silent.

  • Only the Fire tree's single-target rotation pieces are covered (Fireball, Fire Blast, Pyroblast, Wake of Fire, Ignite, Improved Fireball). Utility/defensive/AoE spells named in Icy Veins' guide (Frostbolt, Polymorph, Counterspell, Frost Nova, Blast Wave, Arcane Explosion, Flamestrike, Scorch, Improved Scorch, armors, wards) are not looked up.
  • Frostfire Bolt (replaces Fireball at level 40) not looked up -- out of scope for a level-20 model anyway.
  • Wand damage: Icy Veins repeatedly says wand damage is very strong at low levels and should be used over spells when mana-inefficient -- not sourced, and not modeled by anything in this project yet.
  • No real armor-mitigation-equivalent needed here (casters ignore armor), but spell resistance / partial resists on Fire damage against resistant targets isn't sourced either.
  • Real, sourced finding (2026-09-25): Arcane and Frost's own level-20 guides both describe a rotation that ISN'T actually usable yet at the beta's level-20 cap. Arcane's whole single-target priority depends on Missile Barrage, which its own guide text says "will only be available from Level 24 on" -- mentioned early so players learn the concept, not because it's usable at 20. Frost's headline single-target spell, Ice Lance (wowhead_spell_id 400640), requires character level 28 (confirmed on its own tooltip) despite the guide already investing all 11 points toward it (Frostbite/Ice Shards exist specifically to buff it) -- the level-20 payoff is entirely in Frostbolt's chill uptime and future-proofing, not a distinct level-20 nuke. Both specs' real, actually-castable level-20 damage kit therefore reduces to Frostbolt (shared with every Mage spec, not Fire-tree-exclusive) plus unsourced wand damage -- a real limitation of the level-20 cap, not a gap in this research pass.
  • Frostbolt (below) uses Rank 3's real tooltip (Requires level 14, confirmed via Wowhead's own trainer list). Community rank-ladder data (wow-forever.gg) confirms a Rank 4 exists at exactly the level-20 cap, but its own individual tooltip wasn't re-fetched this pass -- Rank 3's real, sourced values are used as a close, disclosed approximation rather than guessing Rank 4's numbers.
