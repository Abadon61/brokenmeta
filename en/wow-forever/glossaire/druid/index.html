What's still missing

This class isn't complete yet: here's precisely what hasn't been sourced yet, rather than leaving it silent.

  • Starfire, Insect Swarm, Moonkin Form and the full Feral/Restoration trees not looked up.
  • Talents' exact numeric effects not looked up (only names/point allocation confirmed from the real build): Improved Wrath (cast time + Mana cost reduction, per Icy Veins' text), Moonglow, Nature's Reach (hit chance + range, per Icy Veins' text), Improved Moonfire.
  • Real level-20 build note: the guide explicitly frames the last point (Improved Moonfire, taken as 1/2 rather than maxed) as "extremely flexible" -- i.e. not a firmly fixed pick the way the other 10 points are. Worth remembering this build is slightly softer at the margin than Warrior/Mage/Rogue/Priest's fully-determined 11-point builds.
  • Note on "resource_system" above: it's "mana" for Balance specifically. Feral (added 2026-09-25, see talents.feral and the Claw/Rip/Maul abilities below) uses Energy in Cat Form and Rage in Bear Form -- this project's schema declares one resource per file, so each Feral ability's own real resource_cost key (energy/rage) is what actually matters, not this top-level field.
  • Feral (2026-09-25): real, sourced finding -- Wowhead's own "Level 20 Feral Druid DPS" guide calls the level-20 kit "fairly lackluster... only having two damage abilities in Claw and Rip", confirming Icy Veins' own simpler framing. Cat Form's real rotation: Claw (builder) until 4+ combo points on a target expected to live >=6s, then Rip (finisher) -- this exact 4-CP threshold (not 5) is Wowhead's own guide text, more precise than Icy Veins' generic "5 combo points". Bear Form's real rotation only sources Maul (a next-swing damage enhancer, real classic mechanic) -- Enrage (Rage generator) and Swipe (AoE) are real but not sourced this pass (Enrage's own numbers weren't pinned down; Swipe is an AoE tool, out of scope for single-target). Growl (taunt) and Demoralizing Roar are real Bear tools with no simulated-damage relevance.
