Combos and how to play

Written guide · based on the official ability texts

Zaahen is a fighter who gains power with every hit: his attacks and abilities give Determination stacks with bonus attack damage, and at max he can come back to life. The Darkin Glaive slashes twice and can be recast to knock up, Dreaded Return pulls in, Aureate Rush dashes him with a slash, and Grim Deliverance brings him down in a dive.

The trade
Q › AA AA › Q › AA AA

When: An enemy is within range.

The Darkin Glaive slashes twice with bonus damage and a heal; recast it to knock the target into the air.

The engage
W › E › Q › AA AA

When: An enemy is far away.

Dreaded Return pulls enemies toward you, Aureate Rush dashes you with a slash around you.

The ultimate
R › Q › E

When: A group is close.

Grim Deliverance brings you down in a dive, damaging enemies and healing you for part of the damage dealt.

Laning phase

Hit champions to stack Determination. The Darkin Glaive heals you and can be recast to knock the target into the air.

Common mistakes

  • Using the ultimate without having stacked Determination.
  • Casting Dreaded Return into nothing.
  • Forgetting to recast The Darkin Glaive.
