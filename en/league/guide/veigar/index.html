Combos and how to play

Written guide · based on the official ability texts

Veigar is a mage who gains permanent power: hitting a champion with an ability, killing a champion or assisting gives him power. Baleful Strike, on units it kills, gives some too. Event Horizon creates a cage that stuns, and Primordial Burst deals damage that increases with the target's missing health.

Laning phase

Baleful Strike hits two enemies: kill minions with it to gain power. Dark Matter has its cooldown reduced by your power stacks.

Common mistakes

  • Casting the ultimate on a full-health enemy.
  • Neglecting farm with Baleful Strike: it is your power.
  • Placing Event Horizon where the enemies will not pass.

Riot's official tips for playing Veigar

  • Use Event Horizon to increase your chances of landing Dark Matter.
  • Veigar is extremely Mana and Cooldown Reduction dependent. Try buying items with these stats in order to increase the effectiveness of your passive and Baleful Strike.
  • Veigar is very fragile. It is valuable to select at least one summoner spell that can be used defensively.

How to play against Veigar

  • Dark Matter deals very high damage, but it can be avoided. Pay attention to the sound and visual indicator to be aware of when and where the spell will land.
  • Event Horizon only stuns units on the edge. If you're inside the spell, you can still move and attack.
  • Veigar's ultimate deals increased damage based on your missing health.
