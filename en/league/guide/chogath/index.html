Cho'Gath, the Terror of the Void, is a tank and mage champion of medium difficulty. Across our 980 analysed ranked games, Cho'Gath is mostly played in the Jungle (56% of its games), and more rarely in Top (35%), with a 50.1% win rate in that role: tier A among Jungle champions (#11 of 48, ranked by win rate). Cho'Gath's damage is mixed (20% physical, 50% magic): stacking a single type of resistance is less efficient against this champion. 30% of that damage is true damage and therefore ignores resistances. Win rate varies with game length: 55.7% in long games (over 35 min, 70 games) versus 39.9% in short games (under 25 min).

Combos and how to play

Written guide · based on the official ability texts

Cho'Gath is a tank-mage who grows: he regains health and mana for each unit killed, and Feast makes him grow by increasing his max health if it kills its target. Rupture knocks enemies into the air, Feral Scream silences in a cone and Vorpal Spikes slow.

The control combo
Q › W › AA AA

When: An enemy is within Rupture range.

Rupture knocks up and slows, Feral Scream silences in the cone, your attacks fire spikes that slow.

The execution
Q › R

When: An enemy is low.

Feast devours an enemy unit for heavy true damage; if the target is killed, you grow and gain max health.

The control zone
E › AA AA › Q

When: Several enemies are in front of you.

Your attacks with Vorpal Spikes damage and slow every enemy in front of you.

Laning phase

Take kills to heal and regain mana. Rupture is your main control tool: place it where your enemy is going.

Common mistakes

  • Using Feast on a target you cannot kill: its bonus depends on the kill.
  • Missing Rupture: it is your entry point.
  • Neglecting farm: your power depends on kills.

Riot's official tips for playing Cho'Gath

  • Try to line up your attacks with Vorpal Spikes so that they kill minions and harass enemy champions at the same time.
  • If you're having difficulty feasting upon champions, try eating minions until you're larger.
  • Using Rupture on creeps in combination with Carnivore is a good way to gain Health and Mana.

How to play against Cho'Gath

  • Purchasing a few HP items decreases the chances of Cho'Gath killing you quickly.
  • Focus on preventing Cho'Gath from reaching his max size.
  • Rupture has a smoke cloud indicating the area it will strike. Try to watch out for it to prevent Cho'Gath from being able to combo his abilities.
