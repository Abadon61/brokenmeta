Combos and how to play

Written guide · based on the official ability texts

Singed is a tank-mage who leaves a poison trail behind him and gains speed when passing champions. Mega Adhesive slows and grounds, Fling knocks the enemy behind him (rooted if they land in the adhesive), and Insanity Potion improves his stats and makes his trail apply Grievous Wounds.

The capture
W › E

When: An enemy is within Fling range.

Mega Adhesive slows and grounds the enemies in the area; Fling knocks the enemy behind you: if they land in the adhesive, they are rooted.

The chase
R › Q › AA AA

When: You are chasing a carry.

Insanity Potion improves your stats and your trail applies Grievous Wounds.

The escape
Q › W

When: An enemy is chasing you.

Your poison trail damages those following you and Mega Adhesive slows them.

Laning phase

Your poison trail damages the enemies who follow it. Fling on a target who lands in Mega Adhesive roots them.

Common mistakes

  • Chasing without using your trail.
  • Casting Mega Adhesive without an enemy trajectory.
  • Using Insanity Potion too early: it has a limited duration.

Riot's official tips for playing Singed

  • Poison Trail is very effective at farming and harassing, allowing Singed to dominate the flow of whatever lane he's in.
  • Use Insanity Potion to trick your opponents into chasing you through your Poison Trail.
  • Flinging enemies into your tower can deal heavy amounts of damage to them.

Counters and matchups

How to play against Singed

  • Keep your distance to avoid being Flung back into Singed's allies.
  • Singed needs to run close to your team to be effective. Try to take advantage of this by using crowd control effects on him while attacking his allies.
  • Be careful when chasing Singed. He's very difficult to bring down, and can leave his Poison Trail to damage you in your pursuit.
