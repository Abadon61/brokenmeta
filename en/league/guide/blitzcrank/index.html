Combos and how to play

Written guide · based on the official ability texts

Blitzcrank is a support-tank who wins by making picks: Rocket Grab catches an enemy and pulls them in, then Power Fist doubles the damage of his next attack and knocks the target up. His passive gives him a shield at low health based on his mana, Overdrive boosts his attack and movement speed, and his ultimate damages and silences.

The pick
Q › E › AA AA

When: An enemy is isolated on the trajectory.

Rocket Grab pulls them to you while damaging them, Power Fist doubles the damage of your attack and knocks them into the air.

The chase
W › AA AA › E

When: You need to catch up to a target.

Overdrive greatly boosts your attack and movement speed but slows you at the end: only use it if the chase is decisive.

The area ultimate
R › Q

When: Enemies are in melee range.

Static Field marks the enemies you attack (lightning after 1 second); activate it to destroy nearby shields, damage and silence.

Laning phase

The grab is your lane ability: every hit decides the trade. Keep some mana: your Mana Barrier depends on it.

Common mistakes

  • Throwing the grab blind: its cooldown is long.
  • Using Overdrive without being able to take advantage of the speed, since you are slowed afterward.
  • Forgetting your barrier: keep some mana when you are at low health.

Riot's official tips for playing Blitzcrank

  • The 1-2-3 combo of Rocket Grab, Power Fist, and Static Field can devastate an individual opponent.
  • Using Blitzcrank's grab to pull an enemy into your tower range followed by a Power Fist will allow the tower to get several hits on them.

How to play against Blitzcrank

  • Blitzcrank's passive Mana Barrier grants him a shield when he is low on health.
  • Staying behind creeps can prevent you from being Rocket Grabbed. Blitzcrank's Rocket Grab only pulls the first enemy target it encounters.
