Combos and how to play

Written guide · based on the official ability texts

Thresh is an engage and protection support. Death Sentence pulls an enemy toward you (or you toward them), Dark Passage protects nearby allies with a lantern they can use to dash toward him, and The Box traps enemies with walls that slow and damage. His passive collects souls that permanently increase his armor and power.

The hook and the flay
Q › E › AA AA

When: An enemy is hit by Death Sentence.

Death Sentence binds and pulls the enemy; Flay sends them in the direction of the hit (toward your allies or the turret). Your charged attacks deal more damage if you wait between them.

The team-fight engage
Q › Q › R

When: The enemy carry is isolated.

A second activation of Death Sentence pulls you toward the enemy, which positions you to drop The Box around them and their allies. Its walls slow and damage those who break them.

The rescue
W › E

When: Your carry is being dived.

Dark Passage shields nearby allies from damage, and they can click the lantern to dash to you; Flay pushes the aggressors away.

Laning phase

Death Sentence is your main ability: place it carefully, since it decides your lane. Drop the lantern between your carry and the danger, then use Flay to push an enemy away or bring them back under your turret.

Common mistakes

  • Using Death Sentence without checking the minions: they block the path.
  • Dropping the lantern too far from your allies: they can only use it if they reach it.
  • Casting The Box when your team is not ready to take advantage of it.

Riot's official tips for playing Thresh

  • Communication is key when making use of Thresh's lantern. Let your teammates know how you like to use it.
  • Death Sentence and Flay can be combined in either cast order for powerful combinations.
  • Thresh can collect souls without needing to kill units himself. Planning your map position to be near the most deaths will help to maximize soul collection.

How to play against Thresh

  • Thresh's Death Sentence has a long cast time. Watch for the cast to begin to take evasive actions.
  • Intentionally breaking a wall of The Box can allow a vulnerable ally to escape unscathed.
  • Thresh relies on collecting souls for a portion of his defense and damage. Try punishing him when he moves to collect them.
