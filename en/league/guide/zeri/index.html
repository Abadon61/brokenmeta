Combos and how to play

Written guide · based on the official ability texts

Zeri is a marksman whose attacks deal magic damage and are treated as abilities. Moving and casting Burst Fire stores energy in her ionic pack; when fully charged, her next attack deals bonus damage. Her ultimate overcharges her: she gains attack speed and Burst Fire becomes a faster triple shot.

Harassment
Q › W › Q

When: An enemy is within range.

Burst Fire fires 7 bullets that deal your attack damage to the first enemy hit; Ultrashock Laser slows, then fire Burst Fire again.

The piercing dash
E › Q › Q

When: You want to engage or flee.

Spark Surge dashes you, empowers Burst Fire so it pierces, and makes you leap over obstacles.

The overcharge
R › Q › Q

When: A team fight starts.

Lightning Crash triggers a nova and overcharges you: a stacking attack speed bonus on each champion hit and Burst Fire becomes a triple shot with a chain of lightning.

Laning phase

Burst Fire has no cooldown: fire it often while moving to charge your pack. Ultrashock Laser slows and damages; if it hits a wall, it becomes a long-range laser.

Common mistakes

  • Not moving while you shoot: your pack charges while you move.
  • Using the ultimate alone: you need to hit champions to stack attack speed.
  • Casting Spark Surge with no exit plan.
