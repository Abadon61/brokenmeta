Combos and how to play

Written guide · based on the official ability texts

Zac is an elastic tank: every ability that hits makes him lose a bit of himself that he absorbs to heal, and on death he splits into 4 blobs that try to recombine. Stretching Strikes grabs and slams two targets, Unstable Matter damages based on max health, Elastic Slingshot launches him, and Let's Bounce! bounces four times while knocking up.

The engage
E › Q › W › R

When: An enemy carry is within range.

Elastic Slingshot launches you forward, Stretching Strikes grabs, Unstable Matter deals damage based on max health, Let's Bounce! knocks up and slows.

The trade
Q › AA AA › W

When: Two enemies are close to each other.

Stretching Strikes grabs an enemy then, if you attack another one, sends the two targets into each other.

The second life
W

When: You take lethal damage.

Cell Division splits you into 4 blobs that try to recombine; if any remain, you come back to life. This ability has a 5-minute cooldown.

Laning phase

Pick up your bits to heal. Stretching Strikes grabs an enemy then, if you attack another one, sends the two targets into each other.

Common mistakes

  • Leaving the bits on the ground: they are your healing.
  • Forgetting that the second life is available every 5 minutes.
  • Using Elastic Slingshot with no direction: it is a movement ability.

Riot's official tips for playing Zac

  • Picking up goo chunks is very important to staying alive.
  • When Cell Division is ready, try to die in a position that makes it difficult for the enemy team to kill your bloblets.
  • Charging Elastic Slingshot from the fog of war will give opponents less time to react.

Counters and matchups

How to play against Zac

  • Zac heals from the goo that separates from him. You can crush the goo pieces by stepping on them.
  • Kill all of Zac's bloblets when he splits apart to stop him from reforming.
  • Silences, stuns, roots and knockups will all interrupt Zac when he is charging Elastic Slingshot.

Abilities

PassiveCell Division
Each time Zac hits an enemy with an ability, he sheds a chunk of himself that can be reabsorbed to restore Health. Upon taking fatal damage, Zac splits into 4 bloblets that attempt to recombine. If any bloblets remain, he will revive with an amount of Health depending on the Health of the surviving bloblets. Each bloblet has a percentage of Zac's maximum Health, Armor and Magic Resistance. This ability has a 5 minute cooldown.
QStretching Strikes
Zac stretches an arm, grabbing an enemy. Attacking a different enemy will cause him to throw both targets towards each other.
WUnstable Matter
Zac explodes outward towards nearby enemies, dealing a percentage of their maximum health as magic damage.
EElastic Slingshot
Zac attaches his arms to the ground and stretches back, launching himself forward.
RLet's Bounce!
Zac bounces four times, knocking up enemies hit and slowing them.
