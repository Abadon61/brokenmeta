Combos and how to play

Written guide · based on the official ability texts

Tristana is an execution marksman: her attack range grows with her levels, and Rocket Jump lets her dive or escape. Explosive Charge places a bomb on a target or explodes on every takedown, letting her chain kills in team fights. Her ultimate knocks the target back and doubles the charge's blast radius.

The execution
W › E › Q › AA AA

When: An enemy is isolated or weakened.

Rocket Jump brings you closer and slows, Explosive Charge places a bomb on the target, Rapid Fire boosts your attack speed to finish.

The defensive ultimate
R

When: An enemy dives you.

Buster Shot deals magic damage and knocks the target back; if they carry an Explosive Charge, the blast radius is doubled, which also hurts their nearby allies.

The kill chain
E › AA AA › AA AA › Q

When: A team fight is underway.

Every takedown makes your projectiles explode around the victim: position yourself to hit several enemies at once.

Laning phase

Your range grows with your levels: play carefully early, then punish from a distance as soon as you outrange them. Keep Rocket Jump to escape or finish, since its cooldown is long.

Common mistakes

  • Diving with Rocket Jump without an escape: its cooldown is long.
  • Using the ultimate offensively when it knocks the target back: it is also a defensive tool.
  • Forgetting that your range grows with levels: do not play like you are level 1.

Riot's official tips for playing Tristana

  • Her massive gun allows Tristana to fire on targets at a great distance. Utilize this to prevent your enemies from ever laying a hand on you.
  • Use Rocket Jump after you have stacked up your Explosive Charge on an enemy to finish them off with a burst of damage.
  • Use Rapid Fire to help stack up your Explosive Charge on enemy champions.

How to play against Tristana

  • If you see Tristana activate Rapid Fire in a fight, stun her and try to back off until the spell dissipates.
  • Stand away from your creeps in a lane to take less collateral damage from Explosive Charge.
