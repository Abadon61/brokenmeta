Combos and how to play

Written guide · based on the official ability texts

Rakan is a mobile engage support: Grand Entrance dashes him in and knocks enemies up, Battle Dance flies him to an ally while giving them a shield, and his ultimate makes him faster and charms the enemies it hits. His passive periodically gives him a shield.

The engage
W › R › Q

When: An enemy carry is within range.

Grand Entrance dashes you in, knocking up nearby enemies, The Quickness raises your speed and charms the enemies it hits, Gleaming Quill damages.

The rescue
E › E

When: An ally is in danger.

Battle Dance flies you to an allied champion, giving them a shield; recast it at no cost to extend the movement.

Harassment
Q › AA AA

When: An enemy is within range.

Gleaming Quill deals magic damage and heals your allies if it hits a champion.

Laning phase

Gleaming Quill heals your allies if it hits a champion or an epic monster. Battle Dance can be recast at no cost for a short time: chain it to reposition.

Common mistakes

  • Engaging with no ally nearby to follow up.
  • Wasting Grand Entrance with no target.
  • Forgetting to recast Battle Dance.

Riot's official tips for playing Rakan

  • Rakan requires allies near him to make the most out of his tools.
  • Rakan's dash speeds increase with his Move Speed. Use the extra speed to surprise your enemies!
  • Danger is as fun as you let it be.

How to play against Rakan

  • Rakan's movement abilities forecast their destination. Try to use this to your advantage.
  • Champions with crowd control that can be applied quickly excel against Rakan.
  • Catching Rakan with no allies around severely inhibits his mobility. Try to find him alone.
