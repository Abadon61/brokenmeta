Combos and how to play

Written guide · based on the official ability texts

Neeko is a chameleon mage-support: her passive lets her take on the appearance of an ally or a unit, until she is immobilized, casts an offensive ability or takes damage. Blooming Burst blooms twice, Shapesplitter sends a clone, Tangle-Barbs roots, and Pop Blossom knocks up then stuns.

Neeko's strengths

  • Her disguise lets her approach without being identified, and the wind-up of her ultimate is invisible while disguised.
  • Tangle-Barbs roots several enemies and grows if it passes through a champion or kills.
  • Pop Blossom knocks up then stuns every nearby enemy.

Neeko's weaknesses

  • The disguise breaks if she is immobilized, casts an offensive ability or takes enough damage.
  • Her abilities have long cooldowns (Shapesplitter 16 s, Pop Blossom 120 s).
  • Fragile: without her engage, she has no escape.

Role in team fights

Neeko approaches disguised, casts her ultimate to knock up and stun every nearby enemy, then roots the runners with Tangle-Barbs. A single successful engage can decide the fight, but she gets no second chance if the ultimate is missed.

Pop Blossom: the wind-up is invisible if you are disguised, you leap knocking up and on landing you damage and stun; Tangle-Barbs then roots.

Harassment

When: An enemy is within range.

Blooming Burst damages and blooms a second time; every 3 attacks, Shapesplitter deals bonus damage and speeds you up.

The root
E › Q

When: An enemy is isolated.

Tangle-Barbs roots every enemy it crosses; if it kills or passes through a champion, it grows and the root lasts longer.

The disguised trap
R › E › Q › AA AA

When: You are disguised as an ally and the enemies are grouped.

Pop Blossom: its wind-up cannot be seen when disguised; nearby enemies are knocked up then damaged and stunned on landing; follow with Tangle-Barbs and Blooming Burst.

Laning phase

Disguised, you can surprise: the wind-up of your ultimate cannot be seen if you are disguised. Blooming Burst blooms again if it hits a champion or kills.

Common mistakes

  • Breaking your disguise before the engage.
  • Casting Tangle-Barbs with no trajectory.
  • Forgetting the clone's recast.

Riot's official tips for playing Neeko

  • You can set her passive to use hotkeys in the options menu. The default is Shift+F1~F5
  • Try to use Inherent Glamour sparingly, ineffective uses will put enemies on the alert.

How to play against Neeko

  • Pop Blossom's warning visuals are invisible if Neeko is disguised.

Abilities

PassiveInherent Glamour
Neeko can look like an ally champion or as other units on the map. Receiving immobilizing crowd control, casting damaging spells, damaging enemy towers as a non-champion, or your disguise taking damage equal to its healthbar breaks the disguise.
QBlooming Burst
Neeko throws a seed dealing magic damage. The seed blooms again on hitting champions or killing units.
WShapesplitter
Neeko passively deals bonus magic damage every third attack, briefly increasing her move speed. Neeko can activate to send a clone in a direction and recast to change the direction of the clone.
ETangle-Barbs
Neeko slings a tangle that damages and roots everything it passes through. If the tangle kills an enemy or passes through a champion, it becomes larger, faster, and roots for longer.
RPop Blossom
After a short preparation, Neeko leaps into the air, knocking up all nearby enemies. Upon landing, nearby enemies are damaged and stunned. The preparation is hidden if Neeko is disguised.
