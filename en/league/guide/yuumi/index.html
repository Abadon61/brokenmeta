Combos and how to play

Written guide · based on the official ability texts

Yuumi is a support who attaches to her allies: You and Me! dashes her to an ally, and in that state only turrets can target her. Prowling Projectile slows (more if the projectile flies at least 1.35 seconds), Zoomies only benefits the ally when she is attached, and Final Chapter heals and damages.

Harassment
Q › W › E

When: An enemy is within projectile range.

Prowling Projectile slows and damages (stronger if the projectile flew 1.35 s); while attached, you can steer it with your cursor; Zoomies gives your ally a shield and speeds.

Protection
W › E › R

When: Your carry is threatened.

You and Me! puts you safely on your ally, Zoomies protects them, and Final Chapter heals your allies while channeling.

The team fight
R › W › E

When: A fight begins.

Final Chapter channels five waves that damage enemies and heal allies; during it, you can move, attach and cast Zoomies.

Laning phase

Attach to your carry to protect them. Your passive heals you and heals your next ally when you hit a champion.

Common mistakes

  • Detaching in a dangerous area: you are vulnerable when not attached.
  • Casting Prowling Projectile with no direction.
  • Forgetting to strengthen the bond with your best friend.

Abilities

PassiveFeline Friendship
Periodically, when Yuumi strikes a champion with an attack or ability, she restores health to herself and the next ally she Attaches to.

While Attached, Yuumi generates a special bond with her allies. The ally with the strongest bond enhances Yuumi's abilities while she is Attached to them.
QProwling Projectile
Yuumi fires a missile, dealing damage and slowing first target hit. It deals bonus damage and an enchanced slow if it takes at least 1.35 seconds to get to its target. While on her Best Friend, the slow is always enhanced and grants bonus On-Hit damage to her ally.

While Attached, the missile can be controlled with your cursor for a brief period.
WYou and Me!
Yuumi dashes to a target ally, becoming untargetable from everything except turrets. While on her Best Friend, she gains Heal & Shield Power and grants her Ally On-Hit healing.
EZoomies
Shields Yuumi and boosts Move Speed and Attack Speed. If she's attached, she passes it to her ally instead.
RFinal Chapter
Yuumi channels five waves that damage enemies and heal allies. Yuumi can move, attach, and cast Zoomies! while channeling. While on her Best Friend, this spell also follows her mouse.
