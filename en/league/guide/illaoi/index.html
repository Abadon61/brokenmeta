Combos and how to play

Written guide · based on the official ability texts

Illaoi is a tentacle fighter: she and the vessels she creates make tentacles appear on nearby impassable terrain; they deal physical damage and heal her if they hurt a champion. Test of Spirit rips out an enemy's spirit and turns them into a vessel, and Leap of Faith summons one tentacle per champion hit.

The trade

When: An enemy is near terrain features.

Test of Spirit rips out the enemy's spirit: if they move too far away or the spirit is destroyed, they become a vessel and make tentacles appear; Harsh Lesson leaps you in and pushes the nearby tentacles to strike the target.

The area ultimate
R › Q › W

When: Several enemies are close.

Leap of Faith slams your idol, deals physical damage and summons a tentacle per enemy champion hit.

Harassment
Q › AA AA

When: An enemy is in lane.

Tentacle Smash deals physical damage and amplifies the tentacles; they heal you if they hurt a champion.

Laning phase

Play near terrain features: that is where your tentacles appear. Tentacle Smash increases the tentacles' damage and makes one slam down when activated.

Common mistakes

  • Fighting far from impassable terrain features.
  • Casting the ultimate without several targets.
  • Forgetting that the spirit passes part of the damage to the original target.

Riot's official tips for playing Illaoi

  • Tentacles are an immense source of power. Don't fight without them.
  • Spirits inherit their target's current health. If making a
  • Vessel
  • is your goal, try whittling your opponent's health down a bit first to make killing the spirit easier.
  • Leap of Faith is best used to follow-up a strong engage or when being engaged upon. Be careful about being the first one into a fight.

Counters and matchups

How to play against Illaoi

  • Killing Tentacles whenever possible will make it much easier to win a fight against Illaoi.
  • When your spirit gets pulled out, fight back if you can. Dealing damage to Illaoi reduces the spirit's duration.
  • Avoid grouping up for Leap of Faith to reduce the number of Tentacles available to Illaoi.

Abilities

PassiveProphet of an Elder God
Illaoi and the Vessels she creates spawn Tentacles on nearby impassible terrain. Tentacles swing at spirits, Vessels, and victims of Illaoi's Harsh lesson. Tentacles deal physical damage to enemies hit, and will heal Illaoi if they damage a champion.
QTentacle Smash
Increases the damage dealt by Tentacles. When activated, Illaoi smashes down a Tentacle that deals physical damage.
WHarsh Lesson
Illaoi leaps to her target, dealing physical damage and causing nearby Tentacles to also swing at the target.
ETest of Spirit
Illaoi rips the spirit from a foe's body, forcing it to stand before her. Spirits echo a percentage of the damage they take to the original target. If killed, or if the target gets too far from the spirit, the target will become a Vessel and begin spawning Tentacles.
RLeap of Faith
Illaoi smashes her idol into the ground, dealing physical damage to nearby enemies. A Tentacle spawns for each enemy champion hit.
