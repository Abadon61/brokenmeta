Combos and how to play

Written guide · based on the official ability texts

Sejuani is an ice jungler-tank: out of combat she gains Frost Armor (armor, magic resistance, immunity to slows), and she can shatter a stunned enemy for huge magic damage. Arctic Assault knocks up, Winter's Wrath applies Frost stacks, Permafrost stuns a champion at max Frost, and Glacial Prison freezes the first champion hit.

The engage
Q › W › E › AA AA

When: An enemy is within range.

Arctic Assault knocks up and stops on a champion; Winter's Wrath damages, slows and applies Frost; Permafrost stuns a champion at max stacks; your attack shatters the stunned enemy.

The capture ultimate
R › W › E

When: A carry is visible.

Glacial Prison freezes and stuns the first champion hit with bolas and creates an ice storm that slows the other enemies.

The approach
Q › W

When: You want to open a fight.

Arctic Assault knocks enemies into the air, Winter's Wrath slows them with two mace strikes.

Laning phase

Stack Frost with Winter's Wrath then freeze with Permafrost. Take advantage of Frost Armor out of combat to engage.

Common mistakes

  • Using Permafrost without max Frost.
  • Casting the ultimate with no ally to take advantage of the freeze.
  • Forgetting to shatter the stunned enemy.

Riot's official tips for playing Sejuani

  • Use Arctic Assault to escape bad situations, interupt channeled spells or chase runners.
  • Melee allies can stack Frost for your Permafrost, keep an eye on who they are attacking.
  • Save Glacial Prison for encounters where you have teammates present to take advantage of it.

Counters and matchups

How to play against Sejuani

  • Dodging Arctic Assault and Glacial Prison can give you a huge advantage.
  • Focus on avoiding the second swing of Winter's Wrath which carries the brunt of the damage.
  • Melee enemies can also stack Frost for Sejuani, be very careful around them.
