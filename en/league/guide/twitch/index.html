Combos and how to play

Written guide · based on the official ability texts

Twitch is a venom marksman-assassin: his attacks infect the target and deal true damage every second. Ambush camouflages him with a speed bonus, Venom Cask slows and applies poison, Contaminate makes it worse, and Spray and Pray fires bolts that pierce all enemies.

The ambush
Q › AA AA › AA AA › E

When: A carry is within range.

Ambush camouflages you and raises your speed; on leaving it, your attack speed rises: strike then Contaminate worsens the poison.

Harassment
W › AA AA › AA AA

When: An enemy is within range.

Venom Cask explodes in an area, slows and applies Deadly Venom; your attacks reinforce the poison.

The line ultimate
R › AA AA › AA AA › AA AA

When: Enemies are lined up.

Spray and Pray fires long-range bolts that pierce all enemies hit: line them up.

Laning phase

Poison your opponent with attacks then use Contaminate to make it worse. When you leave camouflage, your attack speed briefly rises.

Common mistakes

  • Using Contaminate with no poison.
  • Casting the ultimate without lining up the enemies.
  • Leaving camouflage with no target: you lose your window.

Riot's official tips for playing Twitch

  • Twitch's Attack Speed is among the highest in the game; try purchasing items with on-hit effects such as Black Cleaver or Wit's End.
  • Contaminate has a large range; apply as many stacks of Deadly Venom as possible before using it.
  • You can catch up to an enemy out of attack range with Venom Cask.

How to play against Twitch

  • Twitch is fragile. Work with teammates to focus on him when you're able to catch him out of Camouflage.
  • Spell Shields won't block the damage from Deadly Venom, but they will block the effects Twitch can trigger with it.
  • If you suspect Twitch has left the lane, let your teammates know that Twitch is ''MIA.''
