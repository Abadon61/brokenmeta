Combos and how to play

Written guide · based on the official ability texts

Poppy is an anti-mobility tank: Steadfast Presence stops enemy dashes around her (the enemy is slowed and grounded), Heroic Charge knocks back and stuns against a wall, and Keeper's Verdict knocks enemies very far away. Her passive throws her shield, which she picks up for a temporary shield.

The wall stun
E › Q › AA AA

When: An enemy is near a wall.

Heroic Charge knocks the target back; against a wall they are stunned. Hammer Shock slows then explodes.

Anti-dive
W › R

When: An enemy dashes toward your carry.

Steadfast Presence stops dashes around you: the enemy is slowed and grounded. Keeper's Verdict then knocks the enemy very far away.

Harassment
Q › AA AA › AA AA

When: An enemy is in lane.

Hammer Shock deals damage and creates a zone that explodes; your attack then throws the shield.

Laning phase

Pick up your shield after your passive to get a temporary shield. Hammer Shock creates a zone that slows then explodes after a delay.

Common mistakes

  • Not picking up your shield.
  • Using Steadfast Presence with no dash to stop.
  • Casting Heroic Charge far from a wall when you could wait.

Riot's official tips for playing Poppy

  • Iron Ambassador tends to land near walls, try to take advantage of this with Heroic Charge.
  • Keeper's Verdict can be released immediately to send the enemy straight up, take advantage of this in duels.

How to play against Poppy

  • Poppy can stop nearby enemies from dashing with Steadfast Presence.
  • When Poppy starts to spin her hammer, she is charging Keeper's Verdict.
  • You can step on Poppy's shield to deny it from her.
