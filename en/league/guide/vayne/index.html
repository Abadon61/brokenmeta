Vayne, the Night Hunter, is a marksman and assassin champion that is demanding to master. Across our 570 analysed ranked games, Vayne is mostly played as ADC (54% of its games), and more rarely in Top (46%), with a 51.9% win rate in that role: tier A among ADC champions (#9 of 36, ranked by win rate). Combos and how to play

Written guide · based on the official ability texts

Vayne is a marksman-assassin: her third consecutive attack or ability on the same target deals a percentage of max health as true damage. Tumble repositions her with bonus damage, Condemn knocks back and impales against terrain, and Final Hour raises her damage and makes her invisible during Tumble.

Laning phase

Tumble between your attacks to reposition and deal damage. Attack the same target to trigger Silver Bolts on the third.

Common mistakes

  • Switching targets: you lose the Silver Bolts progress.
  • Using Condemn with no wall behind the target when there is one.
  • Tumbling toward the enemies with no plan.

Riot's official tips for playing Vayne

  • Tumble has many uses, but it cannot pass over walls.
  • Condemn can be used to both pin targets to walls to ensure a kill, or to escape a pursuer.
  • Don't go in first to a large teamfight. Wait for your teammates to initiate.
