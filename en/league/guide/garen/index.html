Combos and how to play

Written guide · based on the official ability texts

Garen is a simple, sturdy fighter: he regenerates a percentage of his health if he has not taken damage recently, and gains armor and magic resistance by killing. Decisive Strike makes him faster and silences, Courage gives a shield and tenacity, Judgment deals area damage, and Demacian Justice tries to execute a champion.

The trade
Q › AA AA › E

When: An enemy is within range.

Decisive Strike gives you speed, your next attack deals bonus damage and silences, Judgment spins around you.

Defense
W › E

When: You are taking heavy damage.

Courage gives a shield and great tenacity for a brief moment, then a smaller damage reduction for longer.

The execution
Q › E › R

When: A champion is low.

Demacian Justice tries to execute an enemy champion: cast it when their health is low.

Laning phase

Decisive Strike removes slows and gives you speed: use it to open the trade. Your regeneration only triggers without damage: step out of range to heal.

Common mistakes

  • Casting the ultimate on a full-health enemy.
  • Staying in lane without ever backing off: your regeneration needs a break.
  • Forgetting Courage before you get caught in a control.

Riot's official tips for playing Garen

  • Garen's regeneration greatly increases if he can avoid receiving damage for several seconds.
  • Judgment deals maximum damage when only hitting a single target. For effective trading, try to position such that only the enemy champion is hit.
  • Garen is only constrained by cooldowns, making items such as Black Cleaver very effective for him.

How to play against Garen

  • Stack armor items to lower the large amount of physical damage that Garen deals out.
  • Try to run away from Garen as your health gets lower, as he can execute you quickly with Demacian Justice.
  • Be careful about attacking Garen in brush. It often will lead to taking full damage from Judgment.
  • Judgment deals maximum damage when only hitting a single target. If getting out of its radius isn't possible, move through allied minions to reduce damage taken.
