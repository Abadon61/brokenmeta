Olaf, the Berserker, is a fighter and tank champion that is easy to pick up. Across our 760 analysed ranked games, Olaf is mostly played in Top (89% of its games), and more rarely in the Jungle (11%), with a 50.8% win rate in that role: tier B among Top champions (#19 of 51, ranked by win rate). Olaf's damage is mostly physical (70%): armor is the best defensive answer against this champion. 30% of that damage is true damage and therefore ignores resistances. Win rate varies with game length: 57.1% in long games (over 35 min, 70 games) versus 46.1% in medium-length games (25 to 35 min).

Combos and how to play

Written guide · based on the official ability texts

Olaf is a fighter who gets more dangerous as he loses health: his passive gives him attack speed and life steal based on his missing health. Undertow throws his axe to damage and reduce armor and movement speed, Reckless Swing deals true damage (including to himself), and Ragnarok makes him immune to crowd control as long as he keeps attacking.

The trade
Q › AA AA › E › W

When: An enemy is within axe range.

Undertow damages and reduces armor and speed, pick up the axe to reduce its cooldown, Reckless Swing deals true damage and Tough It Out gives attack speed and a shield.

The team engage
R › Q › W › AA AA

When: A fight begins.

Ragnarok makes you immune to crowd control as long as you attack; follow with Undertow and Tough It Out.

The exit from a fight
W › R

When: A control threatens you.

Ragnarok cancels crowd control if you keep attacking, and Tough It Out gives you a shield.

Laning phase

Pick up your axe after Undertow: it reduces its cooldown. Reckless Swing costs you health but refunds it if it kills its target.

Common mistakes

  • Not picking up the axe after Undertow.
  • Casting Reckless Swing at low health with no guarantee of a kill.
  • Stopping your attacks during Ragnarok: the immunity depends on your attacks.

Riot's official tips for playing Olaf

  • Olaf can combine Berserker Rage, Vicious Strikes, and Ragnarok at low life to become deceptively strong.
  • The bonus healing granted by Vicious Strikes amplifies your Life Steal from all sources as well as heals from allies.

How to play against Olaf

  • Olaf becomes more dangerous the lower life he is. Save your disables to finish him off.
  • Preventing Olaf from reaching his axe will minimize the amount of harassment he can cause in the laning phase.
  • Olaf has reduced defenses from damage during Ragnarok, despite being immune to disables. If you can't escape from Olaf during Ragnarok, try focusing your damage on him with your teammates.
