Combos and how to play

Written guide · based on the official ability texts

Rengar is a jungle assassin who leaps onto his targets from tall grass. Every ability generates Ferocity, and at max the next ability is empowered. Thrill of the Hunt camouflages him, reveals the nearest enemy champion and reduces their armor.

The ambush
AA AA › Q › AA AA › E › W

When: You are in tall grass near an enemy.

Your attack makes you leap, Savagery stabs for bonus damage, Bola Strike slows (roots with Ferocity), Battle Roar heals you.

The hunting ultimate
R › AA AA › Q › E

When: You are tracking a carry.

Thrill of the Hunt camouflages you, reveals the nearest enemy champion from very far away, gives you speed, lets you leap without tall grass and reduces the target's armor.

The exit
W › E

When: You are under control.

Battle Roar empowered by Ferocity breaks crowd control effects; Bola Strike roots the chasing target.

Laning phase

Leap from tall grass with your basic attack to open the trade. Save your Ferocity: at max, your next ability is empowered.

Common mistakes

  • Spending your Ferocity too fast: at max, your abilities are empowered.
  • Engaging without having positioned yourself in tall grass.
  • Casting the ultimate with no priority target: it reveals the nearest champion.

Riot's official tips for playing Rengar

  • Use Rengar's ultimate to find and assassinate high priority targets in team fights and skirmishes.
  • A lot of Rengar's power comes from empowered abilities he uses at the right moment - choose wisely!
  • Make sure to take paths through brush when chasing opponents to take advantage of Rengar's passive.

How to play against Rengar

  • Rengar gains an empowered ability when his resource bar is full. Try to confront him when it's low.
  • Rengar's passive enables him to leap out of brush, so avoid fighting him when near bushes.
  • Rengar puts an indicator over the nearest enemy champion when he is camouflaged during his ultimate.
