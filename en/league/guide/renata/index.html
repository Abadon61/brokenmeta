Combos and how to play

Written guide · based on the official ability texts

Renata Glasc is a control support: her attacks mark enemies, and her allies deal bonus damage to marked enemies. Handshake roots and throws, Bailout delays an ally's death, Loyalty Program protects and slows, and Hostile Takeover drives the enemies it hits into a frenzy.

Renata Glasc's strengths

  • Her attacks mark enemies and her allies deal bonus damage to them.
  • Bailout delays an ally's death and lets them survive if they take part in a takedown.
  • Hostile Takeover turns the enemies against each other.

Renata Glasc's weaknesses

  • Depends on the presence of allies to benefit from her marks.
  • Handshake has a long cooldown (16 s) and is cast blind.
  • Bailout has a 24 to 28 s cooldown: choose the ally well.

Role in team fights

Renata controls the engage: she roots an enemy or sends them toward her allies, protects her carry with Bailout and Loyalty Program, then triggers Hostile Takeover when the enemies are grouped. Her marks raise her allies' damage on the target.

The team fight

When: Enemies are grouping on your carry.

Hostile Takeover drives the enemies it hits into a frenzy, Loyalty Program protects and slows, Bailout extends the life of a key ally, Handshake roots a threat.

Laning phase

Mark the enemy with your attacks so your carry deals more damage. Handshake can be recast to throw the enemy.

Common mistakes

  • Using Bailout on an ally who cannot take part in the takedown.
  • Casting the ultimate with no grouped targets.
  • Forgetting to mark the enemies.
