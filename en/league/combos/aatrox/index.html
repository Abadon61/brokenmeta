Aatrox combos

Written guide · based on the official ability texts

The lane trade

W › Q › AA AA › E

When: An enemy is within chain range.

Infernal Chains damages the first enemy hit and drags them back if they do not leave the area, The Darkin Blade strikes next, your attack benefits from your passive, Umbral Dash repositions you.

The three strikes

Q › Q › Q

When: The enemy is cornered.

Each hit of The Darkin Blade has a different area of effect: chain all three while moving to hit the target.

The team fight

R › W › E › Q

When: A fight begins.

World Ender gives you damage, healing and speed and terrifies nearby minions; its duration is extended if you take part in an enemy champion takedown.

Common mistakes

  • Casting The Darkin Blade three times in the same place: each strike has a different area.
  • Using Infernal Chains without giving the enemy time to stay in the area.
  • Forgetting your passive: it triggers regularly and heals.

Riot's official tips for playing Aatrox

  • Use Umbral Dash while casting The Darkin Blade to increase your chances of hitting the enemy.
  • Crowd Control abilities like Infernal Chains or your allies' immobilizing effects will help you set up The Darkin Blade.
  • Cast World Ender when you are sure you can force a fight.

How to play around Aatrox's combos

  • Aatrox's attacks are very telegraphed, so use the time to dodge the hit zones.
  • Aatrox's Infernal Chains are easier to exit when running towards the sides or at Aatrox.
  • Keep your distance when Aatrox uses his Ultimate to prevent him from reviving.
