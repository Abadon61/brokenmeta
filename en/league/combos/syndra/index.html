Syndra combos

Written guide · based on the official ability texts

The stun combo

Q › E › W

When: An enemy is within range of a Dark Sphere.

Place a sphere, then Scatter the Weak pushes it and the enemies: those hit by spheres are stunned. Force of Will throws a sphere or a minion to add damage and a slow.

The full burst

Q › Q › E › W › R

When: A carry is within range of all your spheres.

Build up Dark Spheres, stun with Scatter the Weak, then Unleashed Power bombards the champion with all your spheres. The more you have, the harder it hits.

The clean-up

R

When: A weakened enemy champion tries to flee.

With your passive tiers unlocked, Unleashed Power executes targets with low health.

Common mistakes

  • Casting the ultimate with few Dark Spheres: it is much stronger with a full stock.
  • Using Scatter the Weak with no sphere to push: that is the core of the stun.
  • Neglecting Wrath Shards: they upgrade your abilities in tiers, so play them to your advantage.

Riot's official tips for playing Syndra

  • To maximize your ultimate's damage use it when there are many spheres on the battlefield.
  • After you hit an enemy champion with Force of Will follow up with Dark Sphere, they'll be slowed and have a hard time avoiding it.
  • Get one of your spells to rank 5 as quickly as possible, they gain powerful bonus effects at max rank.

How to play around Syndra's combos

  • Get boots early when playing against Syndra, they'll help you avoid many of her spells.
  • Attack Syndra after she's used Scatter the Weak, it has a long cooldown so she'll be vulnerable.
  • At max rank Syndra's spells gain bonus effects and change in appearance.
