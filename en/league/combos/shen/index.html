Shen combos

Written guide · based on the official ability texts

The engage

E › Q › AA AA › W

When: An enemy is within taunt range.

Shadow Dash dashes you and taunts the enemies you cross; Twilight Assault recalls your blade for damage based on max health; Spirit's Refuge blocks targeted attacks.

The rescue ultimate

R › W

When: An ally is in danger far from you.

Stand United gives an absorbing shield to an allied champion then teleports you to them: keep it for an ally under threat.

Harassment

Q › AA AA

When: An enemy is far from their allies.

The blade slows the enemies it hits when they move away from you; attacks are greatly empowered if it hits a champion.

Common mistakes

  • Using the ultimate with no ally to protect.
  • Forgetting your passive: every ability gives you a shield.
  • Placing Spirit's Refuge far from your blade.

Riot's official tips for playing Shen

  • Keep an eye on allies and be ready to save them with your teleport.
  • Leverage your Energy to get a long-term advantage over Mana users.

How to play around Shen's combos

  • Be ready to dodge Shen's taunt and punish him if he misses.
  • Once Shen reaches level 6, watch out for his global-range ultimate, which can turn fights quickly.

Frequently asked questions

What is Shen's main combo?

The engage: E › Q › AA › W. An enemy is within taunt range. Shadow Dash dashes you and taunts the enemies you cross; Twilight Assault recalls your blade for damage based on max health; Spirit's Refuge blocks targeted attacks.

Which ability should I max first on Shen?

The most-played max order is Q > E > W (54 games, 53.7% win rate).

What is the cooldown of Shen's ultimate?

Stand United has a 200/180/160 second cooldown depending on its rank.
