Sett combos

Written guide · based on the official ability texts

The trade

Q › AA AA › AA AA › E

When: An enemy is within range.

Knuckle Down raises your speed toward enemies and your two attacks deal damage based on max health; Facebreaker stuns if there are enemies on both sides.

Recovery

W › AA AA

When: You have taken a lot of damage.

Haymaker spends all the stored Grit: a shield and an area punch, true damage at the center.

The show-stopping ultimate

R › E › Q

When: A carry is within range.

The Show Stopper lifts an enemy champion into the air before slamming them down, damaging and slowing all nearby enemies on landing.

Common mistakes

  • Using Facebreaker with enemies on one side only: they are only slowed.
  • Casting Haymaker with no stored Grit.
  • Forgetting that you can place the ultimate to slam onto the enemy's allies.

Official ability descriptions
PassivePit Grit
Sett's basic attacks alternate between left and right punch. Right punch is slightly stronger and faster. Sett also hates losing, gaining additional health regeneration based off of his missing health.
QKnuckle Down
Sett's next two attacks deal additional damage based off of the target's max health. Sett also gains Move Speed while moving towards enemy champions.
WHaymaker
Sett passively stores damage he takes as Grit. On cast, Sett expends all stored Grit to gain a shield and punch an area, dealing true damage in the center and physical damage on the sides.
EFacebreaker
Sett pulls in all enemies on opposite sides of him, dealing damage and stunning them. If enemies were only on one side, they are slowed instead of stunned.
RThe Show Stopper
Sett carries an enemy champion through the air and slams them into the ground, dealing damage and slowing all enemies near where they land.

Frequently asked questions

What is Sett's main combo?

The trade: Q › AA › AA › E. An enemy is within range. Knuckle Down raises your speed toward enemies and your two attacks deal damage based on max health; Facebreaker stuns if there are enemies on both sides.

Which ability should I max first on Sett?

The most-played max order is Q > W > E (53 games, 49.1% win rate).

What is the cooldown of Sett's ultimate?

The Show Stopper has a 120/100/80 second cooldown depending on its rank.
