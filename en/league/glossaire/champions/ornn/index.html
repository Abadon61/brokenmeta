Lore

Ornn is the Freljordian spirit of forging and craftsmanship. He works in the solitude of a massive smithy, hammered out from the lava caverns beneath the volcano Hearth-Home. There he stokes bubbling cauldrons of molten rock to purify ores and fashion items of unsurpassed quality. When other deities—especially Volibear—walk the earth and meddle in mortal affairs, Ornn arises to put these impetuous beings back in their place, either with his trusty hammer or the fiery power of the mountains themselves.

Abilities

PassiveLiving Forge
Ornn gains an additional bonus Armor and Magic Resist from all sources.

Ornn can spend gold to forge non-consumable items anywhere.

Additionally, he can create masterwork items for himself and for his allies.
QVolcanic Rupture
Ornn slams the ground, sending out a fissure dealing damage and slowing enemies hit. After a small delay, a magma pillar forms at the end location.
Cooldown : 9/8.5/8/7.5/7s Cost : 45 Mana Range : 800
WBellows Breath
Ornn advances, breathing fire. Enemies hit by the final gout of flame become Brittle.
Cooldown : 12/11.5/11/10.5/10s Cost : 45/50/55/60/65 Mana Range : 25000
ESearing Charge
Ornn charges, dealing damage to enemies he passes through. If Ornn collides with terrain while charging, the impact creates a shockwave around him which deals damage and knocks up enemies.
Cooldown : 14/13.5/13/12.5/12s Cost : 35/40/45/50/55 Mana Range : 450
RCall of the Forge God
Ornn summons a massive elemental at a location which travels toward him with increasing speed. Enemies run over by the elemental take damage, are slowed and are made Brittle. Ornn can recast the ability to charge into the elemental, redirecting it in the direction he hits it, causing the elemental to affect any enemies it runs over to be knocked up, dealing the same damage and re-applying Brittle.
Cooldown : 140/120/100s Cost : 100 Mana Range : 2500
